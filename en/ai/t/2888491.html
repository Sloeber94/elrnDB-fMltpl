W11 EXCESSIVE EXHAUST FUMES'

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

There are a great deal of exhaust fumes, with dense smoke and a higher level of particles than specified.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • engine oil level
  • the condition of the air filter

Continue to Step 1

Oil level too high

Restore the correct level

Filter element dirty or damaged

Replace the filter element Op. 1048A14 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE.

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the engine management control unit
  • plausibility of all the parameters

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT - Description

2

BLOW-BY CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the blow-by gases intake circuit. In particular, check that there are no obstructions in the separator or in the pipes

Continue to Step 3

Blow-by circuit faulty

Restore the correct operation of the circuit: if necessary, replace the pipes (see Assembly 1080D)

Fault in the separator

Replace the separator Op. 1080D34 engine oil vapour separator - r + r

3

AIR CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and seal of the air supply circuit (upstream of the turbocharger)

Continue to Step 4

Circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

4

CHECK AIR INTAKE PIPES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check to see if there is oil in air intake pipes (downstream of the turbocharger)

Continue to Step 5

There are traces of oil in the pipes

Clean the pipes or the heat exchanger (see Assembly 1048B)

5

VALVE CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the valve tappet clearance is correct. Op. 1036H14 valve tappets - check clearance

Continue to Step 6

Clearance not correct

Restore the correct valve clearance. Op. 1036H18 VALVE TAPPETS - Adjust. following clearance check

6

CYLINDER COMPRESSION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the cylinder compression test Op. 0510A10 cylinder compression - check for loose spark plugs or injectors

Continue to Step 7

Compression values not correct

Restore the correct cylinder compression, e.g by checking the injector seal Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

7

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
