Functional description

The relay J20 controls the compressor engagment circuit.

The relay is supplied by relay R1 : line controlled by the ignition INT/A, protected by fuse F15 : both are housed in the junction unit B1 .

The supply for the relay R1 comes from the direct line of the maxifuse J/B1 of B5 , whilst the coil is supplied by the line (INT/A) protected by the fuse B26 : this line also supplies the switch unit H80 ,. via fuse B27 .

The relay J20 coil is suppiled by the line protected by fuse B39E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT - Description .

Press the appropriate button on unit H80 to sends a signal to 4-stage pressure switch K10:

This, in turn, if the pressure in the circuit is correct, does not interrupt the signal which reaches the engine control unit M10 (pin 60 of connector A).

Control unit M10 uses its own internal software to send a signal (from pin 18 of connector A) to activate the compressor which energizes the relay J20 .

The relay operates the solenoid valve, at 12 V, which switches on the air conditioning compressor L20 .

The second signal energizes the relay J34 coil which activates the first speed for the fan N85 , according to the description E6020 AIR CONDITIONING .

The fan operates at the first speed if the button for engaging the compressor is pressed, even if the control knob H81 is in position '0'.
