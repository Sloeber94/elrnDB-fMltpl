Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

The CO percentage measured is very low, with a corresponding NOx percentage which is much higher than recommended.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the air filter

Check the condition and seal of the air supply circuit

Continue to Step 1

Filter element dirty or damaged

Replace the filter element Op. 1048A14 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE.

Circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there are no fault in the engine management control unit

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT).

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other iinstruments) carry out the active diagnosis of the iinjectors.

Continue to Step 3

Connections faulty

Restore the connection of the solenoid valve with the control unit ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT).

Injectors faulty

Replace the injectors Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

3

LAMBDA SENSOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the Lambda sensor is working properly

Continue to Step 4

Lambda sensor not working properly

Replace the Lambda sensor Op. 1080B60 LAMBDA SENSOR - R.R.

4

BLOW-BY CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the blow-by gases intake circuit.

In particular, check that there are no obstructions in the separator or in the pipes

Continue to Step 5

Blow-by circuit faulty

Restore the correct operation of the circuit: if necessary, replace the pipes (see Assembly 1080D)

Fault in the separator

Replace the separator Op. 1080D34 engine oil vapour separator - r + r

5
