Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, there is noise coming from one of the front doors.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Firstly check the moving window glass is properly closed.

Check that the noise is actually coming from one of the front doors by carrying out the preliminary check for locating noises See Test 70A Location of wind noise (preliminary location)

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from another area

Check the diagnostic table corresponding to the area located.

1

CHECK THE DUST SEALING STRIP ON THE DOOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the lower seal is properly fitted

Continue to Step 2

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7005A46 front side door lower seal r r

2

FRONT DOOR WIND NOISE LOCATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the detailed check for locating wind noise from the front doors See Test 7005A Location of front door wind noiseand identify the area where the noise is coming from.

No wind noise is located

Continue to Step 3

The noise persists with the entire door taped

Check for sealant between the wing and the pillar under the wheel arch

The wind noise is coming from the area between the pillar and the windscreen

Check the condition of the door compartment seal in that area and ensure it is bonded properly to the body Op. 7005A34 front side door compartment seal r r

The wind noise comes from the handle

Check the condition of the door compartment seal in that area and ensure it is bonded properly to the body Op. 7005A34 front side door compartment seal r r

The wind noise comes from the handle

Check the condition of the handle and make sure it is correctly fitted or replace the handle Op. 7005C11 front side door outside handle r r

The wind noise is coming from the area between the door and the windscreen

Adjust the door hinges Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment.

The wind noise is coming from the area between the door and the wing

Adjust the door hinges Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment

The wind noise comes from the glass scraper area

Check the glass scraper is undamaged and correctly fitted Op. 7005D26 front side descending window glass scraper (outer) r r

The wind noise is coming from the area of the mirror

Check the condition of the fixed window and make sure it is correctly fitted Op. 7005D44 front side door fixed window(1), lh or rh (bondedtype) - r r includes cleaning housing

The wind noise is coming from the area of the mirror

Check the condition of the mirror and make sure it is correctly fitted or replace the actual mirror Op. 7005R30 electrically controlled door mirror (one) - r+r

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
