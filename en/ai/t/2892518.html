Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04

Functional description

The system for the horns is supplied through the horn relay R2 , located in the junction unit B1 from the direct line from the battery through the fuse F13 - always for B1 - and the maxifuse J/B1 for B95

The coil of the relay R2 is energized by an earth signal when one of the steering switches is closed; connected via the clock spring D47 .

The relay R2 thereby supplies the high tone horns P10 and the lone tone horns P11.
