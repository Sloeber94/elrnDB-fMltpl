Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, da 06/04; only with equipment: Automatic Air Conditioning and GPOWER and BIPOWER and BLUPOWER

Functional description

The relay J20 controls the compressor engagment circuit.

The relay is supplied by relay R1 : line controlled by the ignition INT/A, protected by fuse F15 : both are housed in the junction unit B1 .

The supply for the relay R1 comes from the direct line of the maxifuse J/B1 of B5, whilst the coil is supplied by the ignition-operated line (INT/A): this line also supplies switch unit H81 via fuse B27

The relay J20 coil is supplied by the line protected by fuse B36 and the main relay J10E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT .

Pressing the button in group H80 , sends a signal to the climate control system control unit M70 - pin B5 - ; this checks the conditions for engaging the compressor and sends another signal to the 4 stage pressure switch K10 at pin A13.

This, in turn, if the pressure in the circuit is correct, does not interrupt the signal which reaches the engine control unit M10 (pin 48 of connector A).

According to its internal control software, the control unit M10 sends (from pin 14 of connector A) a command to engage the compressor which energizes the relay J20.

The relay operates the solenoid valve, at 12 V, which switches on the air conditioning compressor L20 .

The engagement of the compressor is also controlled, in the same way as described above, by the switch I65 for the 'MAX DEF' rapid demisting function.
