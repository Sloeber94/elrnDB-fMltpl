Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the car in motion, a noise is heard coming from the rear of the passenger compartment.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First of all, check the boot closes properly

Check the wind noise comes from the rear by carrying out the test for preliminary location of wind noise See Test 70A Location of wind noise (preliminary location)

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from another area

Check the diagnostic table corresponding to the area located.

1

CHECK TAIL-LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tail-lights.

In particular, ascertain:

  • alignment between tail-lights and wing
  • tail-light installation

Continue to Step 2

Exterior tail-lights incorrectly fitted

Refit the exterior tail-lights correctly Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

2

CHECK REAR WINDOW

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the rear window weather strip (there should be no wrinkles or kinks)

Continue to Step 3

Weather strip incorrectly fitted

Adjust and if necessary change the weather strip by removing the window Op. 7035B12 Tailgate rearscreen (bonded type) WINDOW GLASS - R.R. - Includes. cleaning edges of glass and housing in tailgate

3

CHECKING REAR FIXED WINDOW GLASS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the rear fixed window glass weather strip (there should be no wrinkles or kinks)

Continue to Step 4

Weather strip incorrectly fitted

Adjust and if necessary change the weather strip by removing the window Op. 7035C10 Third SIDE WINDOW (one), left or right (bonded type) - R.R. - Includes. cleaning edges of window and housing in bodyshell

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
