Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Description

The operation of the electric windows for the rear doors takes place via the switches located on each of the rear doors.

The electric rear windows are manually operated (the glass only moves up or down when the button is pressed).

For safety reasons, a special LOCK switch, located in the dashbaord, makes it possible to exclude the power supply to the rear switches to prevent them being operated accidentally, for example by small children.

The electric rear windows only work with the ignition ON.

The control switches have ideograms that are lit up with the ignition ON.

The circuit for the electric rear windows is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a special relay. Both are located next to the junction unit.
