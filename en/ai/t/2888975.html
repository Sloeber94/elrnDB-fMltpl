Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, da 06/04

Description

The windscreen wash/wipe system is operated by a stalk switch on the right side of the steering wheel. The stalk switch can be set to five positions that correspond to different functions:

  • windscreen wiper off (standstill);
  • intermittent operation;
  • continuous slow operation;
  • continuous medium-fast operation;
  • temporary fast operation (unstable stalk position).

The windscreen washer function is operated by pulling the lever gently towards the steering wheel: the windscreen washer pump is activated in this way; If the lever is kept in this position the windscreen wiper comes on (continuous operation): when the lever is released, the windscreen wiper still works for a few moments.

The windscreen wiper and washer can be operated with the ignition key turned to ON.

The operation of the windscreen wiper is governed by an electronic device, inside the motor, which produces the intermittent function and controls the operation of the actual windscreen wiper motor.

The windscreen wash/wipe circuit is protected by a specific fuse and is operated by the ignition-operated relay located in the junction unit.
