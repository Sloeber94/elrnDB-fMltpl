0520G65 ACCELERATOR PEDAL - End of travel adjustment with S.D.C.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Move the accelerator pedal to the end of its travel.

Connect an SDC or EXAMINER station to the tester connection and check the throttle valve opening angle.

With the pedal at the end of its travel, ensure the throttle valve opening angle is 79°-83° .

Measurement

Value

-

Throttle vavle opening angle (-)

79° - 83°

If the angle is not within the specified range, adjust the screw under the accelerator pedal.

Screw in or out until the correct reading shows on the tester station.

If locking compound (Loktite or similar) is present on the adjustment screw, apply a new layer of locking compound to the screw threads following adjustment.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Connect the tester station (EXAMINER OR SDC) to the connection in the engine bay.

Move the accelerator pedal to the end of its travel (fully depressed).

Measure the accelerator pedal potentiometer output voltage.

Measurement

Value

-

Potentiometer output voltage (Volt)

3.7 - 3.9

If the reading is not within the specified range, adjsut the screw under the accelerator pedal until the tester station shows the correct reading entered previously.

If locking compound (Loktite or similar) is present on the adjustment screw, apply a new layer of locking compound to the screw threads following adjustment.

Disconnect the tester station plug from the connector in the engine bay.
