Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The reading on the instrument in the panel shows a high engine coolant temperature and the cooling fan does not come on (Otherwise D30 ENGINE OVERHEATING / WITH THE FAN WORKING)

Results of the tests

1

CHECK OPERATION OF COOLING FAN

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the test equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the tester connection and carry out active diagnosis on the engine cooling fan: (in engine management control unit mode)

Continue to Step 4

Fault in electric fan operation

Continue to Step 2

2

ELECTRIC FAN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and correct operation of the engine cooling fan (e.g. by powering the fan directly from the battery)

Continue to Step 3

Electric fan faulty

Replace electric fan Op. 1088E10 radiator cooling fan - r + r

3

FAN OPERATING CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • fan fuse
  • fan engagement relay
  • fan resistors
  • connection between engine management control unit and fan relay
  • connection between relay and fan ( See E5020 ENGINE COOLING )

Continue to Step 4

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Engine management control unit faulty

Carry out a thorough check on the control unit (using diagnostic equipment - Examiner or other instruments) and, if necessary, replace the engine control unit

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
