Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

  • Run the engine on PETROL in idling conditions (until the temperature of the coolant is above 80°C).
  • Check that no electrical loads are switched on (including the climate control and radiator fan).
  • Connect the diagnostic equipment and, in CONTROL UNIT TEST, select EOBD METHANE MULTIPLA.
  • Select the parameters ambient and read the intake pressure value.

The pressure reading on the diagnostic equipment should be:

  • less than 370 mbar for engines with mileage below 5000 Km.
  • less than 360 mbar for engines with mileage above 5000 Km.
  • If the pressure values are higher, check the camshaft timing.
  • If this is correct, measure the compression in the individual cylinders.
  • If the compression values are too low, overhaul the engine.
