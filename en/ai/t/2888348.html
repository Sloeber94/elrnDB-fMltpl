Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whe the car sets off and the clutch is released, the vehicle does not start smoothly, but jerks or vibrates

Results of the tests

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the clutch pedal travel

Continue to Step 2

Pedal travel not correct

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal Op. 1820A14 clutch pedal - adjust travel

2

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the engine supports and reaction rod

Continue to Step 3

Engine supports faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008A)

Reaction rod faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008C)

3

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • clutch disc wear or "vitrification"
  • that there is no damaged to the flywheel flexible coupling springs

Continue to Step 4

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch disc or pressure plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Presence of oil or grease

Also replace the gearbox input oil seal Op. 2110C20

4

FLYWHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the flywheel (in particular, the condition of the surface and that there are no signs of overheating, irregular wear, incisions or the removal of material)

Continue to Step 5

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
