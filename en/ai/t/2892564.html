Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The circuit for the seat lifting is controlled by the relay J61. This is supplied by the direct line for the maxifuse J/B2ofB5. The energizing of the coil, supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) protected by the fuse B27, allows the supply of the seat lifting switchH69, through the line protected by the fuseB21(which also protects the wiring for the mini bar).

The driver's seat lifting switch H69is lit up by a special LED.

The operation in one direction or the other of the switch H69determines the direction of rotation of the driver's seat lifting motorN39by providing the motor with an earth or a supply.
