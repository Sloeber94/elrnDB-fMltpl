With the vehicle driving over an umade road or one with a large number of bends, noise can be heard from the front.

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Road test the vehicle on an unmade road or one with a large number of bends to identify the type of noise:

Unspecified noise:

Continue to Step 1

The noise is a creaking

Continue to Step 1

The noise is a whistling

Continue to Step 4

The noise is a knocking

Continue to Step 6

1

FRONT SUSPENSION COMPONENTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the front suspension components for any signs of rubbing:

  • between the suspension arm bushes and washers
  • between the dust cover and the shock absorber
  • between the end of travel buffer and the housing
  • between the spring coils

Continue to Step 2

Signs of rubbing

Lubricate or replace the components involved (see Subassembly 4410A)

2

ANTI-ROLL BAR TIGHTENINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the anti-roll bar flexible mountings and that they are correctly tightened

Continue to Step 3

Anti-roll bar mountings worn or not tightened

Restore the tightenings, lubricate, or replace the mountings Op. 4410C10 front suspension stabilizer bar - r+r - check wheel geometry separately

3

ENGINE COMPARTMENT FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Continue to Step 7

Components not properly connected causing interference

Suitably tighten the pipes or shields involved

SHOCK ABSORBER CHECK
