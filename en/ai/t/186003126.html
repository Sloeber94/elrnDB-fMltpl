Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Description

The horns are operated by pressing one of the two areas at the sides of the steering wheel.

The horn circuit is protected by a specific fuse and controlled by a relay located in the junction unit.

For safety reasons, the horn can be operated at any time even with the ignition OFF.
