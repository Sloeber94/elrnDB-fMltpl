D01 ENGINE NOT RUNNING SMOOTHLY / (HESISTANT)

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

The engine does not run smoothly in all conditions (load, speed, etc.).

In particular, it "hesitates", i.e. there is a lack of or delayed response from the accelerator pedal

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • that there is no water, other fluid or dirt in the fuel tank
  • the state of the spark plugs and leads
  • engine wiring earth correctly fixed
  • battery earth correctly fixed
  • positive lead between battery and alternator correctly fixed

Continue to Step 1

Presence of dirt in the fuel tank

Clean the fuel tank Op. 1040A24

Spark plugs dirty or damaged

Clean or replace the spark plugs Op. 5510D10 spark plugs - r+r

Spark plug leads damaged

Replace the spark plug leads Op. 5510D14 spark plug cable lead bundle - r+r

Earth leads incorrectly fixed

Restore battery earth lead Op. 5530B22 battery earth lead - r+r

Battery positive earth incorrectly fixed

Restore battery positive lead Op. 5530B26 lead between battery and starter motor - r+r

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the engine management control unit
  • plausibility of all the parameters

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT).

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of:

  • vapour recovery solenoid valve
  • electro-injector
  • coils

Continue to Step 3

Faulty vapour recovery solenoid connection

Restore the connection of the solenoid valve with the control unit ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT)

Faulty vapour recovery solenoid

Replace the solenoid valve Op. 1080E28

Injector connections faulty

Restore the connection of the injectors with the control unit, ( See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT)

Ignition coils faulty

Replace the injectors Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemnt

Ignition coils faulty

Restore the connection for the coils with the control unit, See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Ignition coils faulty

Replace the ignition coils Op. 5510C14 ignition coil/s - r+r

3

FUEL PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fuel supply pressure Op. 0510C10 fuel system - check delivery pressure

Continue to Step 4

Pressure not correct

Replace the fuel pump assembly Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

4

LAMBDA SENSOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the Lambda sensor is working properly

Continue to Step 5

Lambda sensor not working properly

Replace the Lambda sensor Op. 1080B60 LAMBDA SENSOR - R.R.

5

AIR CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition and seal of the air supply circuit, and the filter in particular

Continue to Step 6

Circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

Air filter incorrectly fitted

Refit the air filter correctly Op. 1048A14 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE.

Air cleaner dirty or partially blocked

Replace the air filter Op. 1048A14 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE.

6

CYLINDER COMPRESSION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the cylinder compression test Op. 0510A10 cylinder compression - check for loose spark plugs or injectors

Continue to Step 7

Compression values not correct

Restore the correct cylinder compression, e.g by checking the seal of the injectors Op. 1056B70 injector (one) - r + r with fuel manifold pipe removed - includes seal replacemntand the cylinders if necessary
