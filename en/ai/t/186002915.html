Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two reversing lights located in the rear light clusters.

These are operated whenever reverse is engaged via a switch on the outside of the gear housing.

The circuit is supplied with the key ON and protected by a fuse located in the junction unit.

The reversing signal is sent to the navigator control unit

Vedere E4090 NAVIGATORE

and to the parking sensor control unit which only works with reverse gear engaged. Connector B for the right light cluster supplies the parking sensor system which only works with reverse gear engaged.

Vedere E3090 SENSORE DI PARCHEGGIO
