Functional description

The special switch for the steering column switch unit H5controls the operation of the rearscreen wash/wipe.

The stalk unit H5is supplied at pin 3 of connector D from the ignition-operated line (INT/A) of the relayR1protected by the fuseF14: both are contained in the junction unitB1.

The supply for the relay R1 comes from the direct line of the maxifuse J/B1ofB5, whilst the coil is supplied by the line (INT/A) protected by the fuseB26.

When operated, the rearscreen wash/wipe switch for the steering column switch unit H5, sends the control signals, from pins 1 and 2 of connector D, to the device inside the motorN16(pins 1 and 3) which regulates the operation of the rearscreen wiper.

The motor N16is then connected to earth through pin 2.

The rearscreen wiper unit consists of the actual geared motor, the end of travel contacts and the control relay.

When operated, the rearscreen washer switch sends two signals from pins 8 and 9 of connector A for H5which reach the pump for operating the rearscreen washerN22directly (the operation is the reverse of the windscreen washer function See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE ).
