Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the electrical connections and retainers for the passenger compartment integrated wiring.

2. Remove the passenger compartment integrated wiring.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the passenger compartment integrated wiring is not damaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with the anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Reposition the passenger compartment integrated wiring.

Connect the electrical connections and the retainers for the passenger compartment integrated wiring.
