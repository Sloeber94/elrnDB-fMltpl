When working on a three-way connector on the right side, carry out the following procedure:

1. Undo the nuts (1a), (1b) and (1c) for the connectors for the connecting pipes at the three-way connector.

2. Remove the three-way connector.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: fino a 05/04; only with equipment: BIPOWER and BLUPOWER

When working on a three-way connector on the left side, carry out the following procedure:

1. Undo the second section of the repressurizing pipe (1b) from the joining hose (1a).

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: fino a 05/04; only with equipment: BIPOWER and BLUPOWER

1. Undo the nuts for the connectors for the two connecting pipes at the three-way connector.

2. Remove the three-way connector complete with repressurizing pipe.

Remove the second section of the repressurizing pipe from the three-way connector referring to op. 1042A42 CNG repressurizing PIPE from hose to three-way connector - R.R.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: fino a 05/04; only with equipment: BIPOWER and BLUPOWER
