Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The supply for the front courtesy lights ( G10) and the rear courtesy lights (G25andG26) arrives directly from the battery (maxifuse J/B1 forB5) through fuseF10of the junction unitB1.

They are switched on with a direct earth signal (on permanently) to pin 1 of the respective courtesy lights, or through the go ahead - to pin 3 - due to the closing of the switches in the respective doors: I16andI17for the front doors;I18andI19for the rear doors.
