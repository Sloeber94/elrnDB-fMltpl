Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the brake pedal is pressed, the travel is longer than the normal operating travel

Results of the tests

1

CHECK FOR FLUID LEAKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check the braking system for leaks

Continue to Step 2

Fluid leak detected

Retighten the connectors (see Assembly 3330A)

Component defective

Replace faulty component

2

CHECK BRAKE PUMP FOR FLUID LEAKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Disconnect the non-return valve connector from the servo unit and insert a tube brush into the bottom of the servo unit chamber. Check that there is no fluid in the chamber

Continue to Step 3

Fluid present in the servo unit chamber

Replace the servo unit Op. 3330D10 brake servo - r+r - includes separation from master cylinder (pump)

3

CHECK FOR AIR IN THE SYSTEM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check for presence of air in the braking system

Continue to Step 4

Presence of air in the system

Bleed the system Op. 3330A18 front or rear hydraulic brake system - bleed

4

CHECK EFFICIENCY OF REAR BRAKE PLAY TAKE-UP DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the efficiency of the rear brake play take-up device.

Continue to Step 5

Faulty brake play take-up device

Replace the cylinders Op. 3320B20 Rear brake shoe CYLINDER (one) left or right - Replace.

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
