Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the handbrake is released, the rear wheels remain locked

Results of the tests

1

CHECK CONTROL CABLE TRAVEL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the return travel of the control cables is unhindered

Continue to Step 2

Control cables do not run freely

Restore correct operation

Cable damaged or broken

Replace the cable Op. 3380A30 handbrake cable - r+r

Linkage damage or broken

Replace the linkage Op. 3380A38 link/cable connecting handbrake lever to cable - r+r

2

CHECK OPERATING LEVER BUTTON

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the operating lever release button returns freely

Continue to Step 3

Incorrect adjustment

Dismantle and free the button; if necessary, replace the operating lever Op. 3380A10 handbrake lever - r+r

3

CHECK ATTACHMENT OF CABLE TO REAR BRAKE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the cable is attached correctly to the rear brake caliper

Continue to Step 4

Cable attached incorrectly

Restore the correct fixing of the cable Op. 3380A30 handbrake cable - r+r

4

CHECK EFFICIENCY OF REAR BRAKE PLAY TAKE-UP DEVICE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the efficiency of the rear brake clearance recovery device

Continue to Step 5

Faulty brake play take-up device

Replace the cylinders Op. 3320B20 Rear brake shoe CYLINDER (one) left or right - Replace.

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
