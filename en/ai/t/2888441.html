Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the central locking is operagted using the key, the doors do not open / close at all.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the battery voltage is over 9 V

Continue to Step 1

Battery voltage below 9 V: in these conditions the door locks are not supplied by the control unit

Restore the correct battery voltage

1

CHECK OPERATION OF INDIVIDUAL DOORS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that, when the door lock/unlock is operated, none of the locks is activated

Continue to Step 2

Fault in one of the front locks

Check and if necessary replace the front door lock motors Op. 7005N21 front side door lock motor-lock assembley-r r

Fault in one of the rear locks

Check and if necessary replace the rear door lock motors Op. 7010N21 rear side door lock motor-lock assembly r r

Faulty connection between services control unit and one of the locks

Restore the connection between the central locking control unit and the door lock geared motors See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING

2

CHECK CENTRAL LOCKING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • central locking fuse
  • connection between junction unit and control unit
  • central locking control supply
  • central locking control unit earth
  • connection between central locking control unit and locks

See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Control unit not working

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

3

CENTRAL LOCKING CONTROL UNIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the central locking control unit is working properly, see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5505 ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT FOR INSTRUMENTS/INDICATORS

Continue to Step 4

Control unit not working

Replace the central locking control unit Op. 7010N40

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
