0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the fault is accompanied by a noise from the gearbox with the vehicle stationary and in neutral

Check the gearbox / differential oil level Op. 2110A04

Continue to Step 1

Noise detected from the gearbox

See table S22 NOISE' GEARBOX / IN NEUTRAL

Restore the correct level and carry out a road test.

If the defect persists,

Continue to Step 1

1

LEAKS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Thoroughly wash the gearbox, cover it with talc and road test the vehicle.

Visually check that there are no leaks

In particular, check the condition of the gaskets:

  • gearbox input shaft gasket
  • rear cover gasket
  • differential oil seal

Continue to Step 2

Leaks from the seals

Replace the gaskets Op. 2110C22

Leaks from rear cover gasket

Replace the rear cover gasket Op. 2110C30

Leaks from differential oil seal

Replace the oil seal Op. 2110D63 rh differential shaft oil seal - r r

2

GEARBOX CASING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check the condition of the gearbox casing, using talc

Continue to Step 3

Casing damaged, cracked or distorted

Replace the gearbox casing, the rear cover, or the entire gearbox (see Assembly 2110B)
