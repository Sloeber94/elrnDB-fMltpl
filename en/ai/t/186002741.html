Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two dipped beams in the headlamps.

The dipped beams are activated by turning a wheel on the stalk switch to the next position after the side light setting.

A special relay, located under the dashboard next to the junction unit, keeps the dipped headlights on even when the main beam headlights are on

The circuit is protected by two fuses, one for the right hand headlamp, one for the left hand headlamp.

The dipped headlights circuit also provides the go ahead for the operation of the headlight alignment adjustment and the headlight washer circuit
