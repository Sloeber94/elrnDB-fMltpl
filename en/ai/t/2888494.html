Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The engine is running smoothly, but the oil consumption is higher than previously recorded during the life of the vehicle.

high consumption during the first few thousand kilometres is considered normal.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check for any leaks which could have caused the high consumption

Check that the type of oil is suitable for the engine in question

Continue to Step 1

Obvious oil leaks

See table X12 ENGINE OIL LEAKS

Oil unsuitable

Top up with correct type of oil

1

CYLINDER HEAD OIL PENETRATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the cylinder head and check:

  • that there is no oil in the combustion chambers and on the inlet valves
  • that the valve stems are not worn

Continue to Step 2

Excessive clearance between the head components

Head components damaged or distorted

Replace the components involved Op. 1016E10 single cylinder head, removed - overhaul

2

PISTON OIL PENETRATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the piston rings, in particular:

  • that they are not broken or damaged
  • that the actual rings are correctly fitted
  • that there is no seizing or sticking in the grooves

Continue to Step 3

Piston rings damaged or seized

Rings incorrectly fitted

Replace the piston rings Op. 1028H11 piston connecting rod set - r r

3

Check that the fault is no longer present
