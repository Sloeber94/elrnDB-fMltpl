Functional description

ABS electronic control unit M50is supplied (at pin 17 and 18) directly by the battery via a line protected by ABS maxifuseB7. The supply controlled by the ignition (15/54) reaches pin 15 from the line protected by the specific fuse B31. The control unit is earthed via pins 19 and 16.

The four sensors K70,K71,K75,K76send wheel speed signals to pins 6-7, 4-5, 8-9 and 1-2, respectively ofM50.

The brake pedal switch I30sends a go ahead signal to pin 14 of the control unitM50: in effect, the operation of the ABS is excluded unless the brake pedal is pressed; this signal arrives from the line controlled by the ignition (INT) protected by fuseF1of the junction unit (it is the same signal which switches on the lights - See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS )

If the control unit M50detects problems relating to the ABS function, it sends the instrument panelE50a signal, from pin 20, which lights up the "ABS failure" warning light.

the "ABS failure" warning light is a so-called "intelligent" light: thanks to a special operating circuit inside the instrument, it not only comes on to signal a fault in the ABS system, but also if there is no connection or a short circuit to earth in the circuit which connects the instrument with the control unit

If the problems involve the EBD function, the control unit also sends a signal from pin 21 to the "insufficient brake fluid level and handbrake" warning light which comes on together with the "ABS failure" warning light.

This is an earth signal and makes the warning light come on because the instrument panel E50supply is controlled by the ignition (INT) from the line protected by fuseF1of the junction unitB1.

The autodiagnostic data can be read by connecting to connector R10: it receives signals from pin 11 of the control unitM50via the special diagnostic line.
