Functional description

The two-way switch controlling mirrors H31operates electrical motors located in the right and left electrically-operated door mirrorsP60andP61respectively.

The switch is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse B27at pins 4 and 1 ofH31, whilst pin 2 is earthed.

Operating the switch in one direction or the other, sends one of the two motors a positive and an earth signal, thereby determining the direction of rotation. The motors which adjust the vertical position are connected to pin 5 of the mirrors P60andP61. The motors which adjust the horizonatl position are connected to pin 3 of the mirrors P60andP61.

According to the selector position, either the left motors (output signals from pins 8 and 9 of control H31) or the right motors (output signals from pins 7 and 10 ofH31) are connected; the common supply comes out of pin 3 forH31and is connected to pin 4 of the motors.

The mirrors are folded by motors connected to pins 1 and 2 of P60andP61, which receive the command from pins 5 and 11, (right) and 6 and 12 (left) respectively forH31.

Switch H31is lit when the ignition is switched on (INT/A).
