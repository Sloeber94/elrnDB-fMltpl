PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • The state of the spark plugs and leads
  • The condition of the air intake circuit
  • For a possible obstruction of the catalyzer and other exhaust pipes
  • For any obstacles to the complete accelerator pedal travel

Continue to Step 1

Spark plugs worn

Replace the spark plugs

Spark plug leads worn or damaged

Replace the spark plug leads Op.

5510D14 spark plug cable lead bundle - r+r

Air intake circuit fault

Eliminate the problem, if necessary replacing the filter element Op.

1048A14 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE.

Catalyzer/exhaust pipes obstructed

Restore the operation of the exhaust pipe

Accelerator cable faulty

Restore the operation of the accelerator cable

LPG PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic connector, check the following at the LPG engine management control unit: in parameters ambient

  • that the LPG pressure is correct by carrying out See Test1057BA LPG pressure check
