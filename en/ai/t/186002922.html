Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Operational description

The switch for the reversing lights I20

receives an ignition-controlled power supply (INT) from the line protected by fuse F1

from the junction unit B1

.

When reverse gear is engaged, the switch closes and sends a power supply to the reversing lights located in the rear light clusters F30 and F31

.
