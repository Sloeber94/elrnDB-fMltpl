Removing

Refitting

4450B11 WHEEL (one) rear - R.R.

Raise the car.

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the plastic fuel filler pipe protection.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

1. Unscrew the fuel filler fitting retaining bolts.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Using an appropriate stand, lower tank (2a) to gain access to delivery line quick-release fitting (2b) on the fuel pump.

3. Release the single delivery line from the collars on the tank and body.

4. Disconnect the single pipe from the quick-release fitting in the engine bay, then remove.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Lay the pipe in the engine bay and connect to the fittings on the body.

Connect the quick-release fitting in the engine bay.

Connect the pipe quick-release fitting to the pump.

Secure the fuel line to the tank and body using collars.

Raise the tank and tighten the retaining bolts to torque.

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Using a stand, lower tank (2a) to gain access to quick-release fitting (2a) on the outlet line and pipe (2c) on the fuel pump.

3. Release the outlet line from the collars on the tank and body.

4. Undo the connector fastening the pipe to the filter, keeping the copper washers, and remove the actual pipe.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3
