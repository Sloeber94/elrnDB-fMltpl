Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The switch I30is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT) from the line protected by the fuseF1for the junction unitB1.

When the brake pedal is pressed, the switch closes and sends the supply to the side brake lights in the rear light clusters F30andF31and to the centre rear lightF40.
