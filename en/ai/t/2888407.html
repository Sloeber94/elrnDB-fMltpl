Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During starting, the "ABS" and "handbrake" warning lights come on simultaneously and stay on for over 4 seconds. (after the initial test on the control unit, both warning lights should go out)

The "low brake fluid" and "handbrake on" warning light, it also indicates an EBD function fault if it comes on at the same time as the ABS warning light

Under these conditions, the rear wheels may lock and the car may skid when the brakes are operated because the system EBD function is temporarily deactivated.

If the warning light comes on by itself, the brake fluid level is low or the handbrake is on.
