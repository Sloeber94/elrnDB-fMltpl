Refitting

Drain the coolant fluid (refer to op. 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling ).

1048A10 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE

5040B14 air conditioner system multi-level pressure switch - r+r

1. Undo the bolts fixing the pipe to the condenser.

2. Disconnect the pipe connector from the condenser.

Seal the disconnected connectors, using suitable plugs, to prevent humidity and impurities from entering the system.

3. Undo the bolt fixing the pipe connector to the filter.

4. Disconnect the connector.

5. Remove the pipe.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged or cracked.

Reposition the pipe.

Replace the O-rings for the connectors. Only use green coloured seals, resistant to R134A coolant fluid. Lubricate the thread of the connectors using anti-freeze.

Connect the pipe connectors and tighten the bolts.
