I01 SIDE LIGHTS/NO. PLATE LIGHTS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the steering column switch unit ring nut is operated, the side lights or the no. plate lights do not work.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the side light bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 2

Bulb damaged

Change bulbs

Rear light cluster faulty

Renew or replace the rear light cluster Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

Front lights cluster faulty

Repair or replace front lights clusterOp. 5540B20

No. plate lights faulty

Renew or replace the no. plate lights Op. 5540A94 l/h or r/h number plate light (one) - r+r

2

SIDE LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

  • side light fuses
  • connections between the ignition switch and the junction unit
  • connections between steering column switch unit and junction unit
  • connections between the junction unit and the bulbs
  • bulbs earth

( See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS).

Continue to Step 3

Connections broken

Restore the connections

3

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the stalk unit is working properly: in particular, check the side light switch see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5550A stalk unit

Continue to Step 4

Stalk unit not working properly

Replace the steering column switch unit Op. 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag

4

CHECK IGNITION SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the side lights come on when the ignition key is turned in the opposite direction

Continue to Step 5

Ignition switch faulty

Renew or replace the ignition switch Op. 5520A12 ignition switch assembly - r r for versions with air bag

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
