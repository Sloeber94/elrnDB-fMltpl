It is advisable to change the engine oil more frequently than stated in the Routine Maintenance Plan if the vehicle is used predominantly in one of the following conditions:

  • towing
  • dusty roads
  • short (less than 7-8 km) and frequent journeys at sub-zero temperatures
  • engine often running at idle speed or vehicle driven for long distances at low speed (or prolonged inactivity).
