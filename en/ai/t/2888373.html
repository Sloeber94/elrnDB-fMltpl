With the ignition key in the ON position, when the lever on the steering column switch unit is raised or lowered or when the button in the centre of the dashboard is pressed, the direction indicators / hazard warning lights work but their intermittent operation is irregular

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the direction indicators / hazard warning lights: if one of the bulbs is not working, the intermittent operation of the others is affected

Continue to Step 1
