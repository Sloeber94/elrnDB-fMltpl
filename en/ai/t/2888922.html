Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Description

The front interior light includes two courtesy lights with two control switches.

With the left switch (A) it is possible to select the following two operating modes:

  • centre position: both lights on when the front doors are open;
  • left-hand position: both lights are off all the time;
  • right-hand position: both lights are on at all times.

With the right switch (B) is it only possible to switch on one of the lights (map reading light):

  • left-hand position: left-hand light always on;
  • right-hand position: right-hand light always on;
  • central position: both lights always off.

Both rear interior lights contain a courtesy light that operates in different modes according to the position of the lens, which operates an internal switch.

  • neutral position: light comes on when the rear doors are open;
  • positioned to one side: lights always off;
  • positioned to the other side: lights always on.

The courtesy light circuit is protected by a special junction unit fuse.
