Removing

Refitting

1. Unscrew the bolts securing the oil pump to the crankcase.

2. Remove the oil pump complete with engine oil intake duct.

3. Recover the oil pump gasket.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Replace the oil pump gasket on the crankcase.

Fit the oil pump to the crankcase.

Tighten the bolts securing the oil pump to the crankcase.
