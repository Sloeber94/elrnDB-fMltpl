Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two main in the headlamps.

These are activated:

  • with the dipped beams on, by pushing the stalk toward the facia (stable mode)
  • in flashing mode, by pulling the lever toward the steering wheel (unstable mode)

The circuit is protected by two fuses located in the junction unit, one for the right headlight and one for the left headlight.

A warning light on the instrument panel indicates main beam activation.
