Functional description

The cigar lighter current socket P50 is supplied to pin 2 from the line for relay R1: the line is controlled by the ignition INT/A and protected by fuse F15: both are located in the junction unit B1.

The supply for the relay R1 comes from the direct line of the maxifuse J/B1 of B5 , whilst the coil is supplied by the line (INT/A).

Pin 1 for the socket P50 provides the earth.

The ring nut for the current socket is lit up (at pin 3) with the side lights on by means of the line coming from the steering column switch unit H5 switch and is protected by the fuse F2 of the junction unit ( E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS ).

As far as the other currents sockets P51 and P52 are concerned, they are supplied at pin 2 via the line for the relay J61. This is supplied by the maxifuse J/B2 line of B5, whilst the coil is supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) protected by the fuse B27.

The supply for the two current sockets is then protected by the specific fuse B15 .
