D04 ENGINE NOT RUNNING SMOOTHLY

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

The engine does not run smoothly in all conditions (load, speed, etc.). This problem is accompanied by noise and vibrations coming from the engine.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • the seal of the fuel pipes and connectors
  • the condition of the air intake circuit

Continue to Step 1

Fuel pipes damaged

Renew or replace the pipes (see Assembly 1040B)

Air intake circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

Air intake circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the engine management control unit
  • plausibility of all the parameters

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector ( See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT - Description).

2

FUEL FILTER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the cleanliness of the fuel filter:

Continue to Step 3

Filter dirty

Replace the filter element Op. 1044B20 fuel filter filtering element - replace

Restore the condition of the filter, the pipes and the heater device

Filter faulty or damaged

Replace the filter Op. 1044B10 fuel filter assembly - r + r or replace

3

CHECK RETURN LOW PRESSURE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the pressure between the manifold and the return pipe to the tank See Test 1060A Check low pressure fuel circuit

Continue to Step 4

Pressure over 1bar: the return circuit to the tank is clogged

Eliminate any blockages in the return pipe or in the cage for the low pressure pump immersed in the tank

4

CHECK DELIVERY LOW PRESSURE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the pressure between diesel filter and high pressure pump See Test 1060B Check low pressure fuel circuit

Continue to Step 5

Leaks in circuit

Eliminate fuel leaks from low pressure circuit pipes/connectors

Pressure less than 1.8 bar or difference in pressure between Test B and Test A less than 1 bar

Replace the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

Pressure over 3.5 bar

Replace valve on diesel filter mounting Op. 1044B16

5

FUEL INJECTOR LEAK TEST

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check injectors for diesel leaks: See Test 1060C Check high pressure fuel circuit

Continue to Step 6

Leak detected from one or more injectors

Replace injector(s) Op. 1060F10 fuel injectors

6

HIGH PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check diesel pressure parameter measured using diagnostic instrument: See Test 1060D Check high pressure fuel circuit

Continue to Step 7

Pressure reading below 150 bar

Replace high pressure pump Op. 1060E10 pressure pump - r r

Leaks in circuit

Eliminate fuel leaks from high pressure circuit pipes/connectors

7

CYLINDER COMPRESSION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the cylinder compression test Op. 0510A10 cylinder compression - check for loose spark plugs or injectors

Continue to Step 8

Compression values not correct

Restore the correct cylinder compression, e.g. by checking the injector seal ( Op. 1060F10 fuel injectors)

8

VALVE CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the valve tappet clearance is correct. Op. 1036H14 valve tappets - check clearance

Continue to Step 9

Clearance not correct

Restore the correct valve clearance. Op. 1036H18 VALVE TAPPETS - Adjust. following clearance check

9
