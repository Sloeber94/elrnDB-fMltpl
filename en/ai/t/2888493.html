Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

The fuel consumption figures in the technical documentation for the vehicle have been defined during the course of homologation tests in accordance with directive 93/116/CE. These tests simulate an urban route and an "average" extra-urban route and therefore they may not be indicative because fuel consumption depends greatly on the style of driving.
