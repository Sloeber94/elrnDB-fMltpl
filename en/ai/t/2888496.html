X10 ENGINE COOLANT LEAKS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

A leak of engine coolant is seen with dripping and/or fouling of the engine

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a preliminary visual inspection to locate the component or the area which is giving rise to the leak.

If the visual inspection is insufficient, wash the engine, dry it and start it slowly until it reaches operating temperature: to identify the leak, if necessary, carry out the system leakage test Op. 0510C30 cooling system - tightness test

No leak located:

Continue to Step 1

Leak located

Proceed directly with the step involved

1

VISUAL INSPECTION OF CCOLANT EXPANSION TANK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Start up the engine and visually check that there are no air bubbles in the expansion tank

Continue to Step 2

Air in the cooling circuit

Eliminate the air from the cooling circuit

2

PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and seal of the pipes.

In particular:

  • condition and correct fitting of the bands
  • condition of hoses, connectors and plugs

Continue to Step 3

Tightenings not correct or components damaged

Restore the correct tightening or replace the components involved (see Assembly 1088D)

3

CYLINDER HEAD CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that the cylinder head tightening is correct
  • the gasket seal

Continue to Step 4

Cylinder head tightening not correct

Restore correct tightening

Gasket damaged

Replace the gasket Op. 1016C10 cylinder head - r + r and replace gasket

Cylinder head damaged

Replace the cylinder head Op. 1016C20

4

RADIATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the radiator

Continue to Step 5

Radiator damaged or distorted

Reaplce the radiator Op. 1088B24

5

EXPANSION TANK CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect:

  • the condition of the expansion tank
  • the seal of the plug

Continue to Step 6

Tank damaged or distorted

Replace the tank or the cap Op. 1088B10 engine cooling tank - r + r

6

THERMOSTAT GROUP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect:

  • the condition of the thermostat group
  • the seal of the gaskets

Continue to Step 7

Thermostat group faulty

Replace the thermostat unit or the seals Op. 1088C40 thermostat- r + r

7

WATER PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect:

  • the condition of the water pump
  • the seal of the gaskets

Continue to Step 8

Water pump faulty

Replace the water pump or the gaskets Op. 1088C12 water pump - r+r for versions with air conditioner

8

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
