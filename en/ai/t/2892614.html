Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Heater

Functional description

The relay J30controls the engagement of the fanN11.

The supply for the relay arrives directly from the battery from the line protected by the maxifuse B8.

The relay J30has a coil supplied directly by the line controlled by the ignition (15/54) protected by fuseB25. It is energized by an earth signal coming from the engine control unit M10.

If the engine coolant reaches a high temperature, the control unit M10sends (from pin 40 of connector A) an earth signal to the relayJ30.

The energized relay therefore sends a supply which operates the fan N11.
