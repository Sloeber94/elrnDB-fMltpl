Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, da 06/04

Description

The rearscreen wiper is operated by turning the ring nut on the end of the lever located on the right of the steering wheel.

By pushing the lever gently towards the steering wheel the rearscreen washer function is activated; the same pump is used as for the windscreen washer, but it rotates in the opposite direction and sends the detergent fluid to the rear.

If the lever is kept pressed, the rearscreen wiper comes on and operates for a few moments after the lever is released.

The rearscreen wash/wipe can only be operated with the ignition switched ON.

The rearscreen wash/wipe circuit is protected by a specific fuse (which also protects the windscreen wash/wipe circuit) and operated by the ignition-operated relay, located in the junction unit.

The rearscreen wiper is regulated by a device inside the actual rearscreen wiper motor.
