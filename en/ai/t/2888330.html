Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Gear engagement is defective.

In particular, the effort required to engage a gear should reduce at a certain point. In this case, the effort required remains high.

In another case, no effort is required to engage a gear (it "goes in" too easily).

Results of the tests

1

CHECK GEAR SELECTION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the gear selector ball springs (see Assembly 2110B)

Continue to Step 2

Springs or balls damaged

Replace the components involved

2

CHECK GEARBOX CONTROL CABLES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the gearbox control cables: the ball joints and the bushes should not be worn

Continue to Step 3

Ball joints worn

Replace the control cables Op. 2125C30 gear engagement cable - r r

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
