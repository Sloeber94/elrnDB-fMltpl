S23 NOISE' GEARBOX / IN A SPECIFIC GEAR

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the engine running, a noise is heard (whilsting inside the gearbox) when a specific gear is engaged

This noise decreases or disappears on changing gear.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK SYNCHRONIZER RINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Service the gearbox ( Op. 2110B20 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers and internal controls if necessary)

In particular, check the synchronizer rings for the gear involved: they should not show signs of wear on the inner surfaces

Continue to Step 2

Seals worn

Replace the synchronizers

2

CHECK GEARS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Service the gearbox ( Op. 2110B20 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers and internal controls if necessary)

In particular, check the teeth of the gear concerned: they should not be worn or show signs of seizing

Continue to Step 3

Gears worn

Replace the gears involved

3

CHECK THE FORKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Service the gearbox ( Op. 2110B20 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers and internal controls if necessary)

In particular, check the condition of the fork and the relevant gear sleeve

Continue to Step 4

Fork worn

Change the fork and the relevant sleeve

4

CHECKING THE GEAR SET

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the crown wheel and pinion reduction gear ( Op. 2110D20 differential inner case, removed - dismantle and rebuild, check planet and differential gears)

In particular

  • it should not be worn or damaged
  • there should not be excessive clearance

Continue to Step 5

Reduction gear damaged

Restore the clearance or, if necessary, replace the reduction gear

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
