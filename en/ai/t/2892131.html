Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 09/03, da 06/99 a 03/02, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04

Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet and support it in an appropriate manner.

1. Using a screwdriver, prise off (without detaching) the telescopic strut upper clips.

2. Release the telescopic strut upper attachment.

3. Undo the hinge bolts.

4. Remove the bonnet.

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 09/03, da 06/99 a 03/02, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04

Refitting

Removing

Check that the bonnet and hinges are undamaged.

Fit the bonnet.

Screw in the hinge bolts without tightening them.

Connect the upper ends of the telescopic struts and fit the retaining clips

Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet and support it.

1. Undo the bolts for the hinges.

2. Remove the bonnet.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Refitting

Removing

Check that the bonnet lid and the hinges are not damaged.

Position the bonnet.

Screw in the bolts for the hinges without tightening them.

Op. 7025A10 BONNET LID - SQUARE
