D25 ENGINE DOES NOT SWITCH TO GAS

Path

Kind

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER

The engine starts normally on petrol, but does not then switch to gas.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • that there is LPG in the tank

Continue to Step 1

LPG level insufficient

Restore the correct LPG level

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check the petrol and LPG engine management control unit: in errors ambient

  • that there are no faults in the petrol and LPG engine management control units

In parameters ambient

  • the plausibility of all the parameters

Continue to Step 3

Error detected in the petrol engine management control unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Error detected in the LPG engine management control unit

Proceed as indicated on the tester

Failed dialogue between the petrol engine management control unit and the LPG engine management control unit

Restore the connection between the petrol and LPG control units See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

No connection to earth for LPG control unit

Restore the earth connection See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

LPG pressure greater than or equal to 20 bar

Continue to Step 2

2

LPG LEVEL SENSOR FLOAT AND 80% TANK FILLING VALVE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Detach the LPG tank, drain it by sending it to an authorized centre and check the following:

  • that the 80% tank filling valve is working properly
  • that the LPG level float is working properly See Test 1043AE LPG level potentiometer operation check

Continue to Step 3

80% Filling valve faulty

80% tank filling valve Op. 1043A24 lpg tank 80 percent filling valve r r with lpg tank removed

Float defective

Replace the float Op. 1043A62 float controlling lpg level guage r r

3

GAS/PETROL SELECTOR BUTTON CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic connector, check the following at the engine management control unit: in errors ambient

  • that the GAS/Petrol switch is working properly

Check the operation of the GAS/Petrol switch See Test 1057A Petrol/LPG switch operation check

Continue to Step 4

GAS/PETROL switch defective

Replace switch

Wiring broken

Renew the wiring See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Selector supply interrupted

Restore the continuity of the wiring See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

LPG control unit defective

Replace the LPG control unit Op. 1057B80 gpl injection system control unit r r

4

LPG LEVEL SIGNAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • that the LPG level sensor is correctly supplied
  • that the LPG level sensor voltage signal to the LPG control unit is correct See Test 1043AD Check operation of LPG level sensor

Continue to Step 5

LPG level sensor supply not correct

Restore correct supply See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

LPG level sensor defective

Replace the LPG level sensor Op. 1043A60 gpl level gauge control r r

LPG sensor float faulty

Replace the float Op. 1043A62 float controlling lpg level guage r r

5

LPG PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • the correct operation of the LPG pump See Test 1043AA Check operation of LPG pump
  • the correct operation of the pressure sensor See Test 1043BB LPG pressure sensor check
  • the correct operation of the pressure regulator

Continue to Step 6

LPG pump defective

Replace the LPG pump Op. 1043A57 lpg fuel system submerged pump r r with tank and valve unit cover removed

LPG pump control unit defective

Replace the LPG pump control unit Op. 1043A54 cover of gpl tank valve unit with submerged fuel pump ecu r r with gpl tank removed

Pressure sensor defective

Replace the pressure regulator Op. 1043B42 pressure regulaton for gpl supply complete with delivery and return lines from intermediate connector r r

Faulty wiring

Renew the wiring See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

6

ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNITS CAN CONNECTING LINE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity/insulation of the CAN line connecting the petrol engine management control unit and the LPG engine management control unit See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Continue to Step 7

CAN line interrupted

Restore continuity See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

CAN line short circuited

Repair the insulation See E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
