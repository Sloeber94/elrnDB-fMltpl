K40 BATTERY CHARGING WARNING LIGHT NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, the recharging warning light does not come on with the engine switched off.

If the warning light remains on with the engine started up, this indicates that the alternator is faulty and is not charging the battery

Results of the tests

1

ALTERNATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the operation of the alternator
  • the connection between the alternator and the rev counter

( See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING).

Continue to Step 2

Connections broken

Restore the connections

Fault in the alternator

Overhaul the alternator Op. 5530A18 alternator - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check including component replacement if necessary

2

CHECK INSTRUMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the recharging warning light in the instrument see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5560 INSTRUMENT PANEL

The warning lights consist of LEDs built into the electronic circuit, and so CANNOT be replaced.

Continue to Step 3

Instrument not working

Change the instrument Op. 5560B10 analogue control panel - r+r
