Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet lid.

With the ignition key in the OFF position, disconnect the battery negative terminal.

1. Undo the throttle valve position sensor retaining bolts.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Name

Connector

2

Throttle position sensor

K56

3. Remove the sensor from the throttle body.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the sensor and connect the electrical connection.

Tighten the bolts.

Connect the battery negative terminal.

Close the bonnet lid.
