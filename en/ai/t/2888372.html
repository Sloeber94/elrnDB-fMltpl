I10 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS / NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the lever on the stalk unit is raised or lowered, with the ignition on, the direction indicators do not work

When the button in the centre of the dashboard is pressed, the hazard warning lights do not work.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 2

Bulb damaged

Change bulbs

Rear lights cluster faulty

Repair or replace the rear lights cluster Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

Front side light cluster faulty

Renew or replace the front side light cluster Op. 5550C30 l/h or r/h front turn signal (one) - r+r

Side repeater faulty

Repair or replace side repeater Op. 5550C22 L/H OR R/H SIDE FLASHER (ONE) - R+R

2

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the stalk unit is working properly, in particular the windscreen wiper intermittent operation switch and direction indicators switch. see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5550A stalk unit

Continue to Step 3

Intermittent switch faulty

Replace intermittent switch

Stalk unit not working properly

Replace the steering column switch unit Op. 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag

3

CHECK HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the hazard warning lights switch

( See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS - Description).

Continue to Step 4

Hazard warning lights switch faulty

Renew the hazard warning lights switch or replace the control panel Op. 7065C10 air conditioning control panel - r+r

4

CHECK DIRECTION INDICATORS/HAZARD LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • direction indicators/hazard lights fuses
  • connections between junction unit and stalk unit
  • connections between junction unit and hazard lights switch
  • connections between stalk unit and hazard lights switch
  • connections between hazard lights switch and bulbs
  • bulbs earth

( See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS - Description).

Continue to Step 5

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
