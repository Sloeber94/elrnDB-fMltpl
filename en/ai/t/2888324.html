B16 ENGINE STARTS WITH DIFFICULTY'

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

The engine, especially when cold, starts with difficulty only after repeated attempts. When the key is turned, the instruments light up, the starter motor turns, but the engine has trouble starting.

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • that there is no water, other fluid or dirt in the fuel tank
  • the seal of the fuel pipes and connectors
