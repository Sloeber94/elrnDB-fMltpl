Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The optional services relay J60 controls the sun roof power circuit.

The coil for the relay J60 is energized when the ignition is switched on (INT) from the line for fuse F8 of the junction unit B1 , whilst the supply to the relay contact arrives directly from the battery via the maxifuse J/B2 line of B5 and is protected by the sun roof fuse B14 .

The two switches H61 and H62 receive a supply from the relay J60 (the switches are also lit up thanks to the specific LEDs).

Front roof: the operation of the switch H61 in either direction sends the motor N36 a supply or an earth thereby determining the direction of rotation and the consequent movement of the roof.

Rear roof: the operation of the switch H62 similarly controls the control unit M83 .

The latter is also supplied by a direct line ( J/B1 ) protected by fuse F10 of B1 : according to the commands received by the switch H62 it operates the motor N37 .

The lines for pins 9, 11 and 12 connect the control unit to the Hall effect sensor inside the motor.
