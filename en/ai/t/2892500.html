Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 09/03, da 06/99 a 03/02, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04

Functional description

The switch for the reversing lights I20 is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT) from the line protected by fuse F1 for the junction unit B1 .

When reverse gear is engaged, the switch closes and sends the supply to the reversing light in the right rear light cluster F31 .
