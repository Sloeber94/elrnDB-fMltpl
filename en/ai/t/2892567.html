Functional description

The headlamp alignment device H15 is supplied at pin 2 - with the dipped headlamps on - by a line coming from the steering column switch unit H5 : in involves a supply controlled by the ignition (INT/A) for the line protected by the fuse F5 for the junction unit B1 . The supply is maintained even when the main beam headlamps are switched on, through the special relay J45 ( E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS ).

Pin 4 of device H15 is earthed, whilst pin 1 sends the signal to adjust the motors N30 (left) and N31 (right), through a resistance divider which alters the output voltage according to which of the four positions has been selected. Motors N30 and N31 contain a transducer that establishes actual motor travel on the basis of the voltage of an adjustment signal received by pin 2 from device H15 whilst pin 1 is earthed.

Motors N30 and N31 are supplied, at pin 3, by the same line for the circuit for the dipped headlamps and the line protected by the fuse F5 for the junction unit B1 ( E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS ).

The device therefore only works when the dipped headlamps are switched on.
