Functional description

The alarm system is controlled by alarm control unit M30 integral with the siren.

The control unit is supplied directly by the battery at pin 3 of connector A from the line for the maxifuse J/B1 of B5 and is protected by fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 and at pin 7 of connector B (supply for the direction indicators) from the line protected by fuse F9 ; still for the junction unit B1 .

The supply controlled by the ignition (INT) reaches pin 12 of connector B for control unit M30 . through the line protected by fuse F1 of the control unit B1 .

Pin 9 of connector A is earthed.

The control unit M30 controls the closure of the doors via switches N50 , N55 , N51 , N56 which send an earth signal, if the door is open, to pins 8, 15, 9, 16 of connector B.

The bonnet lid is controlled by switch I10 , which is connected to pin 18 of connector B, while the boot lid is controlled by switch I11 , which is connected to pin 1 of connector B (the same switch turns on the boot light: See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT )

Pin 13 of connector B supplies the emergency switch H70 which sends the ON and OFF signals to pins 7 and 4, respectively of connector A.

Pin 1 of connector A of M30 sends the control signal for the alarm LED, located - up to May '99 - in the switch control panel H90 , pin 11 of connector A or located separately in the centre of the dashboard E40 .

Volumetric sensor unit K62 receives supply and earth signals from pins 8 and 11 of connector A of M30 , and sends on the alarm control signal to pin 12 of connector A of M30 .

Pins 1 and 2 of connector B of M30 produce control signals for turn signals ( See E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS ).

The receiver for the remote control M35 is supplied directly by the battery at pin 3 from the line protected by fuse F10 and by a line protected by the ignition (INT) at pin 7 from the line protected by fuse F1 ; both fuses are located in the junction unit B1 . Pin 4 is earthed.

M35 (pins 5 and 6) gives out signals for "door locking" and "door unlocking", respectively, for the central locking control unit M80 ( See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING ).

The control unit M30 and the receiver M35 are both equipped with an autodiagnostic system which can be used by connecting to connector R10 : it receives signals from pin 6 of connector A of M30 and from pin 2 of the remote control receiver M35 . This serial line also acts as a connection between M30 and M35 .
