Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the handbrake is operated, the rear wheels are not locked.

(A correctly-operating handbrake, when fully applied, prevents the vehicle from starting off from a standstill. )

Results of the tests

1

CHECK HANDBRAKE ADJUSTMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the handbrake is adjusted correctly: Op. 3380A04 handbrake - check travel

Continue to Step 2

Incorrect adjustment

Re-adjust the handbrake correctly Op. 3380A04 handbrake - check travel

2

CHECK CONTROL CABLES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that the handbrake cables are attached correctly
  • condition of the cables

Continue to Step 3

Cable attachments loose

Re-attach correctly

Cable damaged or broken

Replace the cable Op. 3380A30 handbrake cable - r+r

Linkage damage or broken

Replace the linkage Op. 3380A38 link/cable connecting handbrake lever to cable - r+r

3

REAR DRUM CYLINDERS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the rear brake cylinders

Continue to Step 4

Shoe control cylinders worn or seized

Replace the cylinders Op. 3320B20 Rear brake shoe CYLINDER (one) left or right - Replace.

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
