Refitting

Removing

Remove the half complete engine to be replaced from the overhauling stand.

Remove the engine mounting brackets and transfer them to the new half complete engine.

Using a suitable tool, lift the engine and fix it to the rotating stand to fit the missing components.

Position the piston for cylinder no. 1 approximately at T.D.C.

Clean the cylinder head and crankcase contact surfaces.

Position the cylinder head gasket with the wording "ALTO" [TOP] facing you.

ASTADUR type cylinder head gaskets are fitted. These gaskets are made out of special material designed to undergo polymerization during engine service. This means that they harden considerably during use.

In order for the polymerization process to take place it is necessary:to keep the gasket in its sealed plastic containerto keep the gasket in its sealed plastic containernot to lubricate the gasket or contaminate with oil, and to ensure the crankcase and cylinder head surfaces are clean.

Before fitting the cylinder head, rotate the crankshaft so that the piston for cylinder no. 1 is approximately at T.D.C.

Position the cylinder head on the crankcase.
