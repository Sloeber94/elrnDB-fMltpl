Functional description

The main beam headlamps circuit is operated by the steering column switch unit H5.

By placing the switch in the main beam position or by closing the "flashing" contacts, the (left) F12and (right)F13lights are supplied: it involves a supply controlled by the ignition (INT/A) for the line protected by fusesF6andF7of the junction unitB1, for the left and right headlamps, respectively.

The line for fuse F7also supplies the circuit for keeping the dipped and main beam headlamps on ( See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS ).

The supply line for the left headlamp also sends a signal to the instrument E50- pin 2 for connector B - in order to supply the "main beam headlamps on" warning light; pin 3 for connector A ofE50acts as an earth.
