Applies to: all engines; all production periods

A leak of engine oil can be seen with dripping and/or fouling of the engine

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a preliminary visual inspection to locate the component or the area which is giving rise to the leak.

If the visual inspection is not sufficient, wash the engine, dry it and cover the parts involved in talc: road test the vehicle to identify the leak

No leak located:

Continue to Step 1

Leak located

Proceed directly with the step involved

1

DRAIN PLUG CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the seal of the oil drain plug

Continue to Step 2

Tightening of drain plug not correct

Restore the correct tightening Op. 1020A30 engine oil drain plug - replace

2

OIL SUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition of the oil sump,
  • the seal of the sealant between the sump and the crankcase
  • that the bolts fixing the sump are correctly tightened

Continue to Step 3

Oil sump damaged or distorted

Replace the oil sump Op. 1020A20 crank case oil sump - r + r clean seals for sealant application

Lack of sealant or tightening not correct

Restore the correct tightening Op. 1020A20 crank case oil sump - r + r clean seals for sealant application

3

OIL FILTER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition of the oil filter
  • the seal and condition of the gasket

Continue to Step 4

Oil filter damaged or distorted

Replace the oil filter and the gasket Op. 1084B30 engine oil filter r r

4

OIL PRESSURE RELIEF VALVE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the oil pump pressure relief valve

Continue to Step 5

Valve stuck in closed position

Replace the oil pump Op. 1084B16

5

ENGINE GASKETS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Gain access to the crankshaft and camshaft gaskets

Check the seal of these gaskets

Continue to Step 6

Crankshaft gaskets damaged

Replace the gaskets involved (see Assembly 1020D)

Camshaft gaskets damaged

Replace the gaskets involved (see Assembly 1036B)

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
