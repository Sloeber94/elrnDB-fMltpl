Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Euro 3

Description

The direction indicators and hazard warning lights delimit the outer dimensions of the car.

The left and right direction indicators are operated by raising or lowering the appropriate lever on the stalk unit.

The hazard warning lights (right and left direction indicators operated simultaneously) are turned on by the pressing the red button at the centre of the dashboard.

The turn signals are activated with the ignition key on; For safety reasons, the hazard warning lights can be turned on at any time, as they are supplied directly from the battery.

The circuits for the direction indicators and hazard warning lights are protected by two different fuses.

Two warning lights on the dashboard, also flashing, indicate when the left and right direction indicators are turned on; one only if there is a trailer.,

The red hazard warning light button contains two leds: the first green one is ignition-operated and lights up the specific symbol; The second red light comes on intermittently when the hazard warning lights are operated.

Versions with alarm: the hazard warning lights are also operated by the alarm system, which is connected directly to the lights power supply.

Versions without alarm: the hazard warning lights are operated - when the door remote control is activated - by a special relay next to the junction unit.

The hazard warning lights are also operated by the alarm system, which is connected directly to the power supply to the lights.

Connection for towing a trailer: by replacing the intermittent device with a double device, the connection which signals that the right or left direction indicator has been switched on through a special warning light in the dashboard is activated.
