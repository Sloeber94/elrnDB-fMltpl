Operational description

The circuit for the dipped headlights is activated by the steering column switch unit H5

.

When the switch is placed in the dipped headlights position it is energized through the ignition operated line (INT) protected by fuse F8

from the junction unit B1

- the relay J45

that supplies the dipped headlights for the lamps F10

(left) and F11

(right). Each circuit is protected by a fuse for the junction unit B1

: F5

for the right lamp and F4

for the left one.

The supply line for the right lamp - protected by fuse F5

also acts as a go ahead signal for the headlight alignment adjustment

Vedere E3080 REGOLATORE ASSETTO FARI

and the headlight washer

Vedere E3022 LAVAFARI
