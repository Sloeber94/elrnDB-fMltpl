STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT

There are two levers on the steering column switch unit which control the following functions:

  • side lights
  • dipped headlamps
  • main beam headlamps
  • direction indicators
  • windscreen wash/wipe
  • rearscreen wash/wipe
  • headlamp washers
