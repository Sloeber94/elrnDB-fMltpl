Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, da 06/04

Functional description

The additional heater unit is connected to the vehicle electrical system through connector D22 .

This connector, pin G, receives a direct supply through the line protected by the maxifuse B37, which also supplies the diesel pre-heating system and by a specific fuse B38; pin E is connected to earth.

Pin A of D22 is connected to the outside air temperature sensor K79 which, in turn, receives the reference supply from the alternator (D+ pin signal): in this way the signal for the outside temperature is only sent to the additional heater control unit if the engine is running.

Pin D receives the on signal from the remote control receiver M74 .

This receiver is supplied by a direct line protected by a special fuse B105 .

Pin B receives the inertia switch I50 earth signal which cuts off the circuit in the case of an impact and prevents the dangerous delivery of fuel.

The heater unit control unit is also connected to the climate control system control unit M70, via pin F to operate the fan N85 with the heater switched on.

This operation is managed by the special relay J36 .

The passenger compartment air temperature sensor, located in the controls H80 , is also operated at the same time.

The additional heater control unit is equipped with an autodiagnostic system which can be used: by connecting the unit to connector R10: it receives signals from pin C of D22 via the diagnostic line.
