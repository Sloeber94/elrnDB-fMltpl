H02 DRIFT WHILST BRAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During braking, the car pulls to the right or left.

If necessary, carry out a test on a straight, flat road with no cambers to check how much the car veers to one side.

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily, check the type, condition and inflation pressure of the tyres

Check that the wheels are correctly fixed

Continue to Step 1

Tyre inflation pressure not correct

Restore the tyre inflation pressures

Tyres not suitable or worn

Replace the tyres

Wheel fixing not correct

Tighten the wheels correctly Op. 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

Operation
