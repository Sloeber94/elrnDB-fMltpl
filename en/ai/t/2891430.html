Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet lid.

1. Disconnect the vacuum intake pipe from the EGR valve.

2. Unscrew the exhaust manifold retaining bolts.

3. Unscrew the exhaust gas intake pipe retaining bolts.

4. Remove the EGR valve.
