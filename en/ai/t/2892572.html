Functional description

The radio P20 is supplied directly (pins 4 and 7 for connector A) by the battery via the line for the maxifuse J/B1 for B5 and is protected by fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 .

It receives a supply when the ignition is switched on (INT) (pin 3 of connector A) via the line for fuse F1 of junction unit B1 .

Pin 6 of connector A receives the supply with the side lights switched on via the line protected by fuse F2 located in the junction unit B1 ( E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS ).

Pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

Pin 5 of connector A is connected with the amplifier P25 which controls the supply of the aerial. The feeder P25 is then connected to the radio by means of the special aerial cable.

Pin 1 of connector A is connected to the radio phone coupling for the preparation for the cellular phone and to the navigation system coupling ( E3580 PREPARATION FOR CELLULAR TELEPHONE , E4090 NAVIGATOR ).

Connector B sends out cables carrying signals to the speakers:

  • pins 5 and 6 to the left front speakers P35 and P45 ;
  • pins 3 and 4 to the right front speakers P36 and P46 ;
  • pins 7 and 8 to the left rear speakers P40 ;
  • pins 1 and 2 for the right rear speakers P41 .

The cable connected to the CD player P75 comes out of connector C.
