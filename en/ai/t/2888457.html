Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whilst the steering is being operated, there is a noise when the end of travel position is reached

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid level in the reservoir

Carry out a test by steering on full lock and then releasing the steering wheel

Continue to Step 1

Level too low

Restore the correct level. if there are leaks,

See table X30 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS

The noise increases towards the end of travel position and with the engine speed (roaring)

Continue to Step 1

The noise remains constant (hissing during steering, puffing on release)

No intervention of any sort is needed: the noise is due to the by-pass valve discharing excess pressure

1

POWER ASSISTED STEERING FLUID QUALITY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir. Make sure that there is no emulsified oil (oil which is lighter in colour and with bubbles)

Continue to Step 2

Emulsified fluid

Eliminate air leaks from the power steering system pipes Op. 4130B10 power steering fluid resevoir r r

2

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PIPES CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether there are any restrictions in the system pipes, flexible and rigid

Continue to Step 3

Pipes faulty

Replace the damaged parts, replacing all the seals (see Assembly 4130B)

3

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the power assisted steering pump is working properly

Continue to Step 4

Power assisted steering pump malfunction

Replace the power assisted steering pump Op. 4130B24 power steering pump r r for versions with air conditioner

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
