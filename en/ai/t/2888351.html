Applies to: all engines; all production periods

As the pressure exerted on the brake pedal increases, the braking action does not correspond.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Check that there are no faults in the ABS control unit.

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore:

  • the connection between the ABS control unit and the diagnostic connector
  • the ABS control unit supply

See E7020 ABS

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of:

  • pump motor
  • solenoids

Continue to Step 3

Fault detected in one solenoid

Replace electrohydraulic unit-electronic control unit Op. 3340A12 A'B.S. system. - electronic and hydraulic CONTROL UNIT R.R.

3

CHECK FRICTION SURFACES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • condition of front pad friction surfaces
  • condition of rear pad friction surfaces

Continue to Step 4

Friction surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water

Clean, check and eliminate the causes of the fault

Front brake pads damaged or wrong type

Replace pads Op. 3310A60 front brake pads - replace

Rear shoes damaged or wrong type

Replace the shoes Op. 3320B10

4

CHECK FRONT DISCS AND CALIPERS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check condition of front brake discs

Check the condition of the brake caliper pistons

Continue to Step 5

Front discs worn or damaged

Grind the discs Op. 3310A54 front brake discs removed - grind, or replace if necessary

Front caliper pistons worn or seized

Replace brake calipers Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (one) - R.R.

5

CHECK REAR DRUMS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condtion of the rear brake drums.

Check the condition of the rear brake cylinders.

Continue to Step 6

Rear drums worn or damaged

Replace the drums Op. 3320B30 lh or rh rear brake drum (one) r r

Shoe control cylinders worn or seized

Replace the cylinders Op. 3320B20 Rear brake shoe CYLINDER (one) left or right - Replace.

6

CHECK VACUUM PIPE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check condition and fitting of vacuum pipe

Continue to Step 7

Pipe fitted incorrectly or bent

Refit correctly

Pipe damaged

Replace the vacuum pipe Op. 3330D24 vacuum pipe for brake servo - r+r

7

CHECK NON-RETURN VALVE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the non-return valve is working properly

Continue to Step 8

Non-return valve not working properly

Replace non-return valve Op. 3330D20 one-way valve for brake servo - r+r

8

CHECK BRAKE SERVO

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that brake servo is working properly: See Test 3330DA Check operation of brake servo

Continue to Step 9

Brake servo not working properly

Replace the servo unit Op. 3330D10 brake servo - r+r - includes separation from master cylinder (pump)

9

CHECK ENGINE VACUUM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

1.9 JTD 8V

ABS

Check vacuum in vacuum circuit:

Continue to Step 10

Vacuum value incorrect

Replace brake servo vacuum unit Op. 3330D30 vacuum pump - r+r

1.6 16v

ABS

Continue to Step 10

10

CHECK BRAKE PIPES/HOSES AND BLEEDING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check that there are no kinks or restrictions in the brake pipes, bleed the system Op. 3330A18 front or rear hydraulic brake system - bleedand check that fluid emerges from the bleed screws as normal

Continue to Step 11

There are kinks or restrictions, fluid does not come out normally

Replace the section of pipe affected by the fault (see Assembly 3340B)
