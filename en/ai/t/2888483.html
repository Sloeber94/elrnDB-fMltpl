Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When it rains or after washing, water enters the rear of the passenger compartment.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK THAT THERE IS NO PENETRATION THROUGH THE REARSCREEN

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the sealant on the rearscreen See Test 7035A windscreen

Continue to Step 2

No sealant

Restore the sealant, following the instructions in the Op. 7035B04 rear window eliminate leaks

2

CHECK REAR WINDOW

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear windscreen. In particular, visually inspect:

  • the condition of the window
  • the flatness of the support surface

Continue to Step 3

Rearscreen faulty

Replace the rearscreen Op. 7035B12 Tailgate rearscreen (bonded type) WINDOW GLASS - R.R. - Includes. cleaning edges of glass and housing in tailgate

3

CHECK THAT THERE IS NO PENETRATION THROUGH THE FIXED REAR WINDOW

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the sealant on the fixed rear window See Test 7035A windscreen

Continue to Step 4

No sealant

Restore the sealant, following the instructions in the Op. 7035C04 side quater light one lh or rh eliminate leaks

4

CHECKING REAR FIXED WINDOW GLASS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fixed rear window. In particular, visually inspect:

  • the condition of the window
  • the flatness of the support surface

Continue to Step 5

Rearscreen faulty

Replace the window Op. 7035C10 Third SIDE WINDOW (one), left or right (bonded type) - R.R. - Includes. cleaning edges of window and housing in bodyshell

5

SEALANT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the sealant on the rear wing is correct

Continue to Step 6

No sealant

Restore the correct sealant

6

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
