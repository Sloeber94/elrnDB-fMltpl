Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The steering oscillates rapidly along a straight road at sustained speed. The effect increases as speed rises.

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily, check the type, condition and inflation pressure of the tyres

Check that the wheels are correctly fixed

Continue to Step 1

Tyre inflation pressure not correct

Restore the tyre inflation pressures

Tyres not suitable or worn

Replace the tyres

Wheel fixing not correct

Tighten the wheels correctly Op. 4450B14 wheels (four) - r+r

WHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that the wheels are properly balanced
  • the condition of the wheel rims (there should not be dents or distortions)

Continue to Step 2

Wheels not balanced

Balance the wheelsOp. 4450B41

Wheel rims dented or distorted

Replace the rims (see Assembly 4450B)

WHEEL ALIGNMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check rear wheel alignment

Continue to Step 3

Alignment not correct

Restore correct alignment Op. 4450A90 rear wheel alignment - check using optical equipment

3

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the centering of the engine supports and the reaction rod

Continue to Step 4

Engine supports faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008A)

Reaction rod faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008C)

4

BUSHES AND FLEXIBLE MOUNTINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there are no accidental blows or damage to:

  • steering box rods
  • wishbone flexible mounts
  • anti-roll bar flexible mountings

Continue to Step 5

Steering box rods worn or damaged

Replace the links Op. 4110B10 steering links - r+r on vehicle - check tow-in/tow-out separately

Arm supports worn or damaged

Replace the mounts Op. 4410B40 lower wishbone (with balljoint) of left or right front suspension (one) - r+r -check wheel geometry separately

Anti-roll bar mountings worn or damaged

Replace the mountings Op. 4410C10 front suspension stabilizer bar - r+r - check wheel geometry separately

5

CHECK FRONT SPRING/SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • condition and efficiency of the front spring/damper unit
  • the fixings for the spring/shock absorber assembly

Continue to Step 6

Tightening not correct

Restore the correct fixing

Shock absorbers not working properly

Replace the shock absorber involved Op. 4410A12 front suspension damper (one), lh or rh - for mcpherson strut system - includes dismantling/re-building of assembly at bench r r

Springs not working properly

Replace the spring involved Op. 4410A12 front suspension damper (one), lh or rh - for mcpherson strut system - includes dismantling/re-building of assembly at bench r r
