Removing

Refitting

1. Raise the tab (1a) and disengage the knob (1b) from the linkage (1c).

2. If necessary, undo the bolt (2a) and remove the seal (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the knob and seal are undamaged.

If removed, fit the seal and secure it with the bolt.

Raise the tab and fit the knob fully home on the linkage.

Check that the safety device is working properly.
