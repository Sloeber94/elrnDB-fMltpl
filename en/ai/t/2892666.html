Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 09/03, da 06/04

Functional description

The ABS electronic control unit M50 is supplied (at pins 2 and 6) directly by the battery via a line protected by ABS maxifuse B7. The supply controlled by the ignition (15/54) reaches pin 23 from the line protected by the specific fuse B31. The control unit is earthed via pins 1 and 5.

The four sensors K70, K71, K75, K76 send wheel speed signals to pins 28-12, 15-16, 13-14 and 30-31 of M50 respectively.

The brake pedal switch I30 with the N.A. contact sends an enablement signal to pin 32 of control unit M50; this signal arrives from the line controlled by the ignition (INT) protected by fuse F1 of the junction unit (it involves the same signal that lights up the brake lights See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS .

The switch for the brake pedal I30 with an N.C. contact, on the other hand, sends a go ahead signal to pin 20 of control unit M50; this signal arrives via the line controlled by the ignition (INT) protected by shunt fuse B10.

Steering sensor K58 is supplied by ABS control unit M50 at pin 39 and receives a reference earth from pin 21: the signal that detects sudden changes in steering direction is sent via the CAN line to engine management control unit M1 and to the ABS control unit M50, pins 24 and 40.

Yaw sensor K74 is supplied from ABS control unit M50 at pin 39, while it receives a reference earth from pin 21: control and command signals are exchanged with ABS control unit M50 , at pins 8, 9, 10 and 41.

Pressure sensor K72 is supplied from ABS control unit M50 at pin 42, while it receives a reference earth from pin 25: the pressure detection signal is sent to pin 26 of ABS control unit M50 .

The ABS control unit M50 is connected, via the special CAN line, from pin 22, to the instrument panel E50 to manage the ABS failure warning light, the EBD warning light and the ASR/ESP warning light.

The ASR OFF button, located in the switch unit H90, connector C, sends a deactivation signal to pin 27 of M50, whilst the signal that switches on the LED when the system is switched off is sent from pin 18; ; switch H90C is supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (INT) protected by shunt fuse B10.

The autodiagnostic data can be read by connecting to connector R10: it receives signals from pin 11 of the control unit M50 via the special diagnostic line.
