Refitting

Removing

Fit new pipes from the manifold to the injectors.

Whenever you work on the pipes, always fit a new pipe when reassembling to avoid leakage from the fittings.

Tighten the fitting on the line to the pressure pump and fuel manifold to torque using the same wrenches and a torque wrench.
