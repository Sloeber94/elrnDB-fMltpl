Operational description

The alarm system is managed by alarm control unit M030

, integrated with the siren.

The control unit receives a direct supply from the battery at pin 3 of connector A from the line for maxifuse J/B1 of B5

and is protected by fuse F10

of junction unit B1

and at pin 7 of connector B (power supply for direction indicators) by the line protected by fuse F9

of junction unit B1

.

The ignition operated supply (INT) reaches pin 12 of connector B of control unit M30

. via the line protected by fuse F1

of junction unit B1

.

Pin 9 of connector A is earthed.

The control unit M30

controls the closing of the doors by means of the switches N50

, N55

, N51

, N56

which, if the door is open, send an earth signal to pins 8, 15, 9, 16 , respectively of connector B.

From pin 14 of connector B of M30

the doors open signal departs for the instrument panel

Vedere E4010 STRUMENTO DI BORDO

.

The bonnet is controlled by switch I10

which is connected to pin 18 of connector, whilst the boot is controlled by switch I11

which is connected to pin 17 of connector B.

Pin 1 of connector A of M30

sends the control signal for the alarm LED E40

located in the centre of the dashboard.

The volumetric sensors K62

receive a power supply and earth, respectively, from pins 8 and 11 of connector A of M30

and send the alarm control signal to pin 12 of connector A of M30

.

From pins 1 and 2 of connector B of M30

the control signals for the direction indicators depart:

Vedere E2020 LUCI DIREZIONE / EMERGENZA

.

The remote control receiver M35

receives a direct supply from the battery at pin 3 from the line protected by fuse F10

of junction unit B1

and an ignition-controlled power supply (INT) at pin 7 from the line protected by shunt fuse B26

. Pin 4 is earthed.

From M35

(pins 5 and 6) the "door locking" and "door unlocking" signals, respectively, leave for the integrated services control unit M40

:

Vedere E3040 BLOCCAPORTE

.

The control unit M30

and the receiver M35

are both equipped with an autodiagnostic system which can be used by connecting to connector R10

: and signals arrive from pin 6 of connector A of M30 and from pin 2 of the remote control receiver M35

. This serial line also acts as a connection between M30

and M35

.
