The fan which sends air into the passenger compartment is not working properly, for example the air flow is not constant

Results of the tests

1

POLLEN FILTER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the pollen filter

It should be rememebered that the pollen filter should be replaced regularly, at least once a year.

Continue to Step 2

Filter obstructed

Replace the pollen filter Op. 5010B09 PURIFYING FILTER (ANTIPOLLEN) FILTER ELEMENT - REPLACE

2

CHECKING THE AIR DUCTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the air ducts inside the dashboard and the vents are intact and there are no obstructions

Continue to Step 3

Ducts obstructed or damaged

Restore the operation of the ducts and the vents (see Assembly 5010B)

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
