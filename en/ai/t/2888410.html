Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The passenger air bag disabled warning light comes on upon start-up and stays lit for longer than 4 seconds. (carry out the initial control unit test)

If the manual switch is turned to OFF, the warning light should stay on

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

check

  • there are no faults in the air bag control unit
  • the plausibility of all the parameters.

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between Air Bag control unit and diagnostic connector See E7030 AIRBAG

2

CHECK PASSENGER'S AIR BAG DISABLEMENT SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the passenger air bag manual cut-out switch is working properly:

  • when the device is switched to ON, the control unit should show a resistance of 100 Ohm;
  • when the device is switched to OFF, the control unit should show a resistance of 400 Ohm.

Continue to Step 3

Switch faulty

Replace passenger Air Bag manual disablement switch Op. 5580C26 LOCK BARREL KEY/SWITCH for deactivating passenger 'AIR BAG - R.R.

3

CHECK INSTRUMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the passenger air bag disablement warning light inside the instrument see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5560 INSTRUMENT PANEL

The warning lights consist of LEDs built into the electronic circuit, and so CANNOT be replaced.

Continue to Step 4

Instrument not working

Replace the instrument Op. 5560B10 analogue control panel - r+r

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
