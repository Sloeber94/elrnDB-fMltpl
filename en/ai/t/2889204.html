Applies to: all engines; production: da 04/02 a 08/03; only with equipment: Navigator

Description

The vehicle is equipped with a radio system that includes:

  • radio supply cables
  • front speakers and separate tweeters, located in the front pillars
  • rear speakers
  • connecting cables between radio and loudspeakers
  • roof mounted aerial, controlled by a specific electronic device
  • aerial coaxial cable.

The following is also fitted, according to the trim level:

  • RADIO with CC or CD.
  • Standard CONNECT device (seeE4091 CONNECT)
  • Radio with NAVIGATOR (seeE4090 NAVIGATOR)
  • CONNECT device with NAVIGATOR (seeE4090 NAVIGATORandE4091 CONNECT)

Full radio specifications and operating instructions are given in the OWNER'S HANDBOOK.

The main radio controls are 'duplicated' on the steering wheel, allowing the driver to operate the radio without taking their hands off the steering wheel.

On versions with a Navigator an impedance adaptor is required for connecting the radio with the controls.

A multi-pole cable connecting the radio to the CD player, located in the boot, may be fitted on request.

The radio circuit is supplied both with the ignition key in the ON position and directly from the battery.

The above two lines are protected by two fuses in the junction unit.

In addition to the radio there is also a supply with the side lights on to light up the radio controls.
