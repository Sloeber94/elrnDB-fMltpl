Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the sides of the steering wheel cover are pressed, the horns do not worn.

Results of the tests

1

HORNS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the horns

Continue to Step 2

Horns faulty

Replace the horns Op. 5550B30 L/H OR R/H HORN (ONE) - R+R

2

HORNS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • the horns fuse
  • the horns relay
  • the connection between the junction unit and the steering column switch unit
  • the steering column switch unit earth
  • the horns supply and earth

See E3010 HORNS

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

3

CLOCK SPRING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • the operation of the clock spring: see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5580C air bag system
  • the connections for the horn switches with the clock spring
  • the connection of the steering column switch unit with the clock spring

Continue to Step 4

Clock spring faulty

Replace the clock spring Op. 5580C12 clock spring for air bag module on steering wheel r r

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
