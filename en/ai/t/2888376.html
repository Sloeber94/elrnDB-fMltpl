Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fog lamps do not come on when the switch located on the control panel in the centre of the facia is operated with the side lights on.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of individual bulbs and lamps

Continue to Step 2

Bulb damaged

Change bulbs

Lamp defective

Repair or replace the fog lamp Op. 5540D10 l/h or r/h fog lamp (one) - r+r

2

CHECK FOG LAMP ELECTRIC CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • fog lamp fuse
  • control panel supply fuse
  • fog lights relay.
  • control panel power supply
  • connection between relay and control panel
  • connection between relay and bulb
  • relay earth and bulbs
  • control panel earth

( See E2030 FOG LIGHTS).

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

3

CHECK FOG LAMP SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fog lamp switch located in the control panel in the centre of the facia is working properly. See E2030 FOG LIGHTS

The switch is built into an electronic control device, which controls the fog lamp operating software.

Continue to Step 4

Fog lamp switch defective

Repair the fog lamp switch or change the control panel Op. 7065C10 air conditioning control panel - r+r

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
