Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER

Check that with the switch pressed, there is continuity between contacts 1 and 3, and that a 12 V voltage signal is sent to pin 47 of the LPG control unit to exclude the changeover to LPG.

Check that with the switch released, the contacts 1 and 3 are open and the LPG supply is enabled.
