Operational description

The relay for the electric rear windows J66

controls the operation of the circuit.

The electric windows relay receives a supply from the direct line from the battery protected by fuse B17

.

Switch H55

locking the electric rear windows is supplied by the ignition operated line (INT) protected by shunt fuse B26

: with the button pressed (enablement for the rear electric windows) the coil for relay J66 is energized

; in this way the switches on the rear doors H53

and H54 are supplied

and from there the motors N65

(left) and N66

(right): reversing the signals depending on the contact which has been closed determines the direction of rotation of the actual motor.
