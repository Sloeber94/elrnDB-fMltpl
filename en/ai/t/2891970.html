Refitting

Closely observe the safety regulations established for this operation.

4110A11 steering wheel - r+r for replacement or check for versions with air bag

7040A26 steering column trim - r+r

1. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Name

Connector

1

Clock spring on steering wheel

Q10

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Remove the clock spring.

The clock spring is fitted with a device which automatically stops its rotation when it is removed from the steering wheel. Avoid forcing its rotation.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. If the clock spring is reused it is advisable to secure the two plates with a band to prevent accidental rotations.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the wheels are straight.

The parts supplied as spares are for fitting in a centred position.

If, for any reason, the upper plate for the clock spring should rotate in relation to the lower one in such a way that the relative position is no longer known, then the clock spring must be replaced.

Place the clock spring in its housing (if it is being resued, remove the band fitted previously).

Tighten the bolts.

Reconnect the electrical connection.

1. If the clock spring is being replaced with a new one, remove the safety tab.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
