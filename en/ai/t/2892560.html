Functional description

The cigar lighter current socket P50is supplied to pin 2 from the line for relay R1: the line is controlled by the ignition INT/A and protected by fuseF15: both are located in the junction unitB1.

The supply for the relay R1 comes from the direct line of the maxifuse J/B1ofB5, whilst the coil is supplied by the line (INT/A) protected by the fuseB26.

Pin 1 for the socket P50provides the earth.

The ring nut for the current socket is lit up (at pin 3) with the side lights on by means of the line coming from the steering column switch unit H5switch and is protected by the fuseF2of the junction unit ( See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS ).

As far as the other currents sockets P51andP52are concerned, they are supplied at pin 2 via the line for the relayJ61. This is supplied by the maxifuse J/B2line ofB5, whilst the coil is supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) protected by the fuseB27.

The supply for the two current sockets is then protected by the specific fuse B15.
