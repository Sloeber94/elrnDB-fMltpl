Refitting

1. Raise the tab (1a) and release the knob (1b) from the linkage (1c).

2. If necessary, unscrew the bolt and remove the gasket (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the knob and gasket are undamaged.

If removed, fit the gasket and secure it with the bolt.

Raise the tab and fit the knob fully home on the linkage.

Check that the safety device is working properly.
