Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, the fuel gauge indicator is not working properly (it indicates a different level from what is actually in the tank)

Results of the tests

1

FUEL GAUGE ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the connection between the fuel gauge and the instrument, through the junction unit
  • the instrument supply
  • the instrument earth

( See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL).

Continue to Step 2

Connections broken

Restore the connections

2

FUEL GAUGE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the fuel gauge.

In particular, check that the drip tray is correctly fitted as it could impede the float.

Continue to Step 3

Sender unit defective

Replace the sender unit inside the fuel pump Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

3

CHECK INSTRUMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the instrument is working properly, particularly the fuel level signal check devicesee DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5560 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 4

Instrument not working

Change the instrument Op. 5560B10 analogue control panel - r+r

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
