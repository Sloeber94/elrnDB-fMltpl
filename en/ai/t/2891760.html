1048A10 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE

Position a suitable container for recovering the coolant fluid.

1. Loosen the pipe retaining bands.

2. Disconnect the pipe and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the pipe is not damaged and that there are no cracks which could affect the seal.

Reposition the pipe and connect it to the connectors.

The heater water supply and return pipes have white or green coloured ends to prevent them from being incorrectly fitted after operations where they are both removed.

Tighten the pipe retaining bands.
