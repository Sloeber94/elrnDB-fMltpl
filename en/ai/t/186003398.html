Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

  • Carry out the electrical test by connecting a braid between the solenoid and its connector to supply it at 14V. Measure the current uptake usng an ammeter.
  • The current measured should be between 1.6 and 2 Ampere.
