Functional description

The headlamp washer J50 is supplied (pin P) by the direct line from the battery through the headlamp washer fuse B12 and the maxifuse J/B2 of B5 .

If the windscreen washer switch is operated, the steering column switch unit H5 sends a signal which reaches the headlamp washer timer J50 (pin S): this is a line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) coming from the steering column switch unit H5 - pin 3 of connector D - supplied by the relay R1 , protected by fuse F14 : both are housed in the junction unit B1 .

The supply for the relay R1 comes from the direct line of the maxifuse J/B1 of B5 , whilst the coil is supplied by the line (INT/A) protected by the fuse B26 ( E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE ).

With the dipped headlamps switched on, a go ahead signal reaches pin 56 of J50 from the line for fuse F5 which supplies the dipped headlamps. ( E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS ).

When there are both signals at pins S and 56, the relay, inside the device J50 which supplies the electric headlamp washer pump N25 with impulses of about half a second, is energized for a certain time.
