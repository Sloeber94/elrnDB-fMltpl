Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle driving, there is a constant noise from the front, especially when steering

When driving in a straight line this noise disappears.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid level in the reservoir

Carry out a test by steering on full lock and then releasing the steering wheel

Continue to Step 1

Level too low

Restore the correct level. if there are leaks,

See table X30 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS

The noise increases towards the end of travel position and with the engine speed (roaring)

Continue to Step 2

The noise remains constant

No intervention of any sort is needed: the noise is due to the by-pass valve discharing excess pressure

The noise is a hissing/whistling

Continue to Step 4

The noise is a hissing/whistling

Continue to Step 4

1

POWER ASSISTED STEERING FLUID RESERVOIR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • reservoir breather plug
  • actual filter

Continue to Step 2

Breather plug blocked

Clean or replace the plug Op. 4130B14 Power assisted steering fluid reservoir CAP - R.R. - Check level and top up if necessary.

Filter dirty or blocked

Replace the reservoir Op. 4130B10 power steering fluid resevoir r r

2

POWER ASSISTED STEERING FLUID QUALITY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir.

  • Make sure that there is no emulsified oil (oil which is lighter in colour and with bubbles)
  • make sure that the oil is not "old" (darker colour)

Continue to Step 3

"Old" fluid

Eliminate air leaks from the power steering system pipes Op. 4130B10 power steering fluid resevoir r r

"Old" fluid

Replace the power assisted steering fluid Op. 4130B10 power steering fluid resevoir r r

3

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PIPES CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether there are any restrictions in the system pipes, flexible and rigid

Continue to Step 4

Pipes faulty

Replace the damaged parts, replacing all the seals (see Assembly 4310B)

4

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a test rotating the steering wheel slightly firstly with the engine running and then with it switched off: check that the knocking takes place both with the engine running and with it switched off

Continue to Step 5

With the engine running there is a knocking noise, with the engine switched off it disappears

Replace the power assisted steering pump Op. 4130B20 power steering pump r r

5

CHECK THE STEERING LINK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the steering link.

Continue to Step 6

Tie-rods damaged or worn

Replace the links Op. 4110B10 steering links - r+r on vehicle - check tow-in/tow-out separately

6

STEERING COLUMN BEARINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there is not too much play in the steering column; check the condition and the efficiency of the steering column support bearings

Continue to Step 7

Bearings worn or damaged

Replace the bearings involved Op. 4110A31 Steering control shaft SUPPORT - R.R. for versions with Air Bag

7

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
