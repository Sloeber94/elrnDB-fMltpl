Functional description

The headlamp alignment device H15is supplied at pin 2 - with the dipped headlamps on - by a line coming from the steering column switch unitH5: in involves a supply controlled by the ignition (INT/A) for the line protected by the fuseF5for the junction unitB1. The supply is maintained even when the main beam headlamps are switched on, through the special relay J45( See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS ).

Pin 4 of device H15is earthed, whilst pin 1 sends the signal to adjust the motorsN30(left) andN31(right), through a resistance divider which alters the output voltage according to which of the four positions has been selected. Motors N30andN31contain a transducer that establishes actual motor travel on the basis of the voltage of an adjustment signal received by pin 2 from deviceH15whilst pin 1 is earthed.

Motors N30andN31are supplied, at pin 3, by the same line for the circuit for the dipped headlamps and the line protected by the fuseF3for the junction unitB1( See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS ). The device therefore only works when the dipped headlamps are switched on.
