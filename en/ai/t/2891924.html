Refitting

5550C30 l/h or r/h front turn signal (one) - r+r .

1. Release the filter casing (1a) from the retainers (1b) and (1c), then move it slightly (for the left lamp only).

2. Undo the bolts fixing the lamp.

3. Move the lamp and disconnect the electrical connections (3a, 3b).
