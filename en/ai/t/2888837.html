Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Euro 3

Description

The vehicle has two special lamps for the dipped beam headlamps.

The dipped beam headlamps are switched on through the steering column switch unit by turning the special ring nut in the position after the side lights.

A special relay - under the dashboard next to the junction unit - keeps the dipped beam headlamps on with the main beam headlamps on.

The circuit is protected by two fuses, one for the right hand headlamp, one for the left hand headlamp.

The dipped beam headlamps circuit also gives the go ahead for the operation of the headlamp alignment and the headlamp washer circuit.
