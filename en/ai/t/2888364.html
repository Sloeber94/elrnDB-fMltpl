During normal steering, the steering stiffens either to the right or to the left.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a test, steering on full lock in various conditions to check whether the stiffness arises in one direction only

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid level in the reservoir

Continue to Step 1

Stiffness in both directions

See table H10 STEERING HARD / (STEERING IN BOTH DIRECTIONS)

Power assisted steering fluid level too low

Restore the correct level, if there are leaks,

See table X30 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS

1

CHECK THE STEERING LINK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering rods are not bent or damaged.

Continue to Step 2

Tie-rods damaged or worn

Replace the links Op. 4110B10 steering links - r+r on vehicle - check tow-in/tow-out separately

2

STEERING BOX CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering box is working properly

Continue to Step 3

Steering box not properly calibrated

Replace the steering box Op. 4130A10 power steering box - r+r - check tow-in if necessary separately - includes bleeding

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION
