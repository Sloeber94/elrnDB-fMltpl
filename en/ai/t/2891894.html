It is not possible to order a single lock barrel or to order barrels simply by using a part code. When ordering barrels, observe the special procedure set out in 5580E - Special accessories.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the lower half casing.

3. Insert the ignition key in the lock barrel and turn to the ON position.

4. Using a screwdriver, insert the retaining tooth.

5. Remove the lock barrel.

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.
