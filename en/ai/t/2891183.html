Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

Position a column jack beneath the engine to support it safely.

1. Unscrew retaining bolt (1a) and withdraw. Unscrew retaining bolt (1b).

2. Remove the rigid mount from the engine.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the rigid mount and tighten the bolts retaining it to the gearbox to torque.

Insert the bolt into the hole on the mount and tighten to torque.

Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

Raise the car.

Place a hydraulic column jack beneath the gearbox in order to work in safety.

1. Unscrew the bolt securing the mount to the block and withdraw.

2. Undo the bolts securing the block to the gearbox.

3. Remove the engine block.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods
