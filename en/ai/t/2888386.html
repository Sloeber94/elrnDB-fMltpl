Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the side lights on, when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the headlamp washer function does not work properly, but the windscreen washer does work

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a preliminary check on the fluid level in the reservoir; also make sure that it is not leaking.

Continue to Step 1

Level too low

Top up level

Reservoir damaged

Reservoir damaged Op. 5050A10 WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID RESERVOIR (IN ENGINE BAY) - R+R

1

HEADLAMP WASHER TIMER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the headlamp washer timer

( See E3022 HEADLAMP WASHER).

Continue to Step 2

Timer defective

Replace the timer

2

HEADLAMP WASHER ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • headlamp washer fuse
  • headlamp washer timer supply
  • connection between the headlamp washer timer and the steering column switch unit
  • connection between the headlamp washer timer and the electric headlamp washer pump

( See E3022 HEADLAMP WASHER)

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

3
