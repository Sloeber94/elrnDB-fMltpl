Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04

Description

The horns are operated by pressing one of the two sides of the steering wheel.

The horn circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a specific relay located in the junction box.

For safety reasons, the horns can be operated at any time, even when the key is turned to OFF (or removed).
