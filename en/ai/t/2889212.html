Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Description

The vehicle is also available, on request, prepared for the installation of a cellular telephone.

This preparation consists of:

  • supply connector for recharging the cellular telephone; it is located near the radio and also acts as a support for the actual telephone.
  • "hands-free" speaker, in the passenger side door (it is the same speaker as the radio with two connections: one for the radio and one for the cellular telephone "hands-free" function).

The connector for the cellular telephone has two supplies; one direct and one only with the ignition switched ON; there is also a supply with the side lights on to light up the telephone, if fitted.

The above two lines are protected by two fuses in the junction unit.

The preparation does not include a microphone or a GSM aerial. These devices are supplied together with the various telephone installation kits.
