With the engine running and the gearbox in neutral, there is noise from the area of the gearbox

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect whether the fault is accompanied by an oil leak from the gearbox / differential

Press the clutch pedal and check whether the noise increases

Check the gearbox / differential oil level Op. 2110A04

Continue to Step 1

Oil leaks from the gearbox

See table X15 GEARBOX/DIFFERENTIAL OIL LEAKS

With the clutch pressed, the noise disappears or decreases considerably

The noise is coming from inside the gearbox:

See table S20 GEARBOX DIFFERENTIAL UNIT NOISY'/ / GENERAL

Oil level too low

Restore the correct level and carry out a road test. If the defect persists,

Continue to Step 1

1

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check

  • that the clutch plate is not distorted
  • that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring

Continue to Step 2

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Presence of oil or grease

Also replace the gearbox input oil seal Op. 2110C22
