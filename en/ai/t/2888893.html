Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 09/03, da 06/99 a 03/02, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04

Description

The vehicle is equipped with a reversing light located at the rear in the right side light cluster.

It is operated each time reverse gear is engaged through the special switch on the outside of the gearbox casing.

The circuit is supplied with the ignition switched ON and is protected by a fuse, in the junction unit.

The reversing signal is sent to the navigation control unit E4090 NAVIGATOR and to the parking sensor system which only works when reverse gear is engaged E3090 PARKING SENSOR .
