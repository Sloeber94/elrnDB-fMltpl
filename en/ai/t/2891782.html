7040A04 facia lower trim-r r

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Move the shield slightly.

3. Release the relay support from the shield.

Position a suitable container for collecting the coolant fluid.

4. Undo the bolt.

5. Remove the plate.

6. Move the pipes slightly.

7. Remove the gaskets.

8. Extract the heater from the casing.
