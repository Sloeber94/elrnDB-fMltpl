With the engine running and the vehicle stationary, the steering wheel oscillates and vibrates

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check the engine idle speed

Continue to Step 1

Engine idle irregular

See table D10 ENGINE IDLE IRREGULAR

1

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the engine supports and reaction rod

Continue to Step 2

Engine supports faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008A)

Reaction rod faulty

Bed in the fixings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque (see Assembly 1008C)

2

STEERING COLUMN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition and the efficiency of the steering column support. In particular, check:

  • that there is no clearance between the support bushes and the steering column
  • that the steering column joints are correctly tightened
  • the condition of the steering wheel adjustment device (correct clearance between device and steering column)

Continue to Step 3

Bushes worn or damaged

Restore the efficiency of the steering column.

If necessary, replace the bearings involved Op. 4110A31 Steering control shaft SUPPORT - R.R. for versions with Air Bag

Spider joint clearance too large

Tighten the bolts or replace the steering column Op. 4110A31 Steering control shaft SUPPORT - R.R. for versions with Air Bag

Adjustment device clearance too large

Restore the efficiency of the steering wheel adjustment device. If necessary, replace it Op. 4110A31 Steering control shaft SUPPORT - R.R. for versions with Air Bag

3

TIMING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the engine timing is correct

Continue to Step 4

Timing not correct

Restore the correct engine timing Op. 1032B14 toothed timing belt - r + r for timing adjustment or replacement versions with air conditioner
