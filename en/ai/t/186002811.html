Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Description

The intermittent turn signals and hazard warning lights delimit the entire vehicle perimeter.

The right and left side turn signals are activated by raising or lowering a steering wheel stalk switch.

The hazard warning lights (right and left side indicators operated simultaneously) are activated by means of a red button in the centre of the control panel in the dashboard.

The turn signals are ignition-operated but the hazard warning lights can always be operated for safety reasons because they are supplied directly by the battery.

The turn signal and hazard warning light circuits are protected by two different fuses.

Two warning lights in the instrument panel, also the intermittent type, signal that the left and right indicators have been switched on; one is only for if there is a trailer fitted.

There are two LEDs in the red hazard warning lights button: the first is ignition-operated and lights up the ideogram; the second, red one, comes on intermittently when the hazard warning lights are activated.

Versions with alarm:

the hazard warning lights are also activated by the alarm system which is connected directly to the power supply for these lights.

Versions without alarm:

the hazard warning lights are operated - when the door remote control is activated - by a special relay next to the junction unit.

The hazard warning lights are also activated by the alarm system which is connected directly to the power supply for these lights.

Connection for a trailer:

by replacing the intermittent device with a double charge it is possible to activate the connection that signals that either the left or the right indicator has been switched on by means of a special warning light in the instrument panel.
