Refitting

1. Using a hammer and suitable chisel, relieve the staking on ad then undo the wheel hub nut. Use a 36 mm diameter socket which is suitably machined so that it fits tightly on the whole surface of the nut.

2. Undo the bolts securing the shock absorber strut.

3. Undo the fixing nut (3a) and using the tool (3b), remove the steering balljoint head from the vertical link.
