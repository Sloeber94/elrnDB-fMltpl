Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the controls are operated, the interior lights do not work

Results of the tests

1

CHECK INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly (which often also contains the light switch)

Continue to Step 2

Bulb damaged

Change bulbs

Bulb holder faulty

Repair or replace the bulb holder assembly

2

CHECK INTERIOR LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • services fuse
  • light supply
  • light earth
  • connections between light and switch (if outside light) and lights
  • connections between switch (if outside light) and earth

( See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTSe See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
