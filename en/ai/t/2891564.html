4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R.

1. Undo the bolts securing the vertical link to the suspension.

When dismantling one or more bolts fixing the driveshafts to the differential, they must always be replaced with new ones because the thread has undergone a special treatment which is no longer guaranteed to be effective when the bolts are removed.

2. Undo the bolts securing the drive shaft to the intermediate shaft and move the drive shaft aside.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the intermediate drive shaft (1b).
