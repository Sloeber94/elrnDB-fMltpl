Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 09/03, da 06/99 a 03/02, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04

Description

The vehicle has two additional fog lamps for use in poor visibility conditions.

The fog lamps are activated through a special button in the switch panel in the centre of the dashboard; this button, which is slightly lit with the ignition switched ON, becomes much brighter when the actual lights are switched on.

The circuit for the fog lamps is protected by a special fuse and operated by a special relay, next to the junction unit.

The fog lamps only come on when the side lights are switched on.

In addition, if the key is turned ot the OFF position or the side lights are switched off, the fog lamps are switched off even if the button is pressed: this control logic is managed by a special electronic device located inside the switch unit.
