Removing

Refitting

Undo the bolts securing the radiator to the upper support beam.

Secure the radiator to the body using cord or wire.

Raise the car.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the crossmember to the bodyshell.

2. Remove the engine radiator support beam.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Removing

Fit the beam and tighten the bolts to the specified torque.
