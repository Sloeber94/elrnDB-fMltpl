Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During braking, a whistle or screech may be heard from the area of the wheels. Vibration may also be detected in the interior compartment.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Steer to the right or the left and check whether the noise increases.

Noise does not change:

Continue to Step 1

The noise varies during steering

See table S45 NOISE' FRONT / ROUND BENDS

1

CHECK FRICTION SURFACES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check

  • condition of front pad friction surfaces
  • condition of rear pad friction surfaces

Continue to Step 3

Friction surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water

Clean, check and eliminate the causes of the fault

Front brake pads damaged or wrong type

Replace pads Op. 3310A60 front brake pads - replace

Rear shoes damaged or wrong type

Replace the shoes Op. 3320B10

2

CHECK FRONT DISCS AND CALIPERS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check condition of the front brake discs

Check the condition of the brake caliper pistons

Continue to Step 4

Front discs worn or damaged

Grind the discs Op. 3310A54 front brake discs removed - grind, or replace if necessary

Front caliper pistons worn or seized

Replace brake calipers Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (one) - R.R.

3

CHECK REAR DRUMS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the rear brake drums

Drum ovalisation, even if slight, causes increased noise

Continue to Step 5

Rear drums worn or damaged

Replace the drums Op. 3320B30 lh or rh rear brake drum (one) r r

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
