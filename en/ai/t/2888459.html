Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving along a straight, even road, there is a noise from the front, often accompanied by vibrations.

As the speed of the vehicle increases, so too does the noise

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily, check the type, condition and inflation pressure of the tyres

Continue to Step 1

Pressure not correct

Restore the tyre inflation pressures

Tyres not suitable or worn

Replace the tyres

WHEEL BEARINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a test, gradually steering to the right or the left; check whether the intensity of the noise increases

The noise remains constant:

Continue to Step 2

The noise increases greatly with the steering angle: bearing worn or damaged

Replace the front wheel hub bearing Op. 4450C32 one front wheel hub bearing, lh or rh - replace

2

INTERMEDIATE SHAFT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Especially if the fault is accompanied by vibrations

Check:

  • the operation of the intermediate shaft bearing
  • the condition of the intermediate shaft

Continue to Step 3

Intermediate shaft worn or distorted

Replace the intermediate shaft Op. 2710A31 intermediate drive shaft - r r

Intermediate shaft bearing worn

Replace the intermediate shaft bearing Op. 2710A34 intermediate drive shaft bearing support - r + r with intermediate drive shaft removed for versions with air conditioner
