When the key is turned to the On position the instruments light up, but the starter motor does not turn

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Place the key in the ON position and check that the recharging warning light comes on (it should then go out when the engine is started up).
