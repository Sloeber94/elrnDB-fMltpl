Operational description

The alarm system is managed by alarm control unit M030

, incorporated with the siren.

The control unit receives a direct supply from the battery at pin 3 of connector A from the line for maxifuse J/B1 of B5

and protected by fuse F10

of junction unit B1

, and at pin 7 of connector B (supply for direction indicators) from the line protected by fuse F9

; of junction unit B1

.

The ignition-operated supply (INT) reaches pin 12 of connector B of control unit M30

. via the line protected by fuse F1

of junction unit B1

.

Pin 9 of connector A is earthed.

The control unit M30

controls the closing of the doors via the switches N50

, N55

, N51

, N56

which, if the door is open, sends an earth signal to pins 8, 15, 9, 16, respectively, of connector B.

From pin 14 of connector B of M30

the doors open signal departs for the instrument panel

Vedere E4010 STRUMENTO DI BORDO

.

The bonnet is controlled by switch I10

which is connected to pin 18 of connector B, whilst the boot is controlled by switch I11

which is connected to pin 17 of connector B.

From pin 1 of connector A of M30

the control signal for the alarm LED, located in the centre of the dashboard E40 is sent

.

The volumetric sensors K62

receive a power supply and earth from pins 8 and 11, respectively, of connector A of M30

and send the alarm control signal to pin 12 of connector A of M30

.

From pins 1 and 2 of connector B of M30

the control signals for the direction indicators depart:

Vedere E2020 LUCI DIREZIONE / EMERGENZA

.

The receiver for the remote control M35

receives a direct power supply from the battery at pin 3 from the line protected by fuse F10

of junction unit B1

and an ignition-controlled power supply (INT) at pin 7 from the line protected by fuse B26

. Pin 4 is earthed.
