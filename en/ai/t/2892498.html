Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04

Functional description

The switch I30 is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT) from the line protected by the fuse F1 for the junction unit B1.

When the brake pedal is pressed, the switch closes and sends the supply to the side brake lights in the rear light clusters F30 and F31 and to the centre rear light F40 .
