Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The clutch is the dry, single plate type with a thrust bearing always in contact with the flexible coupling spring.

The driven disc is driven by the pressure exerted by a diaphram spring. The clutch release is achieved through a flexible cable operated mechanically by the pedal.
