Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, the heater plugs warning light does not come on and then, after a few moments, it does not go off

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket

Carry out the active diagnosis of the heater plugs warning light

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Control unit fault

Check and, if necesary, replace the engine management control unit Op. 1060G80 ds electric injection control unit - r r

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT - Description

2

HEATER PLUGS WARNING LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the connection between instrument and engine management unit ( See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT - Description).

Continue to Step 3

Connections broken

Restore the connections

3

CHECK INSTRUMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the glow plug warning light, located in the instrument see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5560 INSTRUMENT PANEL

The warning lights consist of LEDs built into the electronic circuit, and so CANNOT be replaced.

Continue to Step 4

Instrument not working

Change the instrument Op. 5560B10 analogue control panel - r+r

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
