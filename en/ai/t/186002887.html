Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04; only with equipment: ESP

Operational description

The N.A. contact for the switch - for I30

, receives an ignition-controlled power supply (INT) from fuse F1

of the junction unit B1

.

When the brake pedal is pressed, the switch closes and sends a power supply to the side brake lights in the rear light clusters F30

and F31

and to the centre rear light cluster F40
