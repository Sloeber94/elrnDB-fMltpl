Operational description

Engaging reverse gear closes the switch I20

and provides a power supply to the right rear light cluster F31

Vedere E2022 LUCI RETROMARCIA

. The same line, from connector B of F31, supplies the parking sensor control unit M84

at pin 1 of connector and provides an earth at pin 16 of the same connector

The parking sensor speaker P44

sends two signals to the parking sensor control unit M84

from pins 2 and 10 of connector A; pins 9 and 6 of the same connector are bridged.

The connectors for the four parking sensors K97 are all connected to connector B of the control unit M84, respectively:

  • to pins 9, 5, 12 of M84 to the sensor K97A ;
  • to pins 7, 3, 10 of M84 to the senor K97B ;
  • to pins 1, 2, 6 of M84 to the sensor K97C ;
  • to pins 8, 4, 1 of M84 to the sensor K97D ;
