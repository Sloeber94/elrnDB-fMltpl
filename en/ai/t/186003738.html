WARNINGS

Observe the following precautions when working on the front door assembly:

  • When working on the hinges, take care not to damage the paintwork.
  • Following an adjustment operation that required the hinges to be moved, the tailgate must be removed and the hinge area repainted.
  • To remove, call on the assistance of a second operator and rest the tailgate on a stable support to avoid damage to the paintwork.
