Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Operational description

The circuit for the main beam headlights is activated by the steering column switch unit H5

.

Placing the switch in the main beam headlights position or closing the "flashing" contact supplies the main beams for the headlights F10

(left) and F11

(right): the supply is ignition operated (INT/A) for the line protected by fuses F6

and F7

from the junction unit B1

for the right and left headlights, respectively.

The supply line for the left headlight also sends a signal to the instrument panel E50

- pin 12 of connector C - to supply the "main beam headlights on" warning light; pin 1 of connector B of E50

provides the earth.
