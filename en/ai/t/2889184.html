Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Description

The vehicle is prepared for the installation of a radio system.

This preparation comprises:

  • radio compartment
  • radio supply cables
  • front loudspeakers located in the doors and separate tweeters, in the front pillars;
  • rear speakers, located in the doors;
  • leads connecting the radio to the speakers;
  • aerial;
  • aerial coaxial cable.

The radio circuit is supplied with the key turned to the ON position, or directly from the battery (which allows tuning settings, security codes, etc. to be stored).

The above two lines are protected by two fuses in the junction unit.

In addition to the radio there is also a supply with the side lights on to light up the radio controls.

A radio, specially designed for this vehicle, is available on request and is fitted during production. Full radio specifications and operating instructions are given in the OWNER'S HANDBOOK.

On request, the radio can be connected to a CD player, located under the outside front passenger seat.
