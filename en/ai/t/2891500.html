1043B49 coil of lpg fuel system intake solenoid r rwith tank and valve unit cover removed .

Before servicing, ensure the tank has been sent to the appropriate centres for emptying and rendering inert, after agreement with T.D.A. (Italy)/Teseo (abroad).

Before working on the solenoid valve piston, drain any air remaining in the tank following the procedure described 1043A08 lpg tank check seal .

If a strong smell of LPG should be smelt during this operation, suspend the procedure immediately and send the tank back to the company responsible for drainage. A slight smell of LPG is considered normal.

1. Undo and remove the solenoid valve piston complete with seal.
