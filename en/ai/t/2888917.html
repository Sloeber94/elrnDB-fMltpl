Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, fino a 05/04

Description

The vehicle is fitted with an extra bright rear fog lamp, located in the left rear light cluster which can be used in all poor visibility conditions.

The rear fog lamp is activated by a special button in the switch unit in the centre of the dashboard; The button is faintly lit with the key turned ON and more brightly lit when the light is turned on.

The rear fog lamps are enabled when the dipped beams are on, or when the fog lamps are on.

This control logic is managed by a special electronic device located in the switch uni.
