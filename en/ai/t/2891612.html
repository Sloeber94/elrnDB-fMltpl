3330B23 brake pedal - r r for rhd versions

1. Working on the left side of the pedal assembly, remove the fastening clip (1a) and separate the brake servo rod (1b) from the bracket.

2. Undo the nut (2a) fixing the brake servo bracket (2b) and remove the bracket.

3. Remove the clip (3a) and disconnect the clutch pump push rod (3b) from the pedal.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

1. Working on the right side of the pedal assembly, remove the retaining ring (1a) and the washer (1b) underneath.

2. Release the brake pedal support pin towards the vehicle interior.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

1. Remove the retaining ring (1a) and release the reaction spring (1b) from the mounting pin on the clutch pedal (1c).

2. Undo the nut (2a) fastening the clutch pedal cover (2b) and remove the washer (2c), partly release the cover towards the vehicle exterior until the pedal (2d) can be removed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive
