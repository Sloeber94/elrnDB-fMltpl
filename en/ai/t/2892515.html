Applies to: all engines; production: da 04/02 a 08/03

Functional description

The boot opening switch I11 sends an earth signal, with the boot open, to the boot light G40 .

It comes on through a power supply coming from the integrated services control unit M40 .

The control unit M40 is supplied directly from the battery at pin 8 of connector B through the line of the maxifuse J/B2 of B5 via fuse F19 and via the ignition (INT) - pin 7 of connector B - from line of fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 . Pin 13 of connector B provides the earth.

Having received the boot open signal at pin 9 of connector B, the control unit M40 sends the timed supply to the light G40 from pin 11 of connector B.

The sun visor light G32 is supplied directly by the battery via fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 and is activated by the switch inside the actual light.
