L10 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT COOLING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The control unit operates the fan which sends air into the passenger compartment normally with the pre-set flows, but this air is at the outside temperature and is not cooled.

1

COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) carry out the active diagnosis of the operation of the compressore relay (in the engine management control unit ambient)

Continue to Step 2

Compressore relay faulty

Check and, if necessary, replace the compressor engagement relay

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections between engine management control unit, relay and compressor coupling See E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT

2

CHECK COMPRESSOR ACTIVATION ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • compressor activation control
  • three-stage pressure switch ( See Test 5040BA Check operation of three-stage pressure switch )
  • antifrost thermostat - connection between compressor activation relay and control

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Pressure switch defective

Replace the pressure switch Op. 5040B14 air conditioner system multi-level pressure switch - r+r

Antifrost thermostat defective

Replace antifrost thermostat Op. 5040B64 sensor on evaporator for a/c ecu - r + r

Control defective

Change control unit Op. 7065C10 air conditioning control panel - r+r

3

CHECK COMPRESSOR ELECTRO-MAGNET COUPLING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the compressor electro-magnet coupling and, in particular, that it is correctly connected to earth

Continue to Step 4

Compressor electro-magnet coupling malfunction

Replace the compressor Op. 5040A20 air conditioner compressor - r+r - includes fluid level check and top up if necessarycomplete with electro-magnet coupling

4

SYSTEM PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the system pressure: See Test 5040B Check on operation of air conditioning system (pressures)

Continue to Step 5

High pressure does not increase or low pressure decreases too much: leaks in the circuit

Locate these leaks Op. 5040A14 air conditioner cooling circuit - check and repair any leaks by tightening fittings, then restore the condition of the circuit

High pressure continues to increase: and the compressor cooling fan does not operate.

See table D31 ENGINE OVERHEATING / WITH FAN NOT WORKING

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there are no reasons why the climate control control unit or the engine control unit do not enable the engagement of the cooling circuit.

For more details see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5040 AIR CONDITIONING CASING AND COMPONENTS
