J20 REARSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the button in the control panel in the centre of the dashboard is pressed, neither the heated rear windscreen nor the rear view mirrors warm up properly.

Results of the tests

1

DEMISTING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • heated rear windscreen fuse
  • heated rear windscreen relay
  • connection between heated rear windscreen relay and control panel
  • control panel power supply

( See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING).

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

2

HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the heated rear windscreen switch in the control panel in the centre of the dashboard See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

The actual switch is inside an electronic control device which manages the operating logic of the demisting function.

Continue to Step 3

Heated rear windscreen switch faulty

Renew the heated rear windscreen switch or replace the control panel Op. 7065C10 air conditioning control panel - r+r

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
