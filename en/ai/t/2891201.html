Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

Raise the car.

Place a container under the vehicle to collect the engine oil.

1. Undo the engine oil drain plug on the oil sump and drain the oil.

Used engine oil and old oil filters contain substances dangerous to the environment. Dispose of the old oil and filters in containers designed for this purpose.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods
