Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The boot opening switch I11sends an earth signal, with the boot open, to the boot lightG40.

The same signal is sent to the alarm control unit ( E7015 ALARM ).

The supply arrives via the direct line, - coming from the maxifuse J/B1ofB5- protected by fuseF10of the junction unitB1

.The sun visor light G32is also supplied directly by the battery through fuseF10of the junction unitB1and is activated wih the switch inside the actual light.
