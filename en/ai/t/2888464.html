Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the engine running, in the case of brisk acceleration / variations in torque, there is a noise (banging) in the area of the gearbox / transmission

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check that the wheels are correctly fixed

Continue to Step 1

Fixing not correct

Tighten the wheels correctly Op. 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

1

SPRING/SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the spring/damper unit and ensure the following in particular:

  • the spring/shock absorber assembly fixings are correct
  • there is no contact between the first spring coil and the end of the spring

Continue to Step 2

Fastenings not correct

Restore the fixings for the spring/shock absorber assembly Op. 4110A12

Spring/shock absorber assembly damaged

Replace the spring/shock absorber assembly Op. 4110A12

2

FRONT SUSPENSION BUSHES AND FLEXIBLE MOUNTINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the bushes and mountings. In the order:

  • flexible supports for front selector forks and track control arms
  • anti-roll bar flexible mountings

Check in particular the condition of the rear wishbone bush

Continue to Step 3

Rear wishbone bush damaged

Replace the wishbone Op. 4410B40 lower wishbone (with balljoint) of left or right front suspension (one) - r+r -check wheel geometry separately

Anti-roll bar mountings worn or damaged

Replace the mountings Op. 4410C10 front suspension stabilizer bar - r+r - check wheel geometry separately

3

CHECK THE STEERING LINK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the steering link.

Continue to Step 4

Tie-rods damaged or worn

Replace the links Op. 4110B10 steering links - r+r on vehicle - check tow-in/tow-out separately

4

CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the transmission constant velocity joints

Continue to Step 5

Joint damaged

Replace the relevant joint (see Assembly 2710A)

5

SUSPENSION FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the suspension is correctly fixed to the bodyshell, through the front crossmember

Continue to Step 6

Fastenings not correct

Restore the front beam fastenings Op. 4110D13

6

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
