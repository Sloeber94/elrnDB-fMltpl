Removing

Refitting

1. Unscrew fitting (1a) and remoe the condensed oil return pipe.

2. Unscrew retaining bolts (2a) and remove vibration-proof partition (2b) fastened to the oil sump.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the vibration-proof partition into its seat in the oil sump.

Tighten the retaining bolts.

Fit the condensed oil return pipe.

Tighten the fitting.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the connector for the condensation oil return pipe to the sump.

2. Remove the inner connector for the condensation oil return pipe to the sump complete with O-ring.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the crankcase oil sump partition (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the crankcase oil sump partition and fix it tightening the bolts to torque.
