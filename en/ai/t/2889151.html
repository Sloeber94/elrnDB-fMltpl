Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Description

The seats can be mechanically adjusted into numerous position:

  • adjustment of the backrest angle (all seats);
  • lengthwise adjustment (front seats and centre rear seat - opt.);
  • lumbar adjustment (driver's front seat - opt);

The driver's front seat is also electrically adjustable for height: an electric motor operated by a switch at the side of the actual seat moves a pantograph which produces a 20 cm +/- travel of the seat. The switch is lit up when the ignition is ON.

The system only operates when the ignition is switched ON. The circuit is controlled by the optional services relay and protected by a fuse which also protects the mini bar wiring, both of which are located next to the junction unit.
