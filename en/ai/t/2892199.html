Removing

Refitting

1. Disengage the sunshade lug from its seating and move the sunshade.

2. Remove the embellishments.

3. Undo the screws securing the spectacle holder.

4. Remove the spectacle holder.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the spectacle holder is undamaged.

Fit the spectacle holder.

Tighten the bolts.

Fit the embellishments.

Engage the sunshade lug in its seating.

Check that the spectacle holder moves smoothly.
