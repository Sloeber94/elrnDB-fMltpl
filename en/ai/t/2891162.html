Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Place the car on a lift.

Raise the car.

Undo the gearbox casing filler plug.

Undo the gearbox casing drain plug and collect the oil in a suitable container.

Do not disperse the oil into the environment, as indiscriminate dispersal of this product causes pollution.

Clean the drain plug.

Screw on the drain plug and tighten to torque.
