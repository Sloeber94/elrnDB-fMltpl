Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The CODE control unit M20is supplied at pin 3 by the battery from the line for the maxifuse EFI (UGD) ofB5and is protected by fuseB35.

The supply controlled by the ignition (15/54) reaches pin 8 through the line protected bu fuse B25.

Pin 4 is earthed.

The line leading from pin 6 is a serial line leading to engine management unit M10, which is responsible for identifying the key and exchanging codes.

It is possible to "read" the control unit autodiagnostic system, by connecting to the autodiagnostic connector R10from pin 5 of the control unitM20.

The autodiagnostic system also produces the signal for the "CODE failure" warning light, located in the instrument panel E50: it involves an earth signal coming out of pin 2 of the control unitM20.

The instrument panel E50is controlled by the ignition (INT) at pin 2 of connector A from the line protected by fuseF1of the junction unitB1: when an earth signal reaches pin 12 of connector C, the "CODE warning" light comes on.

A second control unit connector is connected via two leads to the aerial, fitted coaxially to the ignition key, in order to detect key insertion and rotation.
