Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Description

The vehicle is fitted with an extra bright rear fog lamp, located in the left rear light cluster which can be used in all poor visibility conditions.

The rear fog lamp is activated through the special button in the switch unit in the centre of the dashboard; the button is dimly lit when the ignition is switched ON and becomes much brighter when the actual light is switched on.

The rear fog lamps are enabled when the dipped beams are on, or when the fog lamps are on.

This control logic is managed by a special electronic device located in the switch unit.
