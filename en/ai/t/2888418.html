Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The control unit operates the fan which sends air into the passenger compartment normally with the pre-set flows, but this air lowers the high temperature in the passenger compartment too much

Results of the tests

1

THREE STAGE PRESSURE SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the three stage pressure switch See Test 5040BA Check operation of three-stage pressure switch

Continue to Step 2

Pressure switch defective

Replace the pressure switch Op. 5040B14 air conditioner system multi-level pressure switch - r+r

Connections not correct

Restore the connections with the engine management control unit See E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT

2

SYSTEM PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the system pressure: See Test 5040B Check on operation of air conditioning system (pressures)

Continue to Step 3

High pressure does not increase or low pressure decreases too much: insufficient fluid

High pressure continues to increase: too much fluid

Recharge the system: Op. 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
