Refitting

1. Rotate the retaining buttons to the end of their travel using a screwdriver, then remove them.

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Remove the oddment pouch.

Removing

Fit the retaining buttons, then rotate them in their seating to the end of their travel with a screwdriver.
