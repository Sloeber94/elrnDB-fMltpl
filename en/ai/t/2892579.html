Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The connector D75receives a supply directly (pin 9) from the battery via the line for fuseF10of the junction unitB1and the maxifuseJ/B1ofB5.

It receives a supply controlled by the ignition (INT) (pin 7) via the line for fuse F1of the junction unitB1.

Pin 8 receives a supply, with the side lights on, via the line protected by fuse F2of the junction unitB1( See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS ).

Pin 10 of connector D75is earthed, whilst pins 5 and 6 provide the signal for the speakerP36, connector B (hands-free); connector A is used by the radio ( See E3510 RADIO ).

The radio is also reached by pin 1 which manages the "MUTE" function if a call is received.
