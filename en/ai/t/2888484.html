Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After rain or washing, water enters the car in the region of one of the front doors

Results of the tests

1

CHECK DOOR COMPARTMENT SEAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door seal In particular, check:

  • absence of wrinkles in the seal
  • condition of seal

Continue to Step 2

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7005A34 front side door compartment seal r r

2

CHECK DOOR ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the door is properly fitted. In particular, ascertain:

  • door - compartment correctly aligned
  • seal correctly bonded to the door

Continue to Step 3

door incorrectly fitted

Refit the door properly Op. 7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment

3

CHECK MOVING WINDOW GLASS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the moving window glass seal In particular, ascertain:

  • the window glass is properly sealed
  • the window glass channel is correctlly positioned
  • the glass scraper is correctly positioned

Continue to Step 4

Channel incorrectly positioned

Refit the channel properly Op. 7005D18 front side door descending window glass channel r r

Window glass incorrectly fitted

Refit the moving window glass guide correctly Op. 7005D23 front side door descending window glass guide one r r

Glass scraper incorrectly fitted

Refit the glass scraper correctly Op. 7005D26 front side descending window glass scraper (outer) r r

4

CHECK FIXED WINDOW GLASS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check fixed window seal

Continue to Step 5

Window glass incorrectly fitted

Refit the mobile window glass correctly Op. 7005D44 front side door fixed window(1), lh or rh (bondedtype) - r r includes cleaning housing

5

CHECK DOOR DRAINAGE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the drainage on the lower part of the doors. In particular, check for dirt, leaves etc.

Continue to Step 6

Dirt in drains

Clean the drains. If necessary, refit the lower seal correctly Op. 7005A46 front side door lower seal r r

6

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
