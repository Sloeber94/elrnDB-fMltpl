Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Description

The headlight light beam can be adjusted directly from the driver's seat depending on the load. The correct headlamp alignment is quickly achieved in this way. The adjustment device takes the form of a motor fitted on both dipped beams. It is able to tilt the headlamps to lower the light beam if the car is particularly loaded and raise the lamps when the load reduces.

A special knob located in the dashboard, to the right of the steering wheel, can be placed in four different positions:

  • position 0 - up to three persons in the front seats;
  • position 1 - six persons;
  • position 2 - six persons plus load in the luggage compartment;
  • position 3 - driver plus maximum permitted load all stowed in the luggage compartment.

The device is enabled only with the dipped beams on.

The headlamp alignment circuit is protectd by the dipped headlamp circuit fuses located in the junction unit.
