1. Undo the bolts securing the electric motor.

2. Remove the electric motor.

3. Undo the guide bolts.

4. Remove the guides complete with traction cable.

5. Undo the bolt.

6. Remove the motor mounting complete with traction cable tubes.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. If necessary, remove the seal (1a) from its seating, detaching it from the silicone glue (1b).

Refitting

Removing

1. If the seal has been removed from the sunroof, carefully clean the seal seat on the sunroof with compressed air and degrease thoroughly using heptane.

2. Carefully clean the bottom of the sea with compressed air and thoroughly degrease with heptane.

3. Apply the appropriate silicone product along the entire perimeter.

4. Position the seal in its seating.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Position the sunroof temporarily on the seal and check that the contact on the sunroof is correct.

Wait at least 5 hours before opening the sunroof.

Position the motor mounting complete with traction cable tubes, and secure it with the bolt.

Fit the guides complete with traction cables and secure them with their bolts.

The electric motor is of the continuous cycle type, so no adjustment is required before fittiing.

Manually place the sunroof at the rear end-of-travel position and, in this position, fit the electric motor and fix it with the bolts.
