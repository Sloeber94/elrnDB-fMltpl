The warning light indicates a decrease in pressure in the engine lubrication circuit.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • engine oil level
  • operation of the warning light in the panel and the switch

Check that the type of oil is suitable for the engine in question

Continue to Step 1

Engine oil level too low

Top up the engine oil level Op. 1084A10 ENGINE OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary

Faults in the warning light circuit

See table K30 MINIMUM ENGINE OIL PRESSURE WARNING LIGHT NOT WORKING

1

ENGINE OIL TYPE AND FILTER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the engine oil is the recommended type in the correct quantity

Check that the oil filter is the recommended type and that it is correctly fitted

Continue to Step 2

Engine oil unsuitable

Change the engine oil

Oil filter unsuitable or incorrectly fitted

Replace the engine oil filter Op. 1084B30 engine oil filter r r

2

OIL PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the oil pump. In particular check:

  • condition and cleanliness of oil pressure relief valve
  • condition of spring

Continue to Step 3

Oil pump damaged or distorted

Pressure relief valve damaged

Replace the oil pump Op. 1084B16

3

LUBRICATION SYSTEM CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a general check on the lubrication system Op. 0510C20 lubrication system - check delivery pressure

Continue to Step 4

Faults in the lubrication system

Restore the lubrication system (see Assembly 1054B)

4

MAIN/BIG END BEARINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Overhaul the engine: in particular check the condition of the main and big end bearings

Continue to Step 5

Main and big end bearings faulty

Replace main and big end bearings Op. 1024A10 crank shaft - r + r with engine removed - check main and connecting rod bearingand replace if necessary
