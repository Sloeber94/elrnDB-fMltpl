Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Functional description

The relay J20controls the compressor engagment circuit.

The relay is supplied by relay R1: line controlled by the ignition INT/A, protected by fuseF15: both are housed in the junction unitB1.

The supply for the relay R1comes from the direct line of the maxifuseJ/B1ofB5, whilst the coil is supplied by the line (INT/A) protected by fuseB26.

The relay J20coil is always supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) protected by fuseB27: this line also supplies the switch unitH80.

Pressing the button in group H80, sends two signals:

  • one to the electronic frost thermostat K86 which, if the temperature of the evaporator is not too low, sends another signal to the 3 stage pressure switch K5 . This, in turn, if the pressure in the circuit is correct, does not interrupt the signal which reaches the engine control unit M10 . Control unit M10 uses its own internal software to send an (earth) signal to activate the compressor which energizes the relay J20 . The relay operates the solenoid valve, at 12 V, which switches on the air conditioning compressor L20 .
  • a second signal energizes the relay J34 coil which activates the first speed for the fan N85 , according to the description ( See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING ).

The fan operates at the first speed if the button for engaging the compressor is pressed, even if the control knob H81is in position "0".
