Removing

Refitting

Place the car on a ramp.

Position a column jack beneath the engine to support it safely.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the rigid mount to the reinforcement.

2. Unscrew and withdraw retaining bolt (2a). Unscrew retaining nuts (2b).

3. Remove the rigid mount from the engine.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods
