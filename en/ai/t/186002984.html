Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two extra-bright rear fog lamps housed in the rear light clusters that can be used when visibility is poor.

The rear fog lamps are activated via the special button located in the switch unit in the centre of the dashboard; the button is dimly lit up with the ignition ON and then comes on more brightly when the actual lights are switched on.

The enablement for switching on the rear fog lamps depends on the dipped headlights or the fog lights being switched on.

This control logic is managed by a special electronic device located inside the switch unit.
