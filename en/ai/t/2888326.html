B25 THE ENGINE STARTS UP AND CUTS OUT AFTER A FEW SECONDS

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER

When the engine fuel system selector is set at LPG, the engine starts up normally using petrol but cuts out after a few seconds and does not switch to LPG.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that there is enough LPG in the tank checking that at least one green LED is lit up in the panel.

Check that the LPG pump relay is correctly fitted in its housing.

Continue to Step 1

LPG level sufficient

Introduce a sufficient amount of LPG

Relay not correctly fitted

Refit the relay correctly
