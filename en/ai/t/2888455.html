Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the clutch pedal is operated there is a noise (creaking)

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the engine idling and the gear lever in neutral, press the clutch pedal.

Then, with the engine running and the handbrake applied, engage first gear and release the clutch

The noise does not disappear:

Continue to Step 1

The noise disappears with the clutch pressed

See table S01 ENGINE KNOCK

The noise disappears with the gear engaged

See table S40 NOISE' FRONT / DRIVING IN A STRAIGHT LINE ON AN EVEN ROAD

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the clutch pedal travel

Also check for excessive clearance of the pedal pin

Continue to Step 2

Pedal travel not correct

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal Op. 1820A14 clutch pedal - adjust travel

2

CLUTCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • clutch disc wear or "vitrification"
  • that there is no damaged to the flywheel flexible coupling springs

Continue to Step 3

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Presence of oil or grease

Also replace the gearbox input oil seal Op. 2110C22

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
