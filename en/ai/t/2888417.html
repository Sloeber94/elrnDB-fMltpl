Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The control unit operates the fan which sends air into the passenger compartment normally, with the pre-set flows, but this air sometimes makes the temperature in the passenger compartment too low and sometimes too high.

Results of the tests

1

SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the system cooling is correct: See Test 5040A Checking operation of air conditioner (temperature)

Continue to Step 2

The temperature decreases very slowly: insufficient fluid:

Re-pressurize the system: Op. 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling

2

SYSTEM PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the system pressure: See Test 5040B Check on operation of air conditioning system (pressures)

Continue to Step 3

High pressure does not increase or low pressure decreases too much: leaks in the circuit

Locate the leaks Op. 5040A14 air conditioner cooling circuit - check and repair any leaks by tightening fittings

High pressure correct, low pressure very high

Replace the expansion valve Op. 5040B30 expansion valve (tube orifice) - r+r

High pressure high, low pressure below normal

Clean or replace the drier filter Op. 5040A44 air conditioner dehydrating filter/accumulator - r+r

Pressure trends irregular: pipes obstructed

Wash the system, see Op. 5040A10 Air conditioning refrigerant CIRCUIT - Draining and Refilling

Pressure trends irregular: evaporator obstructed.

Replace the evaporator: Op. 5040C64 a/c evaporator - r+r

3

CONDENSER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually inspect the condition of the condenser: the heat exchange surface should not be dirty or fouled

Continue to Step 4

Condenser dirty

Clean the condenser if necessary, replace it: Op. 5040A40 air conditioner condenser - r+r

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
