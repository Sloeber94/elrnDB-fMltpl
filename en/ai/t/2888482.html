V01 WATER PENETRATION PASSENGER COMPARTMENT, FRONT AREA

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When it is raining or after washing, there is water in the front of the passenger compartment.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK THAT THERE IS NO PENETRATION THROUGH THE WINDSCREEN

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the sealant on the windscreen See Test 7035A windscreen

Continue to Step 2

No sealant

Renew the sealant, following the instructions Op. 7035A04

2

WINDSCREEN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the windscreen. In particular, visually inspect:

  • the condition of the window
  • the flatness of the support surface
  • the flatness of the serigraphy

Continue to Step 3

Faults in the windscreen

Replace the windscreen Op. 7035A18 WINDSCREEN GLASS R R INCLUDING CLEANING OF HOUSING

3

WINDSCREEN BASE COVER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the cover at the base of the windscreen In particular, visually inspect

  • the condition of the cover
  • that the cover and the windscreen wiper arm are properly fixed

Continue to Step 4

Faults in the cover

Renew or replace the cover Op. 7055A18 Windscreen base TRIM - R.R.

4

CHECK ON COVER UNDER THE WINDSCREEN

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the cover under the windscreen. In particular, visually inspect

  • the condition of the cover
  • that the cover is properly fixed
  • that the main beam headlamps are correctly fixed

Continue to Step 5

Faults in the cover

Renew or replace the cover Op. 7055A20 FRONT TRIM under WINDSCREEN with main beam headlamps - R.R.

5

ENGINE COMPARTMENT BULKHEAD DRAINAGE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the drainage of the engine compartment bulkhead.

In particular, check for dirt, leaves etc.

Continue to Step 6

Dirt in drains

Clean the drains. If necessary refit correctly

6

ENGINE COMPARTMENT BULKHEAD CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the engine compartment bulkhead. In particular, ascertain:

  • the plugs are fitted
  • the plugs are correctly fitted
  • the rubber cable covers are fitted correctly
  • the sealing is efficient
  • correct assembly

Continue to Step 7

Faults in the engine compartment bulkhead

Restore the engine compartment bulkhead fitting and fixings

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
