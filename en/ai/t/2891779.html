Removing

Refitting

1. Working under the dashboard, undo the bolt.

2. Release the retainer.

3. Rotate the support in an anti-clockwise direction and gently extract it.

4. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Name

Connector

4

Passenger compartment ventilation fan

N85

5. If necessary, press the rubber anti-vibration mountings (5a) and separate the fan (5b) from the support (5c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the fan is not damaged and, if necessary, test it.

If removed, insert the fan in its housing ensuring that the rubber anti-vibration mountings are fitted.

Connect the electrical connection.

Insert the support - fan assembly in the housing and rotate it in a clockwise direction.

Engage the retainer.

Tighten the bolt.

Test the operation of the fan.
