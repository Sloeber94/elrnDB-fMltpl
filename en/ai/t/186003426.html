Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

  • Set the tester to Voltmeter mode.
  • Set the full scale deflection to 20 V, connect the positive probe to pin 60 of the Metafuel 5D0 control unit (gas level analogue signal output) and the negative probe to the vehicle earth.
  • With the key ON, check for a voltage that varies from 0.5 to 4.5 V (according to the level of gas in the cylinder).
