Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER

Important.

Remember that the LPG pressure varies in accordance with the following parameters: - composition of the propane and butane mixture. - ambient temperature - quantity of LPG present in the tank.

Connect the Examiner diagnostic instrument, select 'Control Units Test', and in the 'Parameters' environment read the 'Gas pressure' parameter. The value may vary between 2 and 18 bar.

If the pressure is around 20 bar, the cause may be an excessive supply of LPG produced by a fault in the valve 80%.
