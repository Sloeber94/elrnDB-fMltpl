Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the vehicle is driving over an unmade road or where there are a large number of bends, there is noise from the rear.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check that the tools which come with the vehicle and the spare wheel are properly fixed

Road test the vehicle on an unmade road or one with a large number of bends to identify the type of noise:

Unspecified noise:

Continue to Step 1

The noise is a creaking

Continue to Step 1

The noise is a whistling

Continue to Step 3

The noise is a knocking

Continue to Step 5

1

CHECK REAR SUSPENSION COMPONENTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear suspension components for any signs of rubbing:

  • between the suspension arm bushes and washers
  • between the dust cover and the shock absorber
  • between the end of travel buffer and the housing

Continue to Step 2

Signs of rubbing

Lubricate or replace the parts involved (see Subassembly 4420)

2

ANTI-ROLL BAR TIGHTENINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the anti-roll bar flexible mountings and that they are correctly tightened

Continue to Step 6

Anti-roll bar mountings worn or not tightened

Restore the tightenings, lubricate, or replace the mountings Op. 4420C10 rear suspension stabilizer bar - r+r

3

SHOCK ABSORBER CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check for any friction between the shock absorber stem and the seal Also check for oil leaks from the damper

Continue to Step 4

Signs of rubbing

Lubricate or replace the shock absorber Op. 4420A30 l/h or r/h rear suspension damper (one) - r+r

Oil leaks

Change the damper Op. 4420A30 l/h or r/h rear suspension damper (one) - r+r

4

WHEEL BEARINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the rear wheel hub bearing

Continue to Step 9

Bearing worn or damaged

Replace the wheel hub bearing Op. 4450D10 hub of one rear wheel, l/h or r/h - r+r with wheel removed

5

BUSHES AND FLEXIBLE MOUNTINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the bushes and mountings and that they are correctly tightened In the order:

  • track control arm ball joints
  • shock absorber attachment mounting
  • suspension end travel block

Continue to Step 6

Arm supports worn or damaged

Replace the supports (see Assembly 4420B)

Shock absorber attachment mounting damaged

Change the wishbone Op. 4420B34 rear suspension wishbone (one remaining)- r+r following op.4420b30

Shock absorber attachment mounting damaged

Replace the mounting Op. 4420A30 l/h or r/h rear suspension damper (one) - r+r

End travel block damaged

Change the end travel block Op. 4420W10 rear suspension end buffer - replace with spring disconnected

6

BRAKE FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brake pipes and handbrake cables under the bodyshell are correctly positioned and fixed

Continue to Step 7

Brake pipes not properly fixed

Suitably tighten the components involved.

Replace any damaged components, if necessary (see Assembly 3340B)

Brake cables not properly fixed

Suitably tighten the components involved.

Replace any damaged components, if necessary Op. 3380A30 handbrake cable - r+r

7

RESERVOIR FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brackets securing the reservoir to the underbody are correctly positioned and fixed

Continue to Step 8

Components which are not properly fixed cause noise

Suitably tighten the components involved.

Replace any damaged components, if necessary (see Assembly 1076B)

8

EXHAUST PIPE FIXINGS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the exhaust pipe is correctly positioned and fixed to the underbody

Continue to Step 9

Components which are not properly fixed cause noise

Suitably tighten the components involved.

Replace any damaged components, if necessary (see Assembly 1076B)

9
