4450B11 WHEEL (one) rear - R.R.

Raise the car.

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the plastic fuel filler pipe protection.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

1. Unscrew the fuel filler fitting retaining bolts.

2. Lower the upper part of the filler pipe as far as possible and disconnect the pipe leading to the tank from the vapour separator.

3. Open collars (3a) and disconnect fuel filler pipe (3b) and pipe (3c) from the supplementary vapour separator to the tank.

4. Disconnect both pipes leading from the evaporation control system to the float valves from their quick-release fittings, then remove the filler pipe together with the vapour separator.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods
