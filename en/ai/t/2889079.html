Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: SX setup and Right hand drive

Description

The electric windows in the front doors are operated through the switches in the handles in the front driver's door (both windows) and the front passenger door (passenger side only).

The operation is manual: the glass only goes up/down when the button is pressed.

Both windows can only be operated when the ignition is ON.

The control switch symbols are lit when the key is turned to MAR.

The circuit for the electric windows is protected by two special fuses, one for the driver's side and one for the passenger side and is controlled by a specific relay (which also operates the rear electric windows); all of these are next to the junction unit.
