Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During acceleration, a consistent increase in the engine speed is noticed without a corresponding increase in the speed of the vehicle

This symptom can be easily checked by pressing the clutch pedal and engaging 4th speed; when accelerating and gradually releasing the clutch pedal, the vehicle does not move and the engine does not cut out

Results of the tests

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check clutch pedal action is smooth

Check the pedal travel is correctly adjusted at both ends.

Continue to Step 2

Pedal travel not correct

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal Op. 1820A14 clutch pedal - adjust travel

2

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • clutch plate wear
  • clutch plate fouling (oil or grease)
  • that the clutch plate is not distorted
  • that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring

Continue to Step 3

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

Presence of oil or grease

Also replace the gearbox input oil seal Op. 2110C20

3

CHECK SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check for excess grease on the clutch plate hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft

Continue to Step 4

Gearbox main shaft hub faulty

Replace the gearbox main shaft (see Assembly 2110B)

Clutch plate hub bushes faulty

Restore the correct lubrication for the bushes

If necessary, replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

4

FLYWHEEL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the flywheel (in particular, the condition of the surface and that there are no signs of overheating, irregular wear, incisions or the removal of material)

Continue to Step 5

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheel Op. 1024B22 engine flywheel - r r with clutch or gearbox removed

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
