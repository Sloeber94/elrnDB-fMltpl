Removing

Refitting

1. Open the bonnet lid, then rotate the headlamp cover in an anti-clockwise direction and remove it.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection from the lamp.

3. Remove the retaining spring.

4. Extract the headlamp bulb.

If the lamp is working properly, avoid touching the bulb with your hands. If this does happen, clean the bulb with alcohol.

Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 09/03, da 06/99 a 03/02, da 04/02 a 08/03, fino a 05/04
