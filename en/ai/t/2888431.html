N01 CIGAR LIGHTER/CURRENT SOCKET NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the cigar lighter is pressed or when a consumer is connected to the special connector, the cigar lighter/current socket is not working.

The power sockets all have a heat protection device: if devices with high energy absorption are connected, this protection cuts off the supply.

Results of the tests

1

CURRENT SOCKET CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the current socket: in particular, make sure that the thermal protection device inside the socket has not been affected due to overloading

Continue to Step 2

Thermal protection affected

Replace the thermal protection or the current socket Op. 7065C72 front cigar lighter socket/power point - fasten

2

CIGAR LIGHTER ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • cigar lighter fuse (services)
  • cigar lighter earth
  • connection between cigar lighter and junction unit.

See E3070 CIGAR LIGHTER / CURRENT SOCKET

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Check that the fault is no longer present
