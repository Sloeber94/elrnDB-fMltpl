5050B32 windscreen wiper arm/s - r+r (right and left)

1. Remove the air filter cover.

Removing

Check that the trim is undamaged.

Connect the pipes to the windscreen washer jets.

Tighten the screws.

Fit the air filter cover.

Check that the windscreen washer jets are working.
