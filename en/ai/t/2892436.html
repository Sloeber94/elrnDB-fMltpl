Applies to: all engines; all production periods

5 speed gearbox positioned transversely in the engine compartment, including 3 shafts supported by bearings with the gears always meshed and a final drive reduction. The twin cable external control is made from plastic materials with excellent properties (resistance to fatigue, self-lubrication capacity, reduced weight)

1, Manual gearbox with differential 2, Manual gearbox external controls 2, Speedometer sensor
