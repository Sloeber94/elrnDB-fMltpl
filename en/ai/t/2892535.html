Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, da 06/04; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Functional description

The two-way switch controlling mirrors H31 operates electrical motors located in the right and left electrically-operated door mirrors P60 and P61 respectively.

The switch is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse B27 at pins 4 and 1 of H31 , whilst pin 2 is earthed.

Operating the switch in one direction or the other, sends one of the two motors a positive and an earth signal, thereby determining the direction of rotation.

According to the selector position, either the left motors (output signals from pins 8 and 9 of control H31 ) or the right motors (output signals from pins 7 and 10 of H31 ) are connected; the common supply comes out of pin 3 for H31 .

The mirrors are folded by motors P60 and P61 , which receive the command from pins 5 and 11, (right) and 6 and 12 (left) respectively of H31 .

Switch H31 is lit when the ignition is switched on (INT/A).
