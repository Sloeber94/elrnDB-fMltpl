Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The circuit for the mini bar is controlled by the relay J61. This is supplied by the direct line for the maxifuse J/B2ofB5. The energizing of the coil, supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) protected by the fuse B27, makes it possible to supply pin 2 of the coupling for the mini barD77, through the line protected by the fuseB21(which also protects the seat lifting).

Pin 1 for this coupling, on the other hand, is connected to earth.
