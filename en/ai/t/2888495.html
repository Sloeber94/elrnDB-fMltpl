Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The coolant consumption is high (it needs topping up frequently)

Results of the tests

1

VISUAL INSPECTION OF CCOLANT EXPANSION TANK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Start up the engine and visually check that there are no air bubbles in the expansion tank

Continue to Step 2

Air in the cooling circuit

Eliminate the air from the cooling circuit

2

CHECK FUEL FILLER CAP

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the expansion tank, and the cap seal in particular

Continue to Step 3

Cap faulty

Repair or replace the cap Op. 1088A10

3

CHECK THE SYSTEM FOR LEAKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out system leak test Op. 0510C30 cooling system - tightness test

Continue to Step 4

Detect and locate leaks

Restore the correct tightening, repair or replace the components involved (see Assembly 1088D)

4

CYLINDER HEAD CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that the cylinder head tightening is correct
  • the gasket seal

Continue to Step 5

Cylinder head tightening not correct

Restore correct tightening

Gasket damaged

Replace the gasket Op. 1016C10 cylinder head - r + r and replace gasket

Cylinder head damaged

Replace the cylinder head Op. 1016C20

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
