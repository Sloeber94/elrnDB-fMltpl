Move aside the carpeting and parts necessary for removal of the Air Bag system cable loom.

1. Disconnect the electrical connections and retainers of the Air Bag system wiring loom.

2. Remove the Air Bag system wiring loom.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the Air Bag system wiring loom is not damaged and that the indivdual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with the anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Refit the Air Bag system wiring loom.

Connect the electrical connections and the retainers for the Air Bag system cable loom.

Refit and secure the carperting and the parts moved or removed to remove the Air Bag system cable loom.
