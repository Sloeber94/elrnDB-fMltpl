Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; production: da 09/03, da 06/04; only with equipment: ESP

Description

The gauges and warning lights which form the instrument panel are grouped in a single instrument containing:

  • electronic speedometer
  • electronic rev counter
  • fuel gauge, with reserve warning light
  • warning lights
  • multifunction display (milometer/trip meter - outside temperature gauge
  • oil level gauge
  • digital clock
  • Trip computer)

For more details 5560 INSTRUMENT PANEL

The operation of the warning lights which are not included in other functions is illustrated in depth:

  • speedometer
  • revs counter
  • fuel gauge, with reserve warning light
  • low brake fluid level/handbrake warning light;
  • mnimum engine oil pressure warning light;
  • brake pad wear warning light
  • doors open warning light
  • water in diesel filter warning light
  • inertia switch operation signal

The others are described and illustrated in the diagrams dealing with the components to which they refer:

  • direction indicatorsE2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS / HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS - Description
  • side lightsE2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS
  • main beam headlampsE2012 MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS
  • CODE failureE7010 CODE
  • rechargingE5010 STARTING AND RECHARING
  • injection system failureE5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT - Description
  • engine overheatingE5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT - Description
  • heater plugsE5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT - Description
  • Air Bag failureE7030 AIRBAG
  • passenger Air Bag disabledE7030 AIRBAG
  • ABS, EBD, ESP failure warning lightE7023 ESP

The instrument has direct supply lines and supply lines controlled by the ignition protected by two fuses located in the junction unit.
