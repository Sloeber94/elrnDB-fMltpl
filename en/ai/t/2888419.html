Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fan works properly and sends air into the interior compartment in accordance with the set flows, but this air does not ensure the required heating.

Results of the tests

1

HEATER PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the efficiency and the seal of the supply and return pipes from the heater; with the engine hot, the pipes should be hot

Continue to Step 2

Pipes not connected

Restore the correct connection

Pipes dirty or obstructed

Clean the pipes or replace them (see assembly 5010A)

2

CHECK MIXER CONTROL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the mixer control cable is correctly fitted.

Check that the mixer flap moves freely.

Continue to Step 3

Connection defective

Adjust correction or change the cable Op. 5040D40

Flap blocked or defective

Restore the flap to working order or replace Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

3

HEATER RADIATOR CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the seal of the heater radiator

Continue to Step 4

Pipes not connected

Restore the heater radiator pipe connections

Radiator dirty or obstructed

Clean the radiator or replace it Op. 5040C50 air conditioner heater radiator - r+r

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
