Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: BIPOWER

Functional description

The supply controlled by the ignition (INT) for the instrument E50 reaches pin 2 of connector A from the line protected by fuse F1 of the junction unit B1 .

Pins 3 and 4 of connector A are earthed.

The instrument E50 is also supplied directly by the battery - pin 1 of connector A - via the line for the maxifuse J/B1of B5 protected by fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 (supply for internal clock).

Pins 4 and 5 of connector B receive signals coming from the fuel gauge, located in the electric pump unit N40 : these signals, processed by the internal electronic device, operate the fuel gauge and the reserve warning light.

The methane pressure sensor K54 , located on the adjustment solenoid valve, receives a reference earth and supply from the engine management control unit M10 - pins 30 and 33 - and sends a signal proportional to the pressure of the methane to the control unit M10 - pin 46 and to the instrument E50 pin 11 of connector B.

This signal, processed by the electronic device inside the instrument, operates the (green) indication LEDs and the (amber) reserve one.

Insufficient engine oil pressure is signalled to pin 2 of connector C from the earth signal coming from the sensor K30 .

The signal from the sensor K20 . reaches pin 5 of connector C and lights up the brake pad wear warning light.

Signals from handbrake switch I40 and brake fluid level sensor K25 . reach pin 8 of connector B and pin 13 of connector C for E50 , respectively and light up the insufficient brake fluid level/handbrake warning light. The same warning light is on - together with the ABS failure warning light - if the ABS control unit detects problems involving the EBD function ( E7020 ABS ).

The speedometer sensor K84 , located on the gearbox, sends signals to the instrument panel E50 to pins 8 and 9 of connector C: these signals, processed by the internal electronic device, operate the vehicle speed indicator, the milometer and the output is available: from pin 10 of connector C for the engine management control unit ( E5030 PETROL ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT ), from pin 3 of connector B for the navigation system control unit ( E4090 NAVIGATOR ).
