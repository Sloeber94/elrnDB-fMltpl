Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 06/04

Description

The electric windows in the rear doors are operated by means of switches located in each of the rear doors.

Operation of the rear electrically-operated windows is manual (the window is raised/lowered only when the switch is pressed).

For safety reasons a special lock switch in the dashboard allows the power supply to the rear switches to be excluded to prevent accidental operation, for example, by children.

The rear electrically-operated windows will work only when the key is turned to MAR.

The control switch symbols are lit when the key is turned to MAR.

The circuit for the electric rear windows is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a special relay. Both are located next to the junction unit.
