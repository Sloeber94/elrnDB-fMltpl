Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The engagement of the gears is sometimes difficult; there is also a noise in the area of the gearbox ("the gears grate").

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the clutch releases properly: with the engine running, press the clutch pedal and engage a gear

The defect does not occur again:

Continue to Step 1

The gearbox always grates in any gear: the clutch does not disengage

Continue according to the instructions for the

See table E01 CLUTCH NOT RELEASING CORRECTLY

CHECK GEAR SELECTION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the gear selector ball springs (see Assembly 2110B)

Continue to Step 2

Springs or balls damaged

Replace the components involved

2

CHECK SYNCHRONIZER RINGS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Service the gearbox (see Assembly 2110B).

In particular, check the synchronizer rings: they should not show signs of wear on the internal surfaces.

Continue to Step 3

Seals worn

Replace the synchronizers

3

CHECK GEARS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Service the gearbox (see Assembly 2110B).

In particular, check the gear front teeth: they should not show signs of notches or excessive wear.

Continue to Step 4

Gears worn

Replace the gears involved

4

CHECK HUBS AND SLIDING SLEEVES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Service the gearbox (see Assembly 2110B).

In particular, check the hubs and the sliding sleeves:

  • they should not show signs of notches, wear or excessive clearance
  • they should slide freely

Continue to Step 5

Sleeves or hubs worn

Replace the sleeves or hubs involved
