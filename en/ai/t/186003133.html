Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

Operational description

The horn system receives a power supply via the horn relay R2

; located in the junction unit B1

from the direct battery line via fuse F13

of B1

and maxifuse J/B1 of B5

.

The coil for relay R2 is energized with an earth signal when one of the switches on the steering wheel is closed; connected via the clock spring D47

.

Relay R2

supplies the high tone horn P10

and the low tone horn P11

.
