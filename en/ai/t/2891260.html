Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet lid.

With the ignition key in the OFF position, disconnect the battery negative terminal.

1048A32 sleeve from air cleaner to intake manifold - r + r

1. Undo the bolt fixing the accelerator cable mounting bracket.

2. Raise the accelerator pedal bracket and move the underlying pipe away from the throttle body.

3. Disconnect the accelerator cable from the throttle body.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Removing

If necessary, remove the actuators on the throttle body.

Position the throttle body and connect the electrical connections.

Tighten the bolts.

Position the coolant pipe in the seat on the throttle body.

Connect the accelerator cable.

Tighten the accelerator cable bracket bolts

Adjust the accelerator cable with reference to op.1068A10

1048A32 sleeve from air cleaner to intake manifold - r + r

Connect the battery negative terminal.

Close the bonnet lid.
