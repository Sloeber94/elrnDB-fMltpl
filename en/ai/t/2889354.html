Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Heater

Description

The engine radiator cooling system consists of a fan activated at one speed only corresponding to a high engine coolant temperature.

The operation of the fan is achieved by means of a special relay located in the fuse and relay box located in the engine compartment.

The fan supply line is protected by a special maxifuse located next to this box, whilst the circuit for energizing the relay is protected by the fuse which protects numerous other services controlled by the ignition.

The fan therefore only comes on when the ignition is switched ON.
