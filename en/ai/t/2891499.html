Removing

Refitting

Place two wooden blocks at the base of the tank to prevent it accidentally turning. If it rolls over,: the oil in the pump could come out and impair pump operation.

Before servicing, ensure the tank has been sent to the appropriate centres for emptying and rendering inert, after agreement with T.D.A. (Italy)/Teseo (abroad).

Before carrying out any operations to the 80% valve, discharge any air remaining inside the tank following the procedure described 1043A08 lpg tank check seal .

If a strong smell of LPG should be smelt during this operation, suspend the procedure immediately and send the tank back to the company responsible for drainage. A slight smell of LPG is, however, considered normal.

1043B28 lpg delivery and return pipes from tank to intermediate connector complete with submerged pump wiring r r with lpg tank removed .

1. Graduallly undo the bolts (1a) fixing the electric pump to the tank, following a crossover sequence, in order to release any remaining air pressure. Extract the electric pump (1b) upwards; to facilitate this operation, tighten the bolt (1c) fixing the valve group cover to the housing on the pump.

During this extraction operation, do not turn the pump upsidedown because the oil inside could come out and damage the actual pump.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER and Euro 3

Removing
