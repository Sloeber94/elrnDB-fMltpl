Before removing and refitting the pretensioners, observe all the specified safety regulations ( 7065 SEAT BELTS AND ACCESSORIES ).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

7040H34 central pillar trim - r r

1. Remove the clip (1a) and withdraw the through stud (1b).

2. Undo the bolt.

3. Remove the seat belt with pretensioner (3a).

When the centring pin (3b) is withdrawn from its seating, the pretensioner is automatically disarmed. Do not tamper with the centring pin.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Place the pretensioner in the special cabinet, resting it on a free surface.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Handle the pretensioner with extreme care, without tampering with the centring pin.

1. Refit the pretensioner (1a) checking that the centring pin is positioned correctly (1b).

When the centring pin is positioned correctly in its seating, the pretensioner is automatically armed.

2. Tighten the bolt to the specified torque.

Do not use impact tools for tightening.
