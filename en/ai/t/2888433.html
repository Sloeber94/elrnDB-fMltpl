When the control is operated, the radio does not come on.

Results of the tests

1

RADIO CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the protective fuse behind the actual radio; see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5570T car radio and car telephone

Continue to Step 2

Fuse faulty

Replace the fuse (then search for the cause of the problem)

2

RADIO ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • radio protective fuses (service)
  • radio supply
  • radio earth

See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 3

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

3

RADIO CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the actual radio: see DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION: 5570T car radio and car telephone

Continue to Step 4

Radio faulty

Renew or replace the radio Op. 5570T80 car radio unit - r+r

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
