Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the clutch pedal is pressed, the resistance is excessive,

Since this symptom is "subjective", make sure that there really is an effective hardening compared with the previous condition which was considered "normal"

Results of the tests

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the clutch pedal travel is smooth (no binding)

Continue to Step 2

Pedal travel not correct

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal Op. 1820A14 clutch pedal - adjust travel

2

CHECK CLUTCH CABLE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the clutch cable

Continue to Step 3

Cable defective or incorrectly fastened

Restore the cable fastenings or replace Op. 1820A20 release cable - r + r

3

CHECK PEDAL UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • condition of pedal unit joint bushes
  • pedal correctly fastened

Continue to Step 4

Bushes defective or hardened

Repair the pedal unit Op. 3330B14 pedal unit - overhaul

Fastenings not correct

Restore fastenings Op. 3330B14 pedal unit - overhaul

4

CHECK SPLINED HUB

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check for insufficient grease on the clutch disc hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft

Continue to Step 5

Gearbox main shaft hub faulty

Replace the gearbox main shaft (see Assembly 2110B)

Clutch plate hub bushes faulty

Restore the correct lubrication for the bushes

If necessary, replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

5

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • that the clutch plate is not distorted
  • that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring

Continue to Step 6

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plate Op. 1810A20 clutch - r + r

6

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
