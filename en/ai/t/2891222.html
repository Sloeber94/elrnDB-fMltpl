Removing

Refitting

1. Use tool (1a) to prevent the pulley turning, then unscrew retaining bolt (1b) and remove timing pulley (1c).

2. The exhaust camshaft pulley removal procedure is as described in the previous step.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the newly-removed pulley on the relevant camshaft.

Screw in the pulley retaining bolt without tightening.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Use the tool to prevent the pulley from turning.

2. Unscrew retaining bolt (2a) and remove pulley (2b) from its seat on the camshaft.
