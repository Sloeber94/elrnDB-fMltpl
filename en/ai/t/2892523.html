Functional description

The headlamp washer J50is supplied (pin P) by the direct line from the battery through the headlamp washer fuseB12and the maxifuseJ/B2ofB5

If the windscreen washer switch is operated, the steering column switch unit H5sends a signal which reaches the headlamp washer timerJ50(pin S): it involves a line controlled by the ignition (INT/A) coming from the steering column switch unitH5- pin 3 of connector D - supplied by the relayR1, protected by the fuseF14: both are housed in the junction unitB1.

The supply for the relay R1comes from the direct line of the maxifuseJ/B1ofB5, whilst the coil is supplied by the line (INT/A) protected by the fuseB26( See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE ).

With the dipped headlamps switched on, a go ahead signal reaches pin 56 of J50from the line for fuseF5which supplies the dipped headlamps. ( E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS ).

When there are both signals at pins S and 56, the relay, inside the device J50which supplies the electric headlamp washer pumpN25with impulses of about half a second, is energized for a certain time.
