7055A54 NUMBERPLATE HOLDER - R+R

Open the left front door.

1. Disconnect the retainers (1a) from the bridge (1b) on the number plate holder.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the switch.

3. Remove the switch and bridge.

Removing

Tighten the bolts.
