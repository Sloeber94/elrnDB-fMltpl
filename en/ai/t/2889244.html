Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 06/04

Description

The vehicle is also available, on request, prepared for the installaion of all the electrical equipment required for using the vehicle as a TAXI.

In particular, this preparation includes:

  • supply connection for taxi-meter with all the signals required for this device (direct and ignition-controlled supply, speedometer signal, lighting with side lights on); the connection is located by the coin holder trim in the centre of the dashboard;
  • connection for 'TAXI' sign on the roof, lit up when the side lights are on;
  • supply connection for radio taxi device, located under the outside front passenger seat.

The above supply lines are protected by two specific fuses, one next to the junction unit, the other in the engine compartment next to the maxifuse box.
