Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: SX setup

Functional description

The two-way switch controlling mirrors H30operates electrical motors located in the right and left electrically-operated door mirrorsP60andP61respectively.

The switch is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT/A) by the line for fuse B27at pins 7 and 8 forH30, whilst pin 5 is earthed.

Operating the switch in one direction or the other, sends one of the two motors a positive and an earth signal, thereby determining the direction of rotation. The motors which adjust the vertical position are connected to pin 5 of the mirrors P60andP61, the motors which adjust the horizontal positino are connected to pin 3. According to the selector position, either the left motors (output signals from pins 1 and 3 of connector B for control H30) or the right motors (output signals from pins 2 and 4 of connector B forH30) are connected; the common supply comes out of pin 6 ofH30and is connected to pin 4 of motorsP60andP61.

The switch H30is lit up when the ignition is switched on (INT/A).
