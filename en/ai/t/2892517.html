Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The system for the horns is supplied through the horn relay R2, located in the junction unitB1from the direct line from the battery through the fuse F13 - always forB1- and the maxifuseJ/B1forB5.

The coil for the relay R2 is energized with an earth signal when one of the switches on the steering wheel is closed; on versions with Air Bags, they are connected to the steering column switch unit H5by the clock spring.

The relay R2thereby supplies the high tone hornsP10and the lone tone hornsP11.
