Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER

The operating check is carried out on two positions of the LPG level potentiometer; check that it corresponds to the specified values.

Name

Connector

-

GPL level sensor

K33

With the float at the minimum LPG level, the value measured between pins 1 and 2 must be 48200 Ohm ± 20%.

With the float at the maximum LPG level, the value measured between pins 1 and 2 must be 48700 Ohm ± 20%.
