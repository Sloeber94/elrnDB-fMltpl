Refitting

1. Remove the clamp (1a) from the pipe (1b) and let the power unit coolant flow into a suitable container.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03

Raise the car.

1. Loosen the band (1a) and disconnect the pipe (1b) from the climate control casing.

2. Loosen the bands (2a) and disconnect the pipe (2b).

3. Disconnect the bands (3a), then remove the fluid outlet pipe (3b) from the heater.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03

Refitting

Removing

Check that the heater fluid outlet pipe is not damaged.

Place the heater fluid outlet pipe in position and connect the bands.

Connect the pipe using the band.

Connect the pipe to the climate control casing and secure it using the band.
