Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the car in motion, a smell of exhaust gas is noted coming from the rear area.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK AIR VENTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear air outlet fittings

In particular, ascertain:

  • fin orientation (they must open outwards)
  • correct assembly

Continue to Step 2

Faulty assembly

Reposition the vents correctly Op. 5010C60 lh or rh rear air outlet -one- r r

2

CHECK TAIL-GATE SEALING STRIP

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tail-gate sealing strip In particular, check the sealing strip for wrinkles

Continue to Step 3

Faulty fitting of seal

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7015A30 tailgate compartment seal - r+r

3

CHECK TAIL-GATE ALIGNMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check tail-gate is correctly fitted. In particular, check that the compartment/tail-gate are correctly aligned and the seal is in contact around the entire perimeter.

Continue to Step 4

Tail-gate incorrectly fitted

Refit the tail-gate correctly Op. 7015A14 tailgate - adjust centring and profile - includes hinge and compartment seal adaptation

4

UNDERBODY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the underbody plugs are fitted at the rear of the car

Continue to Step 5

Underbody plugs missing

Refit the missing plugs

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

End of diagnosis
