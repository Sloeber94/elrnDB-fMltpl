Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The bodyshell marking is stamped on the right side of the floor panel, in the passenger compartment and includes:

  • Vehicle type: ZFA 186.000;
  • Vehicle manufacture number (chassis number).

It is accessed by lifting the flap on the right of the right front seat.
