Description

The vehicle is also available, on request, prepared for the installation of a cellular telephone.

This preparation consists of:

  • supply connection for recharging the mobile phone; located near the radio and also acts as a mount for the phone
  • GSM aerial (fitted at the front of the roof: it has two functions (GSM/radio)E3510 RADIO;
  • connection with the radio system which uses the speakers for the hands-free function.

The mobile phone connection has two power supplies; one direct and one ignition-operated; another power supply works with the side lights on and serves to light the phone - where fitted.

The above two lines are protected by two fuses in the junction unit.

- The preparation does not include a microphone which is supplied together with the various telephone installation kits.
