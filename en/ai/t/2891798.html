Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03

Removing

Refitting

Place the vehicle on a lift.

Raise the car.

1. Disconnect the band.

2. Undo the bolt.

3. Remove the additional heater drainage pipe.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03

Refitting

Removing

Check that the drainage pipe is not damaged.

Place the drainage pipe in position and fasten it using the bolt and the band.

Lower the vehicle and remove it from the lift.
