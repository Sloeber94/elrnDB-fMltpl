Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the button in the control panel in the centre of the dashboard is pressed, the external rear view mirrors do not warm up evenly.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK DOOR MIRROR SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • specific fuse for rear view mirrors
  • connection to earth of door mirrors
  • supply line to door mirrors

( See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING).

Continue to Step 2

Connection broken

Restore the connections

2

CHECK MIRROR FILAMENTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the filaments in the mirror are intact

Continue to Step 3

Filaments broken

Repair the heated rear window filaments If necessary, replace the mirror's reflective element Op. 7005R30 electrically controlled door mirror (one) - r+r

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
