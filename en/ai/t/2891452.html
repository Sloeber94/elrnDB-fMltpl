Refitting

4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

7055B42 Additional FRONT WHEEL ARCH (one), left or right - R.R.

1. Using the belt tensioner, slacken the tension of the single drive belt.

2. Remove the belt from the pulleys.

Do not allow oil or solvents to come into contact with the belt, as these could affect the flexibility of the rubber, resulting in reduced grip. Also check that there are no cracks or cuts on the belt, otherwise it will have to be replaced.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Adjust the belt tensioner to fit the single drive belt.

The tensioning device is automatic: a preloaded spring adjusts the belt to the correct tension upon installation and automatically takes up any slack in the belt.
