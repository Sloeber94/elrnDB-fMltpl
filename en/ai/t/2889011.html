Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 04/02 a 08/03, da 06/04; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Description

The rear window heater element, door mirror demister coils and the windscreen heater element absorb a considerable amount of energy. They must therefore be turned off once they have been working for a certain period of time.

The rear window and door mirror heater coils are activated for a timed period by a button in the switch unit in the middle of the facia; the button is faintly lit when the ignition is on and more brightly lit when the heated rear window is on.

The defrosting circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a specific relay located in the junction box.
