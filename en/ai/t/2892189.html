Refitting

5580E22 Anti-theft device VOLUMETRIC SENSORS - R.R. after checking electrical circuit .

1. Remove the covers.

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Gently move aside the courtesy light unit and disconnect the electrical connections (3a, 3b).
