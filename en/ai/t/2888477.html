Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the car moving, there is a smell of fuel, especially after refuelling, coming from the rear.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

First check for leaks from or damage to the fuel tank.

Continue to Step 1

Leaks from the fuel tank

Repair or replace the fuel tank Op. 1040A14 fuel tank - r + r for replacement

1

CHECK AIR VENTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the rear air outlet fittings

In particular, ascertain:

  • fin orientation (they must open outwards)
  • correct assembly

Continue to Step 2

Faulty assembly

Reposition the vents correctly Op. 5010C60 lh or rh rear air outlet -one- r r

2

CHECK FUEL TANK FILLER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the tank filler fitting, in particular:

  • seal of filler housing on wing
  • condition of filler
  • cap seal

Continue to Step 3

Faulty filler cap

Repair filler Op. 1040A30 fuel filler pipe - r + r

Faulty filler cap

Repair cap Op. 1040A40 PLUG FOR FUEL FILLER PIPE - R + R

3

CHECK PUMP COVER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the pump cover/fuel gauge sender unit seal for leaks

Continue to Step 4

Seal worn, cover damaged

Replace the seal or cover Op. 1040A70 submerged pump assembly complete with level guage control - r + r

4

VAPOUR RECOVERY SYSTEM CHECK

Leaks from vapour recovery system
