7040L42 sill plate connection of one front door (1), lh or rh - r r

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the door seal near the kick plate connector.

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Lift the kick plate (3a) slightly and remove the kick plate connector (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the kick plate connector is undamaged.

Lift the kick plate slightly and fit the kick plate connector.

Tighten the bolts.

Fit the door seal near the kick plate connector.
