Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Air Conditioner

Functional description

The supply for the fan N11arrives directly from the battery from the line for the maxifuseB8: the fan therefore operates when it receives an earth command.

The relays J30andJ31have coils supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (15/54) protected by fuseB25.

They are energized by an earth signal coming from the engine control unit M10.

If the engine coolant reaches a first temperature level, or the 3 stage pressure switch K5(pin 1) signals to the control unitM10(pin 35 of conenctor A) that a first pressure level has been reached, then the control unitM10sends (from pin 40 of connector A) an earth signal to the fan 1st speed relayJ30, which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth signal which, via the additional resistance O10, operates the fanN11which rotates at the first (slow) speed.

If the engine coolant reaches a higher temperature, the control unit M10sends (from pin 14 of connector A) an earth signal to the fan 2nd speed relayJ31which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth signal directly to the fan N11which rotates at the maximum speed.
