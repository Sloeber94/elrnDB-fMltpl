Removing

Refitting

Place the vehicle on a lift.

Disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal.

1. Disconnect the condensed oil return pipe from the sump.

Disconnect the electrical connection for the rpm and timing sensor.

2. Undo bolts (2a) and remove rpm and timing sensor (2b).

Refitting

Removing

Fit the rpm and timing sensor and tighten the retaining bolt.

Connect the electrical connection.

Connect the condensed oil return pipe to the sump.

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.
