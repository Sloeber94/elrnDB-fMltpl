Refitting
  • Op. 5580C18 AIR BAG MODULE - R+R

Before working on the Air Bag system, follow the safety instructions for working on the system very carefullyOp. 5580C AIR BAG SYSTEM

  • Undo the bolts (1a) securing the module (1b).
  • Disconnect the electrical connection for the module.
  • Remove the module and bridge (3a) releasing it from the band (3b).
