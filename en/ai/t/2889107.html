Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99; only with equipment: ELX setup

Description

The electric windows in the rear doors are operated by means of switches located in the panel and switches in each of the rear doors.

Operation of the rear electrically-operated windows is manual (the window is raised/lowered only when the switch is pressed).

For safety reasons, a special lock switch in the panel next to the switches cuts off the power supply to the rear switches to prevent accidental operation, for example, by children, making it possible to use the switches in the panel only.

The rear electrically-operated windows will work only when the key is turned to MAR.

The control switch symbols are lit when the key is turned to MAR.

The circuit for the electric rear windows is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a special relay. Both are next to the junction unit.
