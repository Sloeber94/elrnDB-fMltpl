Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the seat mounting.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

For seats without electrical lifting device, proceed as follows.

1. Undo the nuts.

2. Disengage the guide (2a) from the seat slide lever (2b) and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

For seats with electrical lifting device, proceed as follows.

1. Undo the nuts.

2. Remove the split pin.

3. Disengage the guide (3a) from the seat slide lever (3b) and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the support and guide are not damaged.

Refit the guide (1a) and engage it with the seat slide lever (1b). If removed, fit the split pin.

Tighten the nuts.

Fit the seat mounting.

Tighten the bolts.
