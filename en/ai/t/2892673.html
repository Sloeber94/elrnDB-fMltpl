Functional description

The diagnostic socket R10 is supplied directly by the battery at pin 16 by the line protected by fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 . Pins 4 and 5 are earthed.

Pin 1 is connected to the ABS control unit M50 (pin 11) ( E7020 ABS ).

Pin 3 is connected to the Air Bag control unit M60 (pin 9) ( E7030 AIRBAG ).

Pin 11 is connected to alarm control unit M30 (pin 6 of connector A) and remote control receiver M35 (pin 2) ( E7015 ALARM ).

Pin 8 is connected to the Code control unit M20 (pin 5) ( E7010 CODE ).

Pin 7 is connected to engine management control unit M10 via a dedicated line.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Euro 3
