Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

There is a whistling (extremely high intensity noise) coming from the area of the engine which varies with the engine speed

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the auxiliary shaft drive belt and check that the tension is correct

Check that, as the electrical loads engaged increase, the whistling does not increase, but remains constant

Continue to Step 1

Water pump and alternator belt tension incorrect

Adjust belt tension Op. 1092B50 alternator/air conditioner compressor drive belt - adjust tension

Power steering pump belt incorrect

Adjust belt tension Op. 1092C12 power steering pump drive belt - adjust tension

Compressor belt tension incorrect

Adjust belt tension Op. 1092D14

The whistling increases: alternator faulty (voltage regulator not working properly)

Replace the voltage regulator Op. 5530A22 alternator voltage regulator - r+r

The whistling increases: alternator faulty (connections between alternator and battery not correct)

Restore the connections ( See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

1

CHECK AUXILIARY SHAFT BELT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Disconnect the belt to see if the whistling stops. ATTENTION do not run the engine too long with the belt disconnected

The whistling disappears:

Continue to Step 2

The whistling remains constant

Restore the water pump and alternator belt control system and the mobile belt tensioner in particular Op. 1092B82 auxilary belt mobile tensioner (one) -r+r

2

CHECK ON COMPONENTS DRIVEN BY THE BELT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of all the components driven by the belt:

  • water pump
  • power assisted steering pump
  • air conditioning compressor

Continue to Step 3

Water pump faulty

Restore or replace the water pump Op. 1088C12 water pump - r+r for versions with air conditioner

Power assisted steering pump faulty

Restore or replace the power assisted steering pump Op. 4130B26

Air conditioning compressor faulty

Restore or replace the compressor Op. 5040A20 air conditioner compressor - r+r - includes fluid level check and top up if necessary
