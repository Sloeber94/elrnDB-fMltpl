Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The climate control is working properly, but there is excessive noise coming from the distributor duct assembly

Results of the tests

1

ELECTRIC FAN CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the climate control fan and ensure the following in particular:

  • there are no foreign bodies inside the fan casing
  • there are no vibrations at the various speeds (causing operation with the fan switched off, but the electrical connection engaged)

Continue to Step 2

Fan dirty

Clean the fan casing and all the ducts

Electric motor noisy, fan unbalanced

Replace the electric fan Op. 5040C30 fan motor - r+r

2

CHECKING THE AIR DUCTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the air ducts inside the dashboard and the vents are intact and that there are no foreign bodies which could cause noise

Continue to Step 3

Ducts obstructed or damaged

Restore the operation of the ducts and the vents (see Assembly 5010B)

3

CHECK AIR CONDITIONER UNIT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the air conditioner unit and check in particular:

  • the operation of the climate control fan
  • distributing and mixing controls

Check whether there are foreign bodies or damaged parts inside the duct distributor assembly which could cause noise

Continue to Step 4

Defective bowden cable operation

Restore or replace the bowden cable (see Assembly 5040D)

Defective recirculation motor operation

Restore or replace the recirculation motor Op. 5040D68

Defective operation of one flap

Repair or replace the flap Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

Presence of foreign bodies or damaged parts inside the duct distributor assembly

Overhaul the duct distributor assembly Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
