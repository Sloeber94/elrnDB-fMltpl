Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Description

The vehicle is fitted with an autonomous additional heater in case the passenger compartment needs to be heated more quickly, with the engine running, if the climatic conditions are particularly harsh.

The system basically consists of:

  • a heater unit fixed to the front suspension crossmember and the underbody floor panel
  • an electric fuel pump fixed to the underbody near the fuel tank
  • an outside air temperature sensor positioned under the left front light cluster
  • an exhaust pipe connected directly to the heater unit

An electronic control unit integrated in the unit controls the switching on, the maintenance and the switching off of the heater.

The ignition of the mixture is guaranteed by a spark plug, the combustion is ensured by the air coming from a special fan and the fuel coming from an electric metering pump.

The heat produced in the combustion chamber is surrendered to the engine coolant which comes into contact with it. The exhaust gases are expelled through a special exhaust pipe.

For more details, 5020M supplementary independent heater

The additional heater circuit is protected by a specific fuse, located in the engine compartment in the relay and fuse box.

The system only works with the engine running.
