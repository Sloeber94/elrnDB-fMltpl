The recirculation function is not working properly

Results of the tests

RECIRCULATION MOTOR ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • air recirculation motor
  • air recirculation control
  • connection between air recirculation motor and control See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Motor defective

Replace motor Op. 5040D52

Air recirculation control defective

Change control unit Op. 7065C10 air conditioning control panel - r+r

2

RECIRCULATION FLAP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Check that the recirculation flap moves freely

Continue to Step 3

Flap blocked or defective

Restore the flap to working order or replace Op. 5040C24 air conditioner box - dismantle and rebuild at bench
