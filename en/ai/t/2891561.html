2710A20 wheel end constant velocity joint (one), l/h or r/h - r + r with drive shaft removed - includes boot replacement

Removing

Refitting

Place the drive shaft in a vice.

1. Remove the clip and push back the gaiter.

2. Remove the constant velocity joint from the drive shaft.

3. Clean the grease off the inside of the bearing housing, and, using tools (3a) and (3b) while at the same time using pliers on the circlip inside the joint, remove the ring (3c) fixing the constant velocity joint bearing.

4. Then remove the protective gaiter (4a), removing its retaining clip (4b).

Refitting

Removing

Check that the drive shaft is not deformed or out of true in the area where the joint fits, and that the area in contact with the seal is not worn.

Fit the new protective gaiter on the drive shaft.

Fit the bearing on the drive shaft.

Fill the protective gaiter and bearing with TUTELA MRM2 grease.

Position the constant velocity joint and fit it on the bearing with slight pressure.

Secure the protective gaiter to the joint, using two new clips.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods
