Operational description

The headlight alignment device H15

receives a supply at pin 2 - with the dipped headlights on - from a line coming from the steering column switch unit H5

: the supply is ignition operated (INT/A) for the line protected by fuse F5

from the junction unit B1

via a special relay J45

Vedere E2011 LUCI ANABBAGLIANTI

.

Pin 4 of the device H15

is earthed, whilst a signal leaves pin 1 and reaches pin 7 of the headlights F10 (left) and F11 (right,

produced by means of a resistive distributor which varies the output tension depending on which of the four positions is selected.

The motors located in the headlights F10 (left) and F11 (right) are supplied at pins 3, pins 6 of F10 and F11

by the same line for the dipped headlights circuit, line protected by fuse F5

from the junction unit B1

Vedere E2011 LUCI ANABBAGLIANTI

.

The device only works with the dipped beams on.
