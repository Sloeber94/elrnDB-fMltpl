Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: Heater

Functional description

The relay J35controls the heater fan circuit

The supply for the relay contact arrives from the battery through the line for the maxifuse J/B1 of B5 protected by fuse F12 for the junction unit B1 , whilst the energizing of the coil is controlled by the ignition (INT/A) through the line protected by fuse B27 .

The relay J35supplies the control knobH81: this controls the fan at the different speeds via the regulatorO30.

The voltage signal varies according to the position of the knob and through the resistance of the regulator O30determining four different voltage signals for the fanN85which sends outside or recirculation air to he duct/distributor unit at 4 different speeds.

The control panel has ideograms which are lit up, when the side lights are on, by lights G45, supplied by the line protected by fuseF3for the junction unitB1( See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS ).
