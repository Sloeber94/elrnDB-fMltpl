7040A09 passenger air bag compartment cover - r r

Path

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Lift the cover (1a) slightly to remove the studs (1b) from the retainers.

2. Remove the embellishments, in the direction of the arrows.

3. Undo the bolts.

4. Remove the cover complete with belt.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the cover and belt are undamaged.

Fit the belt and secure it with its bolts.

Fit the embellishments.

Fit the cover, engaging the studs on the retainers.

Check that the cover matches the dashboard correctly.
