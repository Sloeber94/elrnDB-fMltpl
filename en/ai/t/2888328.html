The engagement of the gears is difficult in all conditions.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK FIXING OF GEARBOXCONTROL CABLES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the fixings of the gearbox control cables: they should not be slack or incorrectly fixed

Continue to Step 2

Lever support not fitted correctly

Restore the correct fixing of the control cables Op. 2125C30 gear engagement cable - r r

Lever support not fitted correctly

Restore the correct fixing for the lever support Op. 2125C16 gear hand-lever - r r

2

CHECK GEARBOX CONTROL CABLES

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the gearbox control cables: the ball joints and the bushes should not be worn

Continue to Step 3

Ball joints worn

Replace the control cables Op. 2125C30 gear engagement cable - r r

3

CHECK GEAR LEVER BALL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the gear lever ball joint: it should not be worn or too "closed"

Continue to Step 4

Ball worn

Replace the gear lever Op. 2125C28 gear control lever - r r.

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK
