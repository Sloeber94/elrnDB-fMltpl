Removing

Refitting

Place two wooden blocks at the base of the tank to prevent it from being accidentally overturned. If it is overturned, the oil in the pump could come out and cause damage to the surrounding components.

Before carrying out any operations, make sure that the tank is sent to the special service centres for draining and incapacitating.

Before carrying out any operations to the 80% valve, discharge any air remaining inside the tank following the procedure described 1043A08 lpg tank check seal .

During this last operation if there is a strong smell of LPG, stop the procedure immediately and send the tank to the organization responsible for draining it.

1. Working with a special spanner, gradually undo the 80% valve to release any residual pressurized air, then remove the 80% valve.

Thoroughly clean any teflon residues from the housing on the tank for the 80% valve to ensure a perfect seal for the valve.

Do not use compressed air to clean the housing for the 80% valve on the tank. Any teflon residues which have not been removed could fall inside the tank coming into contact with the pump and adversely affecting its operation.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER and Euro 3

Refitting

Removing

1. Wrap teflon bands several times around the threaded part of the 80% valve, working in the opposite direction to the tightening direction of the actual valve on the tank.

Leave the first threads uncovered to allow the 80% valve to fit in the housing on the tank.

2. Fully tighten the 80% valve taking care to position it with the word TOP on the outer hexagon of the valve at the top.

Tighten the 80% valve with the tank at a slight angle to avoid contact with the inner partitions.
