Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER

The operating test consists of a test on the total absorption by the following loads:

LPG pump.

Solenoid positioned on LPG cylinder.

Solenoid positioned on pressure regulator.

Proceed as follows:

Measure the battery voltage with all the loads switched off, and make sure that the no-load voltage is not less than 12.8 V; if it is, recharge the battery.

Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

Fit an ammeter lead to the battery's negative cable.

Disconnect the relay.

Name

Connector

-

Methane/LPG relay feed

J26

Bridge terminals 30 and 87 with a piece of cable containing a 7.5 A fuse.

Check that the total absorption of the supplied loads is about 5 A.
