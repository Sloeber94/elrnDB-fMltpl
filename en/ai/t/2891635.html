Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the vacuum pipe from the vacuum unit.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the vacuum unit (2b) complete with O-ring.

Recover the vacuum unit gasket from the housing with the camshaft cap.
