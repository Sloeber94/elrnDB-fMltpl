Description

The alarm system offers volume-sensing and perimeter protection against intrusion into the vehicle.

An electronic control unit controls:

  • the state of the doors and lids, via switches in the catches (perimeter control)
  • the presence of a moving object inside the passenger compartment, by means of volumetric sensors. (volumetric control)
  • the condition of the system supply leads (both direct from the battery and ignition-operated)
  • the operation of the ignition switch.

The control unit is equipped with an autodiagnostic function that detects and memorizes faults and problems with the actual control unit and the system components and checks the condition of the connecting cables.

The control unit also memorizes the number of times the alarm has been activated and set off.

The control unit operating software may be altered to meet regulations governing the use of alarm systems applicable in different markets.

The electronic control unit, which also incorporates the siren, is located at the front, under the engine compartment right wheel arch.

The receiver is an electronic device which receives the transmitter signal. It is concealed uner the dashboard.

It is connected, via a serial line, to the control unit and manages the following functions directly:

  • receiving, decoding and managing the remote control signals
  • controlling the locking and unlocking of the doors
  • memorizing the codes for the enabled transmitters:

The receiver is also equipped with its own autodiagnostic function that detects and memorizes faults and problems with the actual receiver and the components connected to it and checks the condition of the connecting cables

The door lock system switches are used to monitor the doors and boot. A special switch is located on the bonnet lock.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: da 06/04

The control unit also produces a "doors open" signal, which is activated if even only one of the doors is not properly shut and is sent to the instrument panel.

Applies to: all engines; production: da 06/04

The alarm system control unit has direct and ignition-operated supply lines protected by two junction unit fuses like the remote control receiver.

The alarm system control unit and the remote control receiver have direct and ignition-operated supply lines protected by three junction unit fuses.
