Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; production: da 09/03, da 06/99 a 03/02, da 04/02 a 08/03, da 06/04; only with equipment: GPOWER and Euro 3 and BIPOWER and BLUPOWER

Functional description

The supply for the fan N11 arrives directly from the battery from the line for the maxifuse B8 : the fan therefore operates when it receives an earth command.

The relays J30 and J31 have coils supplied by the line controlled by the ignition (15/54) protected by fuse B25 .

They are energized by an earth signal coming from the engine control unit M10 .

If the coolant temperature reaches the first temperature stage, or the 4-stage pressure switch K10 (pin 3) indicates to control unit M10 (pin 35 of connector A) that the first pressure stage has been reached, control unit M10 sends an earth signal (from pin 40 of connector A) to the fan J30 1st speed relay which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth signal which, via the additional resistance O10 , operates the fan N11 which rotates at the first (slow) speed.

If the engine coolant reaches a higher temperature level or the 4 stage pressure switch K10 (pin 1) signals to the control unit M10 (pin 21 of connector A) that a second pressure stage has been reached, then the control unit M10 sends an earth signal (from pin 14 of connector A) to the 2nd speed fan relay J31 which energizes the coil. The relay then sends an earth signal directly to the fan N11 which rotates at the maximum speed.
