Applies to: all engines; production: da 09/03, da 06/04

Description

The luggage compartment is lit by the special courtesy light on the left in the actual luggage compartment when the tailgate is opened thanks to the boot opening switch.

The coming on of this light is timed according to a logic managed by the integrated services control unit located under the dashboard: when the boot is opened, a light is turned on for a timed interval of 20 minutes: this function ensures that the battery does not go flat if the car is left with the boot open - Timer operation is suspended when the boot is closed.

The light under the (passenger) sun visor comes on when the switch is operated.

The circuit for the boot light and the sun visor light is protected by the same fuse as the courtesy lights.

Numerous other interior instrument and control lights are present. These are turned on when the side lights are turned on. The lines are described and illustrated in the relevant component wiring diagrams.
