Position the complete alternator in its housing and secure it by tightening the fixing bolts.

Position the rear Lambda sensor wiring mounting bracket and tighten the upper fixing bolt.

Check belt condition. In particular, check for tears, cracks, surface wear and dry or hard parts that could lead to loss of grip. Change the belt if one of these defects is noted.
