Removing

Refitting

1. Only for the left mounting, remove the rivets (1a) using a drill.

Recover the mechanical sensor (1b) after disengaging its arming lever (1c).

Do not touch the arming lever (1c) and do not pull / damage the pretensioner connection device (1d).

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Remove the seat mounting.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts.

2. Disengage the guide (2a) from the seat slide lever (2b) and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the support and guide are not damaged.

1. Refit the guide (1a) and engage it with the seat slide lever (1b).

2. Tighten the nuts.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Fit the seat mounting.

2. Tighten the bolts.

3. Only for the left mounting, fit the mechanical sensor (3a) and fix it with the rivets (3b).

Do not touch the arming lever and do not pull / damage the pretensioner connection device.

Check that the arming lever (3d) is positioned correctly.
