Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The passenger compartment air fan comes on, but not at the different speed settings.

Results of the tests

1

CLIMATE CONTROL FAN ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • climate control fan adjustment resistance
  • climate control fan controls
  • connection between climate control fan and its controls, via the adjustment resistor

( See E6010 HEATER).

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Electric fan defective

Replace the electric fan Op. 5020A30 heater fan motor - r+r

Fan regulator defective

Replace fan resistor Op. 5020A44 heater fan motor resistor - r+r

Fan controls defective

Replace fan controls Op. 5020B10 heater control support - r+r

2

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
