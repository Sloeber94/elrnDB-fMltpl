Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when reverse gear is engaged, the reversing light does not come on

Results of the tests

1

CHECK LIGHT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the reversing light and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 2

Bulb damaged

Change bulb

Bulb holder faulty

Renew or replace the right rear light cluster Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

2

REVERSING LIGHT SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the reversing light switch is correctly fixed and working properly

Continue to Step 3

Reversing light switch faulty

Renew or replace the reversing light switch Op. 5550D22

3

REVERSING LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • reversing light fuse (services)
  • connections between junction unit and reversing light switch
  • connection between junction unit and bulbs
  • bulb earth

( See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS).

Continue to Step 4

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
