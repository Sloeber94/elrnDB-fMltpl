Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fan which sends air into the passenger compartment does not come on

Results of the tests

1

CLIMATE CONTROL FAN ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • climate control fan fuse
  • climate control fan relay
  • climate control fan
  • climate control fan controls
  • connection between climate control fan and its controls, via the adjustment resistor See E6010 HEATER

Continue to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Controls defective

Replace control knob Op. 5020B10 heater control support - r+r

Electric fan defective

Replace the electric fan Op. 5020A30 heater fan motor - r+r

2

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
