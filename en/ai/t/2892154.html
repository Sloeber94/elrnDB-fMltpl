Refitting

Removing

Check that the wiring for the sun roof is undamaged and that the individual conductors are firmly attached to the connections.

Check that the connections are fitted with the anti-slip devices (secondary lock).

If necessary, check the continuity.

Position the sun roof wiring.

Connect the electrical connections and the retainers for the sun roof wiring.

Position the ceiling trim.

Fit the seals of the 4 door frames near the ceiling trim.

Fit the cover on the central rear seat belt.

Tighten the cover bolts.

Engage the pillar trim on the retainers.

Fit the sealing strip around the door compartment.

Lift up the front and rear seat backrests.
