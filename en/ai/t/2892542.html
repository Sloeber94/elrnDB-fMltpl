Functional description

The integrated services control unit M40 manages the operation of the circuit.

The control unit M40 is supplied directly by the battery - in 8 of connector B - from the line for maxifuse J/B2 of B5 via fuse B19 . Pin 13 of connector B provides the earth.

An earth signal is sent to the control unit M40 , at pin 17 of connector B by operating the special control H71 .

Similarly, the switch I33 is activated via the key and an earth signal is sent to the control unit M40 at pin 14 of connector B.

The latter sends a signal - pin 19 of connector A - which energizes the boot opening relay J56 which supplies the boot locking geared motor N57 via the line protected by fuse F19 from the additional fuse box B98 .

The speedometer signal from the instrument panel E50 reaches pin 14 of connector A of M40 .

A boot open signal reaches pin 17 of connector A of M40 which inhibits the unlocking.
