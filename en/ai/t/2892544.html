Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02; only with equipment: SX setup

Functional description

The electric windows relay J65controls the operation of the front electric windows circuit.

The electric windows relay is supplied by the direct line from the battery through the maxifuse J/B2forB5.

The coil for the relay is supplied when the ignition is switched on (INT) from the line for fuse F8for the junction unitB1and is always earthed: the relay is therefore always energized when the ignition is switched on (INT).

In this way, the line protected by fuses B22, for the driver's side andB23for the passenger side, supply the switchesH40(driver's front electric window switch in the front driver's door),H41(passenger front electric window switch in the passenger side front door) andH50(electric window switch in passenger side front door) wih the LEDs which light up the actual switches.

Operating the switches sends a supply and an earth to the left electric window motor N60or the right oneN61; reversing the signals according to the contact which is closed, determines the direction of rotation of the actual motor.
