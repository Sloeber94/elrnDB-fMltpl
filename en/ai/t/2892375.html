Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: GPOWER

Ensure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Disconnect the electrical connection (1a) of the solenoid (1b) and check that the resistance of the solenoid coil winding is 13 Ohm ± 20% at 20°C.
