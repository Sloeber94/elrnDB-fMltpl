Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet lid.

Disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal.

1080C70 VACUUM RESERVOIR - R.R.

1. With the assembly at the bench, disconnect the pipes (1a) between the vacuum reservoir and the solenoid valve (1b) and between the solenoid valve and the throttle casing.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the valve (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: Euro 3

Refitting

Removing

Position the valve on the vacuum reservoir and tighten the fixing bolts.

Connect the pipes between the vacuum reservoir and the solenoid valve and between the solenoid valve and the throttle casing.

1080C70 VACUUM RESERVOIR - R.R.

Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.

Close the bonnet lid.
