Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Description

A device allows the headlamp beam to be adjusted, directly from the driver's seat, in accordance with the load. The headlamps are thus always aligned quickly and correctly. The adjustment device consists of a motor mounted on each of the two headlamps which tilts them as appropriate, lowering the light beam if the car is heavily laden, and raising it if the load decreases.

A specific knob in the dashboard selects the four positions indicated:

  • position 0 - up to three persons in the front seats;
  • position 1 - six persons;
  • position 2 - six persons with luggage in the boot;
  • position 3 - driver and maximum load, all stowed in the boot.

The device is only enabled when the dipped headlamps are switched on.

The headlamp adjustment circuit is protected by the same fuses as the dipped beam headlamps circuit, located in the junction unit.
