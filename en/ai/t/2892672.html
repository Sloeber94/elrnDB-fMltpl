Applies to: all engines; production: fino a 05/99, da 06/99 a 03/02

Functional description

The diagnostic socket R10 is supplied directly by the battery at pin 16 by the line protected by fuse F10 of the junction unit B1 . Pins 4 and 5 are earthed.

Pin 1 is connected to the ABS control unit M50 (pin 11) ( See E7020 ABS ).

Pin 3 is connected to Air Bag control unit M60 - pin 9 ( See E7030 AIRBAG ).

Pin 11 is connected to alarm control unit M30 - pin 6 of connector A - and remote control receiver M35 - pin 2 - ( E7015 ALARM ).

Pin 8 is connected to Code control unit M20 - pin 5 - ( See E7010 CODE ).

Pin 7 is connected to engine management unit M10 via a dedicated line.

Pin 12 is connected to the additional heater control unit from pin 4 of D22 ( E6015 ADDITIONAL HEATER - Functional description ).
