Removing

Refitting

If you need to work on the front emblem, proceed as follows.

7055A20 FRONT TRIM under WINDSCREEN with main beam headlamps - R.R.

1. Release the retainers.

2. Heat up the emblem slightly.

3. Detach the emblem using a plastic spatula.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If you need to work on the rear lettering, proceed as follows.

1. Slightly heat the lettering to be removed.

2. Detach the lettering using a plastic spatula.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that the emblem or lettering is undamaged.

Use heptane to clean the area of the bodywork to which the lettering is to be refitted.

Remove the protection from the adhesive surface.

To refit the front emblem, position the emblem and engage the retainers.

7055A20 FRONT TRIM under WINDSCREEN with main beam headlamps - R.R.

To refit the rear lettering, position the lettering, making sure that it is horizontally aligned, and make the adhesive stick.
