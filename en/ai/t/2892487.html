Functional description

The circuit for the dipped beam headlamps is operated by the steering column switch unit H5 .

When the switch is placed in the dipped headlamps position this energizes the relay J45 , via the line controlled by the ignition (INT) protected by fuse F8 of the junction unit B1 , which supplies the left and right headlamps F10 and F11 . Each circuit is protected by a fuse in the junction unit B1 : F5 for the right headlamp and F4 for the left one.

The supply line for the right lamp - protected by fuse F5 - also acts as the go ahead signal for the headlamp alignment E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR and the headlamp washer. E3022 HEADLAMP WASHER
