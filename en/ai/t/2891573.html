Reassembling

1. Remove the two brake pads from the brake caliper.

2. Using the special spanner (2a), undo the connector (2b) on the caliper and remove the pipe.

3. Remove the braking system pipe.

1. Place the front brake caliper in the vice and remove the bleed valve (1a) complete with cover (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the piston from the front brake caliper casing using a jet of compressed air in the brake fluid inlet port.

Remove the piston carefully to prevent the spraying of brake fluid, damage or scratches.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the piston protective boot.

2. Remove the gasket.

During the extraction of the gasket, take care not to scratch the brake caliper body cylinder.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
