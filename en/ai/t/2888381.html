Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, neither the windscreen wash/wipe nor the rearscreen wash/wipe works properly

Results of the tests

1

WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • windscreen wiper fuse
  • starting inhibitor relay
  • connections between junction unit and stalk unit

( See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE).

Continue to Step 2

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections not correct

Restore the connections

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION
