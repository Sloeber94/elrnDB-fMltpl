X30 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

A (red coloured) fluid leak from the power assisted steering system can be seen.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check whether the problem is accompanied by a stiffening of the steering or noise from the steering

Visually inspect the level of the power steering fluid in the reservoir

Continue to Step 1

Power steering fluid level too low

Restore the correct level and carry out a road test. If the defect persists,

Continue to Step 1

Stiffening of the steering detected

See table H10 STEERING HARD / (STEERING IN BOTH DIRECTIONS)

Noise from the steering detected

See table S40 NOISE' FRONT / DRIVING IN A STRAIGHT LINE ON AN EVEN ROAD

1

RESERVOIR LEAK CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check that there are no leadks from the power assisted steering fluid reservoir

Continue to Step 2

Leaks from the reservoir

Replace the reservoir Op. 4130B10 power steering fluid resevoir r r

2

PIPE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check, using talc, that there are no leaks from the connectors for all the power assisted steering system pipes, flexible and rigid.

Continue to Step 3

Leaks from the system

Tighten the connectors or replace the damaged parts, replacing all the seals (see Assembly 4130B)

3

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the power assisted steering pump is working properly

Continue to Step 4

Power assisted steering pump malfunction

Replace the power assisted steering pump Op. 4130B20 power steering pump r r

4

STEERING BOX CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Visually check that there are no leaks (using talcum powder):

  • from the rigid pipes at the steering box and attachments
  • from the steering box lower plug
  • from the steering column attachment
  • from the actual steering box

Continue to Step 5

Leaks from the rigid pipes

Tighten the connectors or replace the steering box Op. 4130A10 power steering box - r+r - check tow-in if necessary separately - includes bleeding

Leaks from the lower plug

Eliminate the leak using sealant, or replace the steering box Op. 4130A10 power steering box - r+r - check tow-in if necessary separately - includes bleeding

Leaks from the steering column attachment or from the actual steering box

Replace the steering box Op. 4130A10 power steering box - r+r - check tow-in if necessary separately - includes bleeding

5

STEERING BOX BOOTS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Inspect the steering box boots and check that there is no fluid

Continue to Step 6

Leaks from the steering box seals

Replace the steering box gaiters Op. 4130A20 l/h or r/h power steering case boot (one) - replace at bench

6

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
