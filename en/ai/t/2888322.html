B11 ENGINE STARTS WITH DIFFICULTY' / WHEN WARM

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

When the engine is warm, for example just after it has been switched off following a long journey, it does not start, or it starts with difficulty, only after repeated attempts. When the key is turned the instruments light up, the starter motor turns, but the engine does not start.

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Preliminarily check:

  • the state of the spark plugs and leads
  • the condition of the air intake circuit

Continue to Step 1

Spark plugs dirty or damaged

Clean or replace the spark plugs Op. 5510D10 spark plugs - r+r

Spark plug leads damaged

Replace the spark plug leads Op. 5510D14 spark plug cable lead bundle - r+r

Air intake circuit component damaged

Replace the component (see Assembly 1048A)

Air cleaner dirty or partially blocked

Replace the air filter Op. 1048A14 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE.
