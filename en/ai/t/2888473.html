With the car in motion, noises can be heard from the upper front area.

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Firstly, check the front aerial is correctly fitted (if present)

Check whether the noise comes from the windscreen by carrying out a preliminary check to locate the noise See Test 70A Location of wind noise (preliminary location)

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from another area
