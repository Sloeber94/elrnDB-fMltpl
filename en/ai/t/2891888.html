Removing

Refitting

Open the bonnet lid.

With the engine cold, blow through with compressed air in the spark plug openings to remove any foreign bodies or traces of dirt.

1. Remove the dust covers.

2. Undo the spark plugs.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check the condition of the spark plugs and, if necessary, replace them.

Using different spark plugs from those recommended can cause damage to the engine and alter the exhaust emission levels.

Fit the spark plugs and tighten them to the recommended torque.
