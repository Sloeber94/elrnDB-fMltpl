Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the brake pedal is pressed with the ignition ON, the brake lights do not work.

Results of the tests

1

CHECK EXTERIOR LIGHTING

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the brake light bulbs and the associated bulb holder

Continue to Step 2

Bulb damaged

Change bulbs

Rear lights cluster faulty

Repair the tail-light Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

2

CHECK CENTRAL LIGHT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual ten bulbs and complete bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 3

Bulb damaged

Replace individual bulbs

Bulb holder faulty

Repair or replace the third stop light Op. 5550D16 third stop light - r+r

3

CHECK STOP LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • stop lights fuse (services)
  • connections between junction unit and brake pedal switch
  • connection between junction unit and bulbs
  • bulb earth

See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS - Description

Continue to Step 4

Component defective

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections

4

BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the brake pedal switch is fitted correctly and working

See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS - Description

Continue to Step 5

Brake pedal switch faulty

Repair or replace the brake pedal switch Op. 5550D10 brake light switch - r+r

5

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Search for the fault starting from another symptom'
