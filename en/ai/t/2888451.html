Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the engine running, when the various gears are engaged, there is a noise during take-off: the noise varies as the speed varies

Results of the tests

1

GEARBOX SHAFT FIXING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the nuts fixing the gearbox main and layshafts are correctly tightened ( Op. 2110B20 manual gearbox (5 speed) with differential - dismantle and rebuild - wash and check parts - replace synchronisers and internal controls if necessary

Continue to Step 2

Nuts not tightened

Restore the correct tightening

2

CHECKING THE GEAR SET

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the crown wheel and pinion reduction ( Op. 2110D20 differential inner case, removed - dismantle and rebuild, check planet and differential gears)

In particular

  • it should not be worn or damaged
  • there should not be excessive clearance
  • the ring gear fixings should not be slack

Continue to Step 3

Reduction gear damaged

Restore the clearance or, if necessary, replace the reduction gear

3

CHECKING PLANET CARRIER

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the wear of the satellite carrier bearing or the housing in the differential housing ( Op. 2110D20 differential inner case, removed - dismantle and rebuild, check planet and differential gears)

Continue to Step 4

Differential case worn

Replace the differential casing

4

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
