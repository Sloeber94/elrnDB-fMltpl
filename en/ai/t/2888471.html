Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the car is in motion, noise can be heard from one of the rear doors.

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Firstly check the moving window glass is properly closed.

Check that the wind noise does in fact come from one of the rear doors by carrying out the test for preliminary wind noise localisation See Test 70A Location of wind noise (preliminary location)

Continue to Step 1

The wind noise comes from another area

Check the diagnostic table corresponding to the area located.

1

CHECK THE DUST SEALING STRIP ON THE DOOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the lower seal is properly fitted

Continue to Step 2

Lower seal installation defective

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7010A46 lower rear side door seal r r

Wind strip incorrectly fitted

Fit the sealing strip correctly Op. 7010A50

2

CHECK LOCATION OF WIND NOISE FROM THE REAR DOORS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out the test for detailed localisation of wind noise from the rear doors See Test 7010A Location of rear door wind noiseand locate the area from which the noise originates.

No wind noise is located

Continue to Step 3

The window noise comes from the upper area of the central pillar

Adjust the door hinges Op. 7010A14 rear side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment

The wind noise comes from the lower area of the central pillar

Adjust the door hinges Op. 7010A14 rear side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment

The wind noise comes from the handle

Check the rear handle is undamaged and correctly positioned or replace the handle Op. 7010C11 rear side door outside handle-r r

The wind noise comes from the rear pillar area

Check the condition of the door compartment seal in that area and ensure it is bonded properly to the body Op. 7010A34 rear side door compartment seal r r

The wind noise comes from the glass scraper area

Check the glass scraper is undamaged and correctly fitted Op. 7010D26 rear side descending window glass scraper (outer) r r

3

CHECKING CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists
