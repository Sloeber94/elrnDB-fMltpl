0010M60 160,000km service coupon - emission control versions

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Place the vehicle on a ramp.

Check that the tyre treads are not unevenly worn.

Some types of tyres have wear indicators; the tyres must be replaced as soon as these indicators become visible.

Do not cross-interchange the tyres.

Check that the tyres pressures are correct, including the spare tyre.

The tyre pressures should be checked on cold tyres.

Earth the front brake pad wear indicator cable.

Turn the ignition on (MAR).

Press the brake pedal and check that the front brake pad wear warning light comes on.

Check that the rubber gaiters on the drive shafts and power steering and the balljoint caps are not friction damaged or broken.

Check that there are no leaks from the brake and fuel pipes/connectors.

3330A10 BRAKE FLUID - Check level and top up, if necessary. .

Refer to op.. 5530B18 electrolyte - check density and top up if necessary .

Check and if necessary top up the windscreen washer fluid level (refer to Op. 5550A14 stalk unit support with modules - r+r ).

Check and if necessary top up the power steering fluid level (refer to Op. 4130B14 Power assisted steering fluid reservoir CAP - R.R. - Check level and top up if necessary. ).

Refer to op.. 1088A10 Engine COOLANT - Check density' and top up level, if necessary .

0010T12 engine oil and filter replace .

Refer to op.. 2110A04 Manual gearbox and differential OIL - Check level and top up, if necessary .

1048A14 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE. .

Check and if necessary adjust the travel of the handbrake lever (refer to Op. 3380A04 handbrake - check travel ).

Check and if necessary adjust the travel of the clutch pedal (refer to Op. 1820A14 clutch pedal - adjust travel ).

Connect the EXAMINER or other diagnostic instruments to the diagnostic socket (select the control unit in question), then check the plausibility of all the parameters and if necessary carry out the available active diagnoses.

Applies to: engines: 1.6 16v; all production periods

Visually check that the alternator/water pump drive belt shows no signs of uneven wear or damage.

Visually check that the power steering pump drive belt shows no signs of uneven wear or damage.

Visually check the evaporation control system (pipes - connectors).

5510D10 spark plugs - r+r .

Refer to op.. 1080A12 idling co level - check using special equipment and adjust speed .

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8V; all production periods

Visually check that the single drive belt shows no signs of uneven wear or damage.

1036H10 valve tappets - check and adjust clearance .

1044B20 fuel filter filtering element - replace .

Refer to op.. 1080A20 smokiness at exhaust - check with opacimeter .

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Remove the vehicle from the ramp.
